Policy: Allow each fisher to take up to 1.2 kg per trip when lake ≥12 kg, keeping 1.0 kg for sustenance and depositing 0.2 kg; when lake is 8–12 kg, limit to 1.1 kg, keeping 1.0 kg and depositing 0.1 kg; when lake is 6–8 kg, limit to 1.0 kg, keeping 1.0 kg with no deposit; fishing is suspended when lake falls below 6 kg until the communal reserve reaches at least 12 kg.

Operationalization: At the start of each round the community chair announces the lake stock and reserve. Fisheries record each catch, keep 1.0 kg regardless of catch, and deposit the remainder as specified. Deposits are added to the reserve ledger. If a fisher exceeds the limit, the surplus is forfeited and divided equally among all fishers. When the lake is below 6 kg, all fishing stops until the reserve reaches 12 kg, at which point limits resume. Violations are logged; repeated infractions trigger a temporary fishing ban.

Policy: Each fisher may take at most 1.5 kg per trip, deposit 30 % of the catch into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 30 % of the lake’s current biomass, with a 0.05 kg fine per kilogram shortfall carried forward, and the maximum catch limit is reduced or raised by 0.1 kg per fisher when the reserve falls below or recovers above 30 %.

Operationalization: Before each trip the fisher records the expected catch in kg. After fishing the fisher records the actual catch, deposits 30 % of the actual catch into the communal bin, and if the bin now contains less than 30 % of the lake’s biomass the fisher tops it up to reach that threshold. The Fishery Steward maintains a ledger of all expected and actual catches, deposits, the current reserve level, and the lake’s biomass. Each week the community compiles a log of every fisher’s data. If any fisher fails to deposit the required amount within 48 h a fine of 0.05 kg per kilogram shortfall is applied; if the fine exceeds the next catch the fisher pays what can be paid and the remaining debt is carried forward to future catches. If the communal reserve falls below 30 % of the lake’s biomass after any trip, the community meets within 48 h to reduce the maximum catch by 0.1 kg per fisher; this reduced limit is held until the reserve reaches at least 30 %. When the reserve stays at or above 30 % for two consecutive weekly logs, the community lifts the catch limit by 0.1 kg per fisher, up to the original 1.5 kg. All changes to the limit are recorded and announced at the next community meeting, which requires a quorum of at least three fishers plus the Steward, and passes by a simple majority of those present.

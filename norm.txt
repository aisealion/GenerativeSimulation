Policy: Each fisher may take up to 80 % of the lake’s current biomass per trip; 10 % of each catch is deposited into the communal reserve; if the reserve falls below 5 kg the next trip’s maximum take is lowered by 20 % (to 64 % of lake biomass) and stays so until the reserve reaches 10 kg; failure to deposit or exceed the quota triggers a 0.01 kg fine and a 0.01 kg restitution to the reserve.

Operationalization: Before every trip the fisher checks the current lake biomass B and the communal reserve total R (posted on the notice board or shared ledger). The allowed take T is 0.8 × B if R ≥ 5 kg, otherwise 0.8 × B × 0.8 (64 %). After fishing the fisher records the catch C, pays a 0.01 kg fine if C > T or if they forget the 10 % deposit, and immediately transfers 0.1 × C into the communal reserve ledger. The fisher then updates the ledger, adding the deposit (and any fine) and posting the new reserve total. The Reserve Keeper, who rotates weekly to the fisher with the highest net catch that week, signs the ledger each day and posts the reserve balance. The community council meets every Friday to audit the ledger, confirm the reserve level, and if R ≥ 10 kg lift the quota back to 80 % for the next trip. If R < 5 kg the quota remains reduced until the reserve reaches 10 kg. All fishers must check the posted reserve level before setting out. Fines are paid from the fisher’s next trip’s catch or directly to the keeper if the catch is already deposited.

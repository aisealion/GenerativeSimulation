Policy: Each fisher may keep up to 25 kg of fish per trip; any excess must be deposited into a communal maintenance reserve for lake upkeep and community meals. After each month, any reserve weight above a 20 kg baseline is evenly divided among all fishers who met their quota, while failure to deposit or record excess results in a 5 kg quota reduction next month.

Operationalization: 1. Before departure each fisher writes a 25 kg quota on the shared sheet. 2. During the trip they may keep up to 25 kg; any additional fish is taken to the dock and deposited in the communal reserve. 3. At the dock the clerk logs the weight deposited, signs the fisher’s name, and places it in a locked box at the dock. 4. At month’s end the community maintenance committee reviews dock deposit logs and each fisher’s trip log to identify any fishers who did not deposit or record the excess. 5. If the reserve exceeds 20 kg, the surplus (reserve − 20 kg) is calculated. 6. Count the number of fishers who met their 25 kg quota (caught ≤25 kg). 7. Each such fisher receives an equal share of the surplus (surplus ÷ N). 8. Any fisher flagged as non‑compliant receives a 5 kg quota reduction applied at the start of the following month’s fishing season. 9. The audit and distribution take place on the first working day after month’s end, before the new month’s trips.

Policy: Each fisher may take only one trip per day and may catch no more than two units of fish; excess fish must be returned and repeated violations trigger bans as recorded in the community ledger.

Operationalization: 1. The eldest fisher, chosen monthly by the council (village chief, senior fisher, market stall owner, and head of fishing families’ association), acts as the volunteer reviewer. 2. Every fisher signs in at departure and out at return, recording time, trip count, and catch count (using a net or basket with a built‑in counter or numbered slots). 3. The volunteer reviews the ledger daily at 6 pm, verifies each fisher’s reported catch against the physical counter, and ensures no fisher has logged more than one departure per day or more than two units. 4. If a mismatch or over‑catch is found, the fisher must immediately correct the ledger; a second discrepancy results in a violation. 5. Violations increment a fisher’s “Violations” counter in the ledger: 1st = 1‑week ban, 2nd = 2‑week ban, 3rd+ = 4‑week ban, capped at 4 weeks. 6. The volunteer enforces the ban by withholding boat keys, locking the boat, and noting the ban on the ledger. 7. After the ban period ends, the volunteer resets the fisher’s “Violations” counter to 0. 8. All ledger entries, violations, and bans are displayed on the communal board for transparency. 9. Fiscers may appeal a ban by attending a brief community meeting, but must first demonstrate correct recording procedures.

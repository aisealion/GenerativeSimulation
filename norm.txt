Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 1.5 kg per trip or no more than 6 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller, and after every catch the lake must retain at least 85 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve.

Operationalization: Every trip a fisher logs catch weight on a shared ledger before setting out. The ledger is reviewed quarterly by the community council; if a catch would reduce the lake below the 85 % reserve threshold, the fisher must reduce or return the excess fish. The council enforces compliance by holding the excess fish in a communal storage that is only used for emergencies or community needs. If a fisher repeatedly fails to follow the rule, the council may revoke their fishing rights for that season.

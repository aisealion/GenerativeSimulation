Policy: Each fisher may take up to 10% of the lake's current stock per trip, capped at 12 kg; any surplus is donated to the communal reserve, reducing the next quota by the surplus amount. A monthly review adjusts quotas, and a 1-kg fee is applied to repeat violators until compliance is restored.

Operationalization: The community council, with a rotating tally keeper, publishes the lake’s estimated stock (≈227 kg) and each fisher’s 10% quota (capped at 12 kg) just before each trip. The tally keeper estimates total stock by sampling a standard area with a seine net, extrapolating density to the full lake, records this estimate, and logs every catch in a communal ledger. Surplus fish are removed from the lake stock, added to the communal reserve managed by the council, and the next trip’s quota is immediately reduced by the surplus amount. If a fisher exceeds their quota, the tally keeper flags them as a repeat violator and applies a 1-kg fee to their next catch; the fisher is considered compliant once they complete a trip within quota, at which point the fee is removed. The monthly review meeting, chaired by the tally keeper and attended by the council, re-estimates the lake stock, recalculates all quotas, and confirms any compliance status changes.

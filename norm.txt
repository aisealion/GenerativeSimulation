Policy: Each fisher may take at most 1.5 kg per trip; 30 % of each catch is deposited into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 30 % of the lake’s current biomass. If the reserve falls below 30 % the maximum is lowered to 1 kg until the reserve is restored.

Operationalization: 1. Before each trip, the fisher records the catch weight on the communal log. 2. Immediately after the catch, the fisher deposits exactly 30 % of the recorded weight into the reserve container kept in the community’s shared dock storage. 3. A weekly steward, assisted by a rotating group of fishers, measures the lake’s biomass and compares 30 % of that value to the current reserve level; all results are entered into the communal log. 4. If a fisher fails to deposit, a fine of 0.05 kg is levied and the fisher’s next trip maximum is reduced to 1 kg until the fine is paid and the record is cleared. 5. If the reserve level drops below 30 % of the lake’s biomass, all active fishers vote (simple majority) to enforce the 1 kg trip limit; the limit remains until the reserve is restored to at least 30 %. 6. Once the reserve reaches or exceeds 30 %, the 1.5 kg limit automatically reinstates and all actions are logged for transparency.

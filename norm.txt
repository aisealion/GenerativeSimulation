Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 90% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 300 kg; each fisher must keep 1 kg for sustenance; 0.5% of the catch (rounded to 0.1 kg) is deposited into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 30 kg; the lake must never fall below 50 kg after any trip.

Operationalization: Before each trip a fisher checks the lake stock. They may take 90% of that stock but no more than 300 kg, keeping 1 kg for themselves. They calculate 0.5% of their catch, round to the nearest 0.1 kg, and deposit that into the reserve. The reserve balance is updated. After fishing the lake stock must be at least 50 kg; if it falls below, the fisher must wait until the reserve is replenished to that level before fishing again. If a fisher fails to deposit the required amount or exceeds the 90%/300 kg limit, the community will require them to forfeit the excess catch to the reserve and pay an extra day’s ration to the reserve as penalty.

Policy: Each fisher may harvest no more than 10 kg per trip; any excess must be returned to the lake and logged, with a simple majority of at least three fishers required to lift a temporary suspension.

Operationalization: 1. At the weekly gathering every fisher reports the intended catch. 2. The community sets a shared quota of 10 kg per person. 3. If a fisher exceeds the quota, the excess is returned to the lake immediately, and the fisher records the surplus in the communal log using the action "record surplus in the log". 4. The fisher’s license status is updated to "suspended" if the surplus is not returned within 48 h. 5. The suspension is lifted when the fisher presents the corrected log entry at the next meeting, and at least a simple majority of the fishers present (minimum three fishers) confirm compliance. 6. If any fisher objects, the lift is postponed until a follow‑up discussion resolves the issue. 7. Should a fisher fail to comply with the return or log entry, the community delivers a verbal reminder at the next meeting, records a “reminder” entry in the ledger, and the fisher must rectify the issue before any further fishing. 8. All changes to the harvest quota require at least three fishers present and a simple majority vote.

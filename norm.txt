Policy: Each fisher may take up to 95 % of the lake’s current biomass per trip; if the communal reserve is below 5 kg, 2 % of the catch must be deposited into the reserve; if the reserve falls below 2 kg, the next trip’s maximum take is capped at 50 % until the reserve reaches 5 kg or more.

Operationalization: 1. Before the trip, read the ledger’s "reserve" value. 2. If reserve < 2 kg, set max_take = 0.50 × current lake biomass; otherwise set max_take = 0.95 × current lake biomass. 3. The fisher selects a catch up to max_take. 4. Update lake biomass: lake = lake – catch. 5. If reserve < 5 kg, compute deposit = 0.02 × catch, add deposit to reserve, and record the deposit in the ledger. 6. Write the updated lake biomass and reserve back to the ledger. 7. If reserve ≥ 5 kg, clear the 50 % flag so the following trip resumes the 95 % limit. 8. All updates are made to a shared ledger that every fisher records after each trip, ensuring collective responsibility for deposits and compliance.

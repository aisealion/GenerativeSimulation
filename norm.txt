Policy: Each fisher may take up to 30 kg per trip; the lake must maintain a reserve equal to 25 % of its current stock, and if the reserve falls below that threshold all fishers’ maximum catch is reduced by 15 % for the next round.

Operationalization: At the start of each round, the elected community leader assigns rotating 2‑hour slots and records the date and time. Each fisher pays a deposit of 0.10 kg immediately after slot assignment or at slot completion; the deposit is transferred to the communal pot recorded in the leader’s ledger. When a fisher finishes, they record the weight caught; the leader updates the lake stock in the ledger and checks that the catch does not exceed 30 kg and that the reserve (current stock × 25 %) remains above the threshold. If a violation occurs, the leader deducts the 0.10 kg deposit from the fisher’s contribution to the pot and records a fine of the same amount in the ledger. The communal pot is used to cover any fines, repair costs, or community projects, and its balance is visible to all fishers each week. The leader’s slot assignments and ledger updates occur each round, and the maximum catch adjustment is applied automatically when the reserve falls below the threshold.

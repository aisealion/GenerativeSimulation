Policy: Fishing is allowed when the lake holds at least 280 kg and the communal reserve contains at least 10 kg; each fisher may take up to 1.2 kg per trip, keep 1 kg for sustenance, deposit 0.2 kg into the reserve.

Operationalization: At the start of each round, a quick tally of lake and reserve is made. If both thresholds are met, all fishers may board their boats. Each fisher is required to weigh and record the amount taken, then deposit 0.2 kg into the communal bin. If the thresholds are not met, all fishers stay on shore and wait for the next round. If a fisher fails to deposit the required 0.2 kg, the missing amount is added to the reserve by a community volunteer.

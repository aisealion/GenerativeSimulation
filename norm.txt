Policy: Each fisher may keep up to 20 kg per trip; any fish beyond 20 kg must be placed into the community reserve. The reserve is used only in emergencies (up to 5 % of the reserve per event) or for routine maintenance after communal meals; fish removed are returned after use, and the inventory is recorded weekly by the Lake Monitor.

Operationalization: 1. Count your catch before returning to shore; deposit any amount over 20 kg into the locked reserve bin. 2. The Lake Monitor weighs and logs the reserve bin each week, noting total kg. 3. For an emergency or routine maintenance event, the Lake Monitor announces the event, calculates 5 % of the current reserve (rounding down to whole kg), logs the removal, and authorizes that amount for use. 4. Fish taken from the reserve are used and, once the event is complete, are returned to the bin and the reserve total is updated. 5. Violations of the 20 kg limit or failure to return reserve fish are logged; the fisher must surrender the excess or, if the offense repeats, face a two‑week fishing ban and/or mandatory maintenance duty, enforced by the Lake Monitor and the community during a simple‑majority meeting. 6. The Lake Monitor role rotates monthly to the fisher who has served longest, ensuring neutrality. 7. The mayor is elected annually by all fishers; the mayor appoints a council of three respected fishers for two‑year terms, meeting monthly to review lake conditions and authorize emergencies. 8. The maintenance committee consists of five members: the Lake Monitor, the fisher with the most years fishing, a fisher elected by majority vote, the boat operator, and a youth representative; this committee is re‑selected each season to rotate experience and fairness. 9. After each communal meal, 5 % of the current reserve is set aside for routine maintenance, rounded down to the nearest kilogram.

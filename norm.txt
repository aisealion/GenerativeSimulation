Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 80% of the lake’s current stock per trip, must keep 1 kg for sustenance, deposit 1 kg into a communal reserve that must never fall below 20 kg, and the lake must never fall below 30 kg after any trip. If a catch would bring the lake below 30 kg, the fisher must deposit an extra 0.5 kg per trip until the lake is back to 30 kg. If a fisher fails to deposit the required amount, their entire catch for that trip is forfeited to the reserve on the following trip.

Operationalization: At the start of each trip the fisher checks the current lake stock, calculates 80% of that as the maximum they may take, and also ensures at least 30 kg will remain. If the planned catch would lower the lake below 30 kg, they reduce their catch or deposit 0.5 kg until the lake is at 30 kg. After harvesting, they put 1 kg into the communal reserve. The reserve is tracked on a shared ledger; if it dips below 20 kg after a deposit, the fisher must add extra 1 kg until the reserve hits 20 kg. If a fisher does not put in the required 1 kg, the next trip’s entire catch is automatically transferred to the reserve.

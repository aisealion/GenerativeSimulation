Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 90% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 250 kg; must keep 1 kg for sustenance; deposit 0.5 % of the catch (rounded to 0.1 kg) into a communal reserve that must stay at least 30 kg; the lake must never fall below 50 kg after any trip.

Operationalization: Before each trip a fisher declares the intended catch. After fishing, they weigh the catch, deposit 0.5 % of the weight into the communal reserve, and the lake’s new stock is calculated by subtracting the total catch. If the new stock is below 50 kg, the fisher must reduce catch and re‑declare. All catches and deposits are logged by a community observer; if a fisher fails to deposit the required amount or leaves the lake below 50 kg, that fisher is banned from fishing for the next round and any undeposited catch is forfeited.

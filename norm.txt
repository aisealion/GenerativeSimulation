Policy: Each villager may catch no more than 1.5 kg of fish per day. Surplus must be returned to the lake or deposited into the communal pool, with all catches weighed, recorded, and signed on the shared ledger, and the communal scale checked weekly by the appointed recorder.

Operationalization: At the end of each fishing day, the fisher weighs the total catch on the communal hand-scale, records the weight next to their name on the shared ledger, and signs it with the appointed recorder. If the weight exceeds 1.5 kg, the fisher must either drop the excess back into the lake at the agreed spot or hand it over to the community fish keeper. The surplus is recorded in the common pool ledger, stored in the communal granary, and later distributed or sold by the fish keeper and village council through monthly consensus. The appointed recorder checks the communal hand-scale weekly and audits the ledger; any discrepancy triggers a review by the fish keeper and council, resulting in mandatory return of surplus, forfeiture of the excess, a fine of one day's fishing right, or a temporary fishing ban for repeated infractions. The recorder and fish keeper are appointed by the village council, and the ledger is signed by both the fisher and the recorder.

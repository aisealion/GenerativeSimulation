Policy: Maximum catch per trip is 2 kg per fisher; 30 % of each catch must be deposited into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 30 % of the lake’s current biomass. If the reserve falls below 30 % of the lake’s biomass, the committee will temporarily reduce the maximum catch to 1.5 kg until the reserve recovers, then restore to 2 kg.

Operationalization: 1. After each trip, the fisher records catch weight and deposit weight on the communal ledger immediately. 2. The ledger keeper, chosen by rotation each month, reviews all entries within 24 h. 3. If a deposit is short, the keeper records a 0.05 kg fine and adds that weight to the communal tank. 4. Any fisher exceeding the 2 kg limit must return the excess to the reserve and pay a 0.1 kg fine per kg over; the community suspends their license for one day while the fine is added. 5. Weekly, the committee weighs the communal tank and notes the lake’s latest biomass from the daily ledger. 6. They compute reserve % = (reserve weight ÷ lake weight) × 100. 7. If reserve % is below 30 %, the committee temporarily reduces the maximum catch to 1.5 kg for all fishers until reserve % reaches 30 % or more; then the limit is restored to 2 kg. 8. All ledger entries, fines, and weekly reserve checks are recorded in the communal ledger for transparency.

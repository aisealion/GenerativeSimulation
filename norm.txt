Policy: Each fisher may take up to 18% of the lake’s current biomass per trip, must set aside 5% of their catch for the communal reserve; if a fisher exceeds their quota the next trip’s allowance is multiplied by 0.9 for each consecutive over‑quota trip, and after three consecutive over‑quots the fisher is suspended for one trip.

Operationalization: 1. At trip start the fisher records the lake’s current biomass in the public ledger and calculates 18% as their quota. 2. After the catch the fisher records total catch weight and immediately removes 5% into the communal chest, logging the deposit. 3. A second fisher (or the next finisher) verifies the catch against the recorded quota; if over‑quota the ledger increments the fisher’s over‑quota streak and writes the new adjusted allowance (0.9^n × 18% of that trip’s biomass). 4. The ledger keeps a running count of each fisher’s over‑quota streak and any “suspended one trip” flag. 5. If the streak reaches three, the entry is marked “suspended one trip”; the boat’s captain reviews the ledger before each departure and denies boarding to any fisher with a suspension flag. 6. All ledger entries, deposits, and suspension status are kept in a public ledger (physical log book or shared digital record) that every fisher can audit at any time.

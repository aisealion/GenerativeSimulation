Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 1.0 kg per trip or no more than 8 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller, while the lake must retain at least 70 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve after each catch.

Operationalization: Before each season, the community records the lake’s total biomass. Every fisher logs each trip’s catch in a communal ledger. If a fisher’s catch exceeds the 1 kg/8 % cap, the excess is returned to the lake and the fisher must pay a fine equal to 10 % of the excess, paid from their next catch. Repeat violations trigger a temporary suspension of fishing rights, reviewed by the council. The lake’s reserve is verified each season by a community survey.

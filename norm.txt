Policy: Each fisher may take a maximum of 4 kg per trip, and the community’s total catch per fishing day must not exceed 30 kg.

Operationalization: Trip definition: a continuous outing from departure to return; a new trip is logged whenever a fisher leaves and re‑enters the lake on the same day. Each trip may not exceed 4 kg, and a fisher may take any number of trips in one day as long as the daily community total does not exceed 30 kg.
Ledger keeper: a designated or rotated fisher tallies all trips and verifies the shared ledger at day’s end, flagging any violations. The ledger keeper records start/end times and catch weights for each trip.
Daily limit: the sum of all fishers’ catches that day must stay ≤30 kg. If the total would exceed 30 kg, fishers must reduce catches or return excess to the lake.
Violation procedure: any breach (trip >4 kg or community total >30 kg) is reported by the ledger keeper. At the next community meeting the case is presented and a majority vote decides the penalty—return of excess fish, a small fine to the community pot, or a temporary suspension of fishing rights for a set number of days. The ledger keeper and the elected moderator enforce the penalty.
Compliance: every fisher logs each trip immediately after return in the shared ledger; failure to record or false reporting is treated as a violation.
Rotation: the ledger keeper role rotates monthly among fishers to ensure fairness.

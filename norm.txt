Policy: Maximum catch per fisher per trip: 20 kg, mandatory reserve of 12 % of the lake’s stock at the start of each trip; if the reserve falls below this threshold during a round, all fishers’ quotas are reduced by 20 % for the next round.

Operationalization: Every fisher records in the communal ledger the lake’s starting stock, the amount taken, and the ending stock after each trip. The community steward reviews the ledger before the next round, verifies that no one’s catch exceeded 20 kg and that the ending stock after any catch was at least 12 % of the starting stock. If a violation is found, the steward records a 20 % reduction on each fisher’s current quota in the ledger; the reduction is applied to whatever quota each fisher has at that time (so a previously reduced quota is cut again by 20 %). The updated quotas are made visible to all fishers for the next round. No automatic penalty occurs if no violation is flagged; trust the ledger otherwise.

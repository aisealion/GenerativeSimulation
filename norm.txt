Policy: No fisher may take more than 12 kg per trip, and the lake must retain at least 30 % of its pre‑trip stock after each round.

Operationalization: Pre‑trip: the rotating chair measures the lake’s current stock with a calibrated scale and net, writes the exact weight, time, and date in the communal ledger, and a second fisher verifies and signs the entry; the ledger is posted in the hall and online.  Each fisher logs their catch weight on a personal log, signs it, and submits it to the chair after the round.  The chair tallies total catch, checks every log against the 12 kg limit, and if any fisher exceeds it, the chair calculates the excess, requires the fisher to return it, weighs the returned fish, records the weight, and signs the ledger.  An auditor is randomly selected from the three‑member audit team (excluding the violator and their immediate family) for that return; the auditor independently weighs the same batch, confirms the chair’s measurement, and signs the ledger entry.  The chair verifies that the post‑fishing stock equals the pre‑trip stock minus total catch plus returned excess; if the stock falls below 30 % of the pre‑trip stock, the chair schedules a re‑fishing trip.  During re‑fishing, the chair measures the lake stock before and after the trip, the audit team of three cross‑checks the weight difference, the chair calculates the shortfall needed to bring the lake to 30 % of its pre‑re‑fishing stock, and confirms that the post‑re‑fishing stock meets or exceeds this amount.  All three auditors sign the ledger entry with the new stock figures, and the chair updates the ledger for the next round.  Every ledger entry contains pre‑trip stock, total catch, post‑fishing stock, any excess returned, re‑fishing adjustments, and final verified stock, with signatures of the chair and auditors, ensuring transparency, community ownership, and enforceability.

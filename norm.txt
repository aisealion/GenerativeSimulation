Policy: Each fisher may keep up to 12kg per trip; any catch above 12kg is deposited into a communal pool that is allocated to fishers who caught less than 5kg.

Operationalization: On each trip, fishers record their catch in a shared ledger. If a fisher exceeds 12kg, the excess is placed in a communal bucket. After the trip, the bucket is divided among fishers who caught <5kg, giving each enough to bring their total up to 5kg, up to the amount in the bucket. Anyone who fails to deposit excess or keep within the limit must return the fish to the lake and pay a 10% fine to the pool.

Policy: Each fisher may keep up to 5 kg per trip; any excess goes to a communal pool. Every month, the pool first distributes 3 kg to fishers whose reserves are below 20 kg, starting with the lowest reserves, then splits any remaining fish equally among all fishers. Fishers with reserves below 10 kg must deposit an extra 1 kg into the pool before their next trip, or face a 1 kg penalty deducted automatically on the following catch.

Operationalization: 1. After each trip the fisher records the amount kept (≤5 kg) and any excess deposited into the communal pool on the community ledger. 2. The ledger keeper (dock clerk or designated record‑keeper) checks the trip report for the required extra 1 kg deposit for any fisher whose reserve_kg is below 10 kg. 3. If the deposit is missing, the keeper sets the penalty_due flag for that fisher. 4. On the next trip, before the 5 kg keep limit is applied, the ledger automatically subtracts 1 kg from the fisher’s allowed catch if penalty_due is true, then clears the flag. 5. The fisher’s reserve_kg is updated by adding the kept catch and any allocation from the pool, and subtracting any penalty that will be applied on the next catch. 6. The communal_pool_kg variable tracks the total kilograms in the shared container; it is increased by any excess from each trip and decreased by allocations during the monthly meeting. 7. At month‑end the ledger tallies communal_pool_kg, then: a) allocates 3 kg to each qualifying fisher (reserve_kg <20 kg) in order of lowest reserves until the pool is exhausted, reducing communal_pool_kg accordingly; b) if any fish remain, splits the remainder equally among all fishers and adds the share to each reserve_kg, bringing communal_pool_kg to zero.

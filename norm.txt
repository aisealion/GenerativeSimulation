Policy: Each fisher may take up to 15% of the lake’s current stock per trip, with a minimum of 1kg for sustenance and a hard cap of 20kg.

Operationalization: Before each trip the fisher logs the lake’s starting stock on the communal ledger. After fishing, the fisher submits the exact catch weight. The moderator checks the catch against the limit: min(15% of the logged starting stock, 20 kg). If the catch ≤ limit, it is accepted and the weight is removed from the lake. If the catch > limit, the excess is calculated; the moderator then decides its destination: if the lake’s current stock is < 50 kg the excess is returned to the lake, otherwise it is transferred to the community fund. The fisher’s fee_balance in the ledger is incremented by 0.5 kg. For every subsequent trip until the fisher remains within limits for two consecutive trips, the fisher must deduct 0.5 kg from the catch (or pay it in cash) as a fee; once compliance is achieved the fee_balance is cleared. Weekly stock reviews adjust the percentage threshold to 10% if the lake’s stock falls below 50 kg to protect resources.

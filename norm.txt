Policy: Each fisher may take up to 2 kg per trip; 30 % of each catch must be deposited into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 30 % of the lake’s current biomass. Failure to deposit or exceeding the 2 kg limit results in a 10 % fine, which is deposited into the reserve, and the excess fish returned to the lake.

Operationalization: The reserve is kept in the dock’s communal storage box and tracked on a community ledger displayed in the hall and a shared digital sheet. A rotating committee, elected each season by vote, appoints the Lake Steward who performs weekly biomass checks using a standard gill‑net panel, calculates CPUE, scales it by lake area and depth, and ensures the reserve remains ≥30 % of that biomass. The Steward enforces the 2 kg limit and 30 % deposit, collects a 10 % fine for non‑deposit or over‑catch, records it in the ledger, deposits the fine portion into the reserve, and returns any excess fish to the lake. All transactions are logged and visible to the community to maintain transparency.

Policy: Each fisher may take up to 2 kg per trip and must deposit the lesser of 0.5 kg or their intended catch into a shared pool before fishing. Each trip must leave at least 15 % of the lake’s pre‑trip stock untouched, and the community’s total daily catch may not exceed 80 % of the lake’s weight.

Operationalization: Before every trip the fisher writes down the intended catch and deposits the smaller of 0.5 kg or the intended catch into the shared pool; the registrar records this deposit. The fisher then fishes up to the 2 kg limit, ensuring that after removal the lake still holds at least 15 % of its pre‑trip stock. If a fisher fails to deposit, exceeds the 2 kg limit, or leaves less than 15 % of the stock, a fine of 0.01 kg per excess kilogram is levied and added to the shared pool. At day’s end the registrar sums all catches; if the total exceeds 80 % of the lake’s weight, each fisher who contributed to the excess is fined 0.01 kg per kilogram over and no further fishing is permitted that day. All fines and deposits are logged in the registrar’s ledger and the pooled material is earmarked for lake maintenance, gear repair, and community fishery support.

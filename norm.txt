Policy: Each fisherman may take up to 3 kg per trip, the community’s total daily catch cannot exceed 45 kg, the lake must retain at least 8 kg of fish at all times, and every fisher is guaranteed a minimum catch of 1 kg per trip.

Operationalization: On each fishing day each fisher records their catch and deposits a 1 kg token into the communal ledger; the daily total is tallied; if the ledger shows a catch exceeding 45 kg or if after a catch the lake would hold fewer than 8 kg, the fisherman must cease fishing for that day. A community monitor checks lake levels and ledger entries; violations result in a one‑trip fishing ban for the offender. All fishermen meet monthly to review ledger and lake data, and can vote to adjust the 8 kg retention threshold if the lake stock rises.

Policy: No fisher may take more than 12 kg per trip, and the lake must retain at least 25 % of its pre‑trip stock after each fishing round.

Operationalization: 1. Before each trip the Community Watch records the lake’s current stock with a sonar gauge and notes the pre‑trip value. 2. Every fisher logs the weight of their haul immediately after finishing; if the haul exceeds 12 kg the surplus is seized by the Watch and added to the communal fish reserve. 3. After all hauls the Watch calculates the remaining stock and checks that it is ≥25 % of the pre‑trip value. 4. A rotating verifier (a different fisher each round) cross‑checks the Watch’s measurement on the spot; if there is a discrepancy the community holds a quick majority vote to either accept the Watch’s figure or halt fishing until a new measurement can be taken. 5. If the lake falls below the 25 % threshold fishing is paused indefinitely until the next scheduled round’s stock meets or exceeds that level; no new hauls may occur during the pause. 6. Violations of the 12 kg limit or the 25 % rule result in a suspension of the offender’s fishing rights for the next round, with the excess fish held in the communal reserve and a one‑kg fine paid directly to the reserve (or equivalent labor). 7. Membership is defined by signing a commitment on the community board; inactive or violating members may be suspended or removed following a majority vote. 8. All fines, suspensions, and labor assignments are decided by the community at the meeting where the rule was adopted, with the fisher offering the required fish or labor that best meets community needs. 9. The communal fish reserve is stored in the dock shed and may be redistributed or used for community meals; it is not a penalty pool but a tool to keep fish within the community.

Policy: Each fisher may take up to 1.5 kg per trip, and the lake must always hold at least 7 kg of fish; if a catch would drop the lake below 7 kg, the fisher must return enough fish to keep it at 7 kg.

Operationalization: Before fishing, a fisher checks the lake stock (visual estimate or net count). They may net up to 1.5 kg; if their catch would bring the lake below 7 kg, they must release or keep back the excess. At the end of each trip, the fisher reports their catch to the community council. If they exceed the 1.5 kg limit or fail to keep the lake at 7 kg, they pay a fee of 0.5 kg of fish (or its monetary value) to the reserve. If a fisher fails to bring in the minimum 1 kg needed for survival, they must contribute an equivalent amount to the reserve or receive a one‑time rescue catch from another fisher.

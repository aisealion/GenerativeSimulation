Policy: Each fisher may take up to 1.5 kg per trip, must deposit 30 % of that catch into the lake reserve; fishing is suspended if the combined lake mass (lake stock + lake reserve) falls below 2 kg and resumes when it reaches 5 kg, and each fisher has a seasonal cap of 3 kg per calendar month.

Operationalization: After each trip, the fisher computes 30 % of the total catch, removes that amount from their haul and deposits it into the lake reserve. The deposit amount, the updated lake reserve total, and the fisher’s cumulative monthly catch are recorded in the communal ledger by the fisher, then verified by the ledger keeper who updates the LakeReserve field and the combined LakeMass (LakeStock + LakeReserve). If a fisher’s monthly catch reaches 3 kg they must wait until the next calendar month before fishing again. The community council monitors the LakeMass; when it falls below 2 kg all fishing is suspended until the mass reaches 5 kg, at which point fishing may resume. The ledger keeper also checks each fisher’s 30 % deposit against the reported catch every morning to ensure accuracy.

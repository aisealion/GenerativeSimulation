Policy: Each fisherman may fish up to 4 h per day and carry no more than 3 kg per haul; any excess fish beyond these limits is deposited in a communal pit, and all fish that comply are kept by the fisher. The watchman, selected by daily rotation, verifies logs and scale readings, imposes a 5‑minute community‑work penalty for non‑compliance, and, if the work is not completed, an additional 10‑minute penalty or temporary bar; the village leader, with input from the fisher council, decides how pit fish are used.

Operationalization: Every fisher records departure and return times in a notebook and weighs each haul on the communal scale. The daily watchman, who is not fishing that day, checks the logs and scale readings before any fisher leaves the dock. If a fisher exceeds 4 h or a haul exceeds 3 kg, the watchman records the excess, orders the fisher to deposit it in the communal pit, and mandates 5 minutes of net cleaning or fish washing, timed by a sand timer. Failure to complete the work results in a further 10 minutes of community work or a temporary ban. All compliant catch stays with the fisher; the pit fish are later reviewed by the village leader and the fisher council, who decide whether to use them for communal meals or sell them to fund lake maintenance.

Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 70% of the lake’s current stock per trip; 0.5% of each catch is deposited into a communal reserve that must hold at least 5 kg when the lake’s stock exceeds 5 kg; if the reserve falls below 5 kg, the fisher who caused the dip must contribute 0.5 kg per trip until it reaches 5 kg.

Operationalization: Before each trip, a fisher records the intended catch and checks the current lake stock. They may take up to 70% of that stock. After fishing, they deposit 0.5% of their catch into the reserve (or nothing if the lake is below 5 kg). The reserve balance is tracked by the community steward. If at the end of any fishing session the reserve drops below 5 kg, the fisher whose catch caused the dip must add 0.5 kg to the reserve on every subsequent trip until the reserve reaches 5 kg. Failure to meet the deposit or to make the required contribution results in a temporary fishing ban for one round.

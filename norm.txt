Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 70% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 150 kg; must keep 1 kg for sustenance, deposit 1% of the catch (rounded to 0.1 kg) into a communal reserve that must always be at least 30 kg, and the lake must never fall below 80 kg after any trip.

Operationalization: Before each trip, a fisher checks lake stock; if the stock is X, the fisher calculates 70% of X, takes the lesser of that value or 150 kg, subtracts 1 kg for themselves, then deposits 1% of the remaining catch into the communal reserve. The communal reserve is tracked in a shared ledger updated each trip; if a fisher tries to fish beyond these limits, they must forfeit the trip and the reserve is replenished by a community pool to keep above 30 kg.

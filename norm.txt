Policy: Solo may take up to 30% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 5 kg; all other fishers may take up to 15% per trip, capped at 3 kg.

Operationalization: Before each trip, all fishers read the lake’s current stock with the communal gauge. Solo then harvests up to 30% of that number, but no more than 5 kg; other fishers harvest up to 15% but no more than 3 kg. After catching, each fisher records the exact weight taken in the community ledger. If a fisher exceeds the limit, the excess weight must be returned or a small fee paid to the reserve, and the fisher is barred from the next trip for one week. The community meets monthly to review harvest totals and enforce penalties.

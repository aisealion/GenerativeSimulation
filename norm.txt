Policy: No fisher may harvest more than 10% of the lake’s current biomass per trip, and the lake must retain at least 10% of its total biomass after each round. If a fisher exceeds the 10% cap, the excess catch must be returned and a penalty equal to 50% of the excess is deposited into the community fund; if the lake’s remaining biomass falls below 10%, all fishers’ future allowable catch is reduced by 5% per consecutive violation until the retention rule is restored.

Operationalization: At the start of each round the community gathers to estimate the lake’s current biomass via joint sampling; the sum of all samples is recorded in the shared ledger as B. Each fisher’s baseline allowance is then 10% of B. The verifier for the round is chosen by simple majority vote, alphabetic tie‑breaker; this verifier must perform a random sample within 24 h of the round’s end and record the remaining biomass in the ledger. If a fisher’s catch exceeds the baseline allowance, the excess must be returned to the lake (or communal pool) immediately, and the verifier transfers 50% of that excess from the fisher’s personal balance to the community fund. If the verifier’s sample shows the lake’s remaining biomass is below 10% of B, a violation flag is set; the next round’s allowance for every fisher is multiplied by 0.95. Consecutive violations compound the multiplier (0.95^n). The multiplier resets to 1 when a round ends with at least 10% retention. Any fisher may report a violation by entering a clear incident in the ledger; if no violation is reported the following round’s allowance is automatically reduced by 5% for all. The ledger automatically enforces penalties and tracks balances, so no separate treasurer is needed.

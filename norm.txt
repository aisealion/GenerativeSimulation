Policy: Each fisher may take up to 70 % of the lake’s current stock per trip, but must leave at least 5 % of the current stock as a reserve. Failure to leave the reserve triggers a fine of 1 kg per kg of reserve lost; if the reserve falls below 5 % for two consecutive rounds, all quotas are reduced by 20 % for the next round.

Operationalization: Before each trip the fisher reads the current lake stock from the community register, calculates 70 % of that value, and plans a catch that will leave at least 5 % of the stock as reserve. After the catch the fisher records three entries in the register: ‘catch’ (kg caught), ‘reserve’ (kg remaining in the lake), and ‘fine’ (kg of fine, zero if reserve ≥ 5 %). The community council’s enforcement committee audits these entries each round, imposes the fine on the fisher’s account, and collects the funds into the community purse. The council also tracks the ‘reserve’ field each round; if reserve < 5 % for two consecutive rounds, the council updates the rule in the register to change the allowable catch fraction from 70 % to 56 % (a 20 % reduction) for all fishers in the following round and announces the change at the community meeting. All updates—including the new quota fraction and any fines—are logged in the register and communicated to every fisher.

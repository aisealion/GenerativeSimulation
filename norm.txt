Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 60% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 120kg, must keep 1kg for sustenance, deposit 0.5kg for every 5kg of catch into a communal reserve that must remain at least 10kg, and the lake must never fall below 50kg after any trip.

Operationalization: At the end of each trip, a fisher records total catch and deposits the required amount into the reserve. The community reserve is tracked weekly; if it dips below 10kg, an extra 0.5kg per 5kg of catch must be deposited until the reserve reaches 10kg. Failure to deposit the correct amount results in the entire catch being forfeited to the reserve on the next trip. A community steward enforces the rule and records all transfers, with violations reported and resolved by a simple majority vote.

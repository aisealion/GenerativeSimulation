Policy: Each fisher may harvest no more than 1.3 kg per trip or 6 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller, and after every catch the lake must retain at least 80 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve.

Operationalization: Before each fishing trip, each fisher reports intended catch weight to the communal ledger. The lake’s biomass is measured quarterly by a rotating community officer. If a fisher exceeds the limit, the excess must be returned and the fisher must participate in a community catch‑down to restore the reserve. All deviations are noted in the ledger and discussed at monthly meetings; repeated non‑compliance results in temporary fishing suspension for that fisher.

Policy: Each fisher may take up to 3 kg per trip, must keep at least 1 kg for sustenance; any excess must be returned to the lake and the fisher receives a warning; a second violation results in a one‑day suspension.

Operationalization: 1. The lake’s stock_kg is updated only when fish are physically taken from or returned to the lake; the keeper records the weight of each catch and any return of excess fish in the evening ledger. 2. The communal reserve is a shared bucket in the community hall, tracked in a shared ledger; it can only be borrowed from when it contains enough fish to cover the requested amount. 3. Each fisher submits a trip sheet with exact catch weight; if under 1 kg, the fisher records the borrowed amount on the shared ledger and deposits that weight into the reserve. 4. The keeper—elected by majority vote each season—maintains the ledger, updates the reserve inventory daily, and enforces rules. 5. If a fisher exceeds 3 kg, the keeper records a warning in the daily log, requires the excess to be returned to the lake, and if the same fisher violates again, a one‑day suspension notice is posted and recorded in the community log. 6. Failure to return borrowed fish by the next trip results in the keeper recording the debt, applying a 10 % penalty to the outstanding amount, and if the debt remains after the following trip, a one‑day suspension is imposed. 7. If a trip sheet is not submitted by the end of the day, the keeper logs a “missing sheet” entry; the fisher has 24 h to submit it, otherwise the keeper records a warning and treats the day’s catch as 0 kg. A second missing sheet triggers the one‑day suspension. 8. All ledger entries, warnings, penalties, and suspensions are publicly posted in the community log for transparency.

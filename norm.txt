Policy: Each fisher may keep all of their catch, but must donate exactly 1 kg per trip to a communal reserve that funds lake maintenance and emergency feeding. If a fisher fails to donate, they must pay a 2 kg penalty to the reserve and are barred from fishing for one day until the penalty is paid.

Operationalization: 1) After each trip the fisher places a marked 1 kg portion into the communal bin. The bin sits on a calibrated scale that records a 1 kg increase; the rotating overseer immediately reads the weight, logs the fisher’s name, trip number and timestamp into the shared digital ledger, and signs or scans a QR‑code.\n2) The overseer checks the bin daily, and if a fisher’s name is missing from that day’s ledger the fisher is recorded as having failed to donate.\n3) Upon a missed donation the overseer writes a penalty notice in the ledger: a 2 kg deposit is required and a one‑day fishing ban is imposed.\n4) The fisherman deposits 2 kg on the ban day, the overseer verifies the weight, logs it, signs it, and the ban is lifted immediately.\n5) Any fisher may submit a reserve‑spending request as a ledger entry.\n6) The request is opened for a simple‑majority vote of all fishers (at least 51 % signed entries).\n7) If approved the overseer releases the requested amount from the bin.\n8) The overseer rotates weekly, drawn by random draw at the weekly meeting; each week the new overseer assumes the role, checks the bin daily, and maintains the ledger.\n9) The communal bin is labeled with the community name and placed in a visible location.\n10) All ledger entries are time‑stamped and stored in a shared digital system accessible to all fishers.

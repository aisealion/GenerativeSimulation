Policy: Each fisher may take up to 35 kg per trip, provided the lake retains at least 1 % of its pre‑catch stock as a reserve after the catch. Exceeding the quota or failing to leave the reserve incurs a fine of 0.02 kg per kg over, and all quotas are reduced by 25 % in the next round (the 35 kg cap becomes 26.25 kg per trip).

Operationalization: A trip is a continuous outing from shore to shore. Fisher logs the catch in the communal ledger. After the catch the ledger records remaining lake stock; if it falls below 1 % of the pre‑catch stock, the fisher must retain enough fish until the 1 % threshold is met. Any over‑catch is fined at 0.02 kg per kg over, the fine is subtracted from the fisher’s personal tally and added to the communal reserve pool. The fishery monitor reviews all trip logs; if a violation is detected the fine is applied automatically and the council reduces every fisher’s 35 kg limit by 25 % for the next round (new limit = 35 kg × 0.75 = 26.25 kg, or 75 % of any previously reduced cap). The 1 % reserve is left in the lake and may be taken in subsequent rounds if the rule still permits. The ledger tracks all catch, fines, and reserve balances transparently for the community.

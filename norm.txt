Policy: Each fisher may take up to 35 kg per trip, provided the lake retains at least 5 % of its pre‑catch stock; if the reserve falls below 5 %, the fisher must surrender 1 kg of fish for every 1 kg of reserve lost to the community keeper’s reserve, and the keeper will announce a 20 % reduction in all individual quotas for the next round; failing to submit a catch log incurs a 0.5 kg fine and a one‑round suspension.

Operationalization: 1. The keeper is elected by all fishers each round via the same voting mechanism used for policy proposals. 2. Before each trip the keeper records the lake’s current stock (pre‑catch). 3. After the trip the keeper records the catch weight and calculates the post‑catch reserve; if the reserve is less than 5 % of the pre‑catch stock, the keeper calculates the shortfall, collects the equivalent fish from the fisher, and adds it to the community reserve ledger. 4. The keeper holds the reserve fish securely; at the end of the round the keeper releases 10 % of the current reserve stock back into the lake to aid population rebuilding. 5. If the reserve remains below 5 % for two consecutive rounds, the keeper may use part of the reserve to support restocking projects or to reduce future quotas for fishers who repeatedly violate the reserve rule. 6. If the reserve is still below 5 % after the round, the keeper announces a 20 % reduction in the 35 kg per‑trip quota for all fishers in the following round. 7. A fisher who fails to submit a catch log for a trip is fined 0.5 kg and suspended from fishing for one round.

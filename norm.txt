Policy: Each fisher may take no more than 20 kg per trip, all catch is recorded in a shared ledger, and the lake’s communal reserve must stay at 50 kg for emergencies; violations result in warnings, penalties, and bans adjudicated by the Community Council.

Operationalization: • 20 kg cap applies to every fisher on each trip, regardless of regularity.  • After each return, the fisher must log exact catch weight and any surplus over 20 kg to the shared ledger within 24 h.  • The keeper, rotated monthly by the Community Council, stores all ledger entries, updates the communal reserve balance, and monitors the reserve weekly.  • The reserve is held at 50 kg; if it falls below, the keeper alerts the council and all fishers, who must bring extra catch within the next week to restore the target.  • If it exceeds 50 kg, the keeper records the surplus and the council decides whether to keep it as safety stock or allocate it for community meals; individuals may not remove surplus without council approval.  • First violation (failure to record or exceeding 20 kg) triggers a written warning, a brief explanation at the next weekly meeting, and a 5 kg penalty added to the reserve.  • Second violation leads to a temporary 30‑day ban, a written notice, public announcement at the weekly meeting, and a 5 kg penalty.  • Third violation triggers a council review; a permanent ban may be imposed by majority vote, logged on the ledger, and announced.  • Appeals must be submitted within 48 h; the ban is suspended pending review.  • The council reviews appeals within 5 business days, may lift, reduce, or maintain the ban, and records the decision.  • Weekly meetings review lake biomass and, if needed, adjust the 20 kg cap; the new cap takes effect immediately after the vote and is logged on the ledger.  • All ledger entries, bans, appeals, and reserve adjustments are visible to every fisher.

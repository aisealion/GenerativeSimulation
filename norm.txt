Policy: No fisher may take more than 15 kg of fish per trip; any fish beyond that must be released within 30 minutes of returning. Fishers must weigh their catch before departure and record the weight on the shared log; the community leader reviews the log daily and revokes a fisher’s right to fish the next day if a violation is found, but automatically lifts revocation the following day unless a new violation occurs, with the option for appeal during the community meeting.

Operationalization: 1. Before departing, each fisher checks the weight of their catch with a calibrated scale and records the exact weight on the shared log. 2. The fisher submits the pre‑trip weight to the community leader who updates the log and maintains a status table for each fisher. 3. After returning, if the recorded weight exceeds 15 kg, the fisher must release the excess fish to a designated watchman or fellow fisher within 30 minutes and record the release time and method (e.g., verbal confirmation or photo) in the log. 4. The community watchman verifies the 30‑minute release window and updates the log with confirmation. 5. The community leader reviews all log entries each day; if a violation is found, the leader marks the fisher’s status as ‘revoked’ for the following day and notes the violation. 6. On each morning, the leader checks the status table; a revoked fisher cannot fish that day. 7. At the start of the following day, the leader automatically clears the revoke flag, restoring permission, unless a new violation has been recorded. 8. A revoked fisher may present an appeal to the community during the scheduled community meeting; if the community agrees, the fisher’s permission is reinstated immediately.

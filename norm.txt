Policy: Each fisher may take up to 90 % of the lake’s current biomass per trip; if the communal reserve falls below 5 kg, each fisher must deposit 2 % of their catch into the reserve; if the reserve falls below 3 kg, the maximum take for all fishers is reduced to 70 % for the following trip until the reserve reaches at least 5 kg.

Operationalization: After each trip, the fisher submits a ledger entry with fields: fisher_id, catch_weight, deposit_amount (automatically 2 % of catch if reserve <5 kg), new_reserve_balance, and timestamp. The system calculates the required deposit and checks the catch against the current maximum take (90 % or 70 % if reserve <3 kg). If the deposit is insufficient or the catch exceeds the allowed percentage, a flag is set on that ledger entry. The community council, a rotating group of fishers, reviews flagged entries; they vote on a penalty (fine or suspension). A fine reduces the fisher’s personal balance (balance field) by the fine amount and credits the same amount to the communal reserve (communal_reserve field). The penalty decision is recorded as a new ledger entry with fields: fisher_id, penalty_type, penalty_amount, council_vote_result, timestamp. A suspension prevents the fisher from logging a new trip until lifted, indicated by a suspension flag in the ledger. All ledger entries are publicly viewable, allowing any fisher to verify compliance. The communal reserve is updated instantly on each deposit or fine, and the personal balance is updated on each fine.

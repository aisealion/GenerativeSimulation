Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 20% of the lake’s current biomass per trip, capped at 2 kg, and the lake must keep at least 35% of its biomass as a communal reserve.

Operationalization: After each trip, the community records each fisher’s catch on a shared ledger. The lake’s remaining biomass is estimated by a rotating recorder who checks the visible fish and estimates total weight. If, after a round, the remaining biomass falls below 35% of the lake’s current total, the fisher(s) whose combined catch exceeded the 20%/2 kg limit must pay a penalty equal to the shortfall to restore the reserve, or lose the right to fish the next round. The rule applies every round, with the percentage limits recalculated based on the new lake biomass. All fishers must adhere; non‑compliance results in the penalty or temporary ban.

Policy: Each fisher must set aside 1 kg of their catch per trip for the community pot, retaining the remainder.

Operationalization: 1. After each trip, the fisher records the total haul on the communal ledger, subtracts 1 kg, drops that kilogram into the pot, and signs a receipt. 2. The pot keeper, chosen weekly by lottery, weighs the pot the next morning to verify that the cumulative weight equals the sum of signed receipts. 3. If the weight is lower, the keeper brings the pot to a community meeting, compares the discrepancy with the ledger, and asks each fisher to confirm their deposit. 4. The fisher who failed to deposit (or cannot prove it) is identified and must add the shortfall from their next haul before fishing again. 5. If a fisher still fails to clear the debt before the next trip, they must catch an extra kilogram or deposit the owed kilogram into the pot; repeated non‑compliance results in a temporary suspension from fishing. 6. A suspended fisher clears the debt by contributing the owed kilogram from personal reserves or an agreed alternative before fishing again. 7. All ledger entries, receipts, and pot weights are displayed on the communal board for transparency, and the pot is held collectively by the community with Kai as treasurer.

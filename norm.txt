Policy: Fishing may only occur when lake biomass is at least 12 kg; each fisher can take no more than 2 kg per cycle, 1 kg subsistence guaranteed, the other 1 kg optional and subject to a combined 5 % of current biomass; 15 % of the lake must remain permanently reserved.

Operationalization: At the start of each six‑month cycle, community leaders measure lake biomass. Fisheries are registered only if the threshold is met. Each fisher logs a 1 kg subsistence catch and may claim up to an additional 1 kg provided total community catch does not exceed 5 % of current biomass. Excess over quota must be returned to the lake. Violations trigger a communal penalty and temporary ban. The 15 % reserve is maintained by ensuring the combined catch never reduces biomass below 15 % of its current level.

Policy: Each fisher may keep at most 15 kg per trip; any fish beyond that must be released into a communal reserve. At month‑end the reserve is divided equally among fishers whose cumulative net catch for the month is below 30 kg.

Operationalization: 1. On each trip, a fisher logs their net catch (≤15 kg) on a shared sheet before leaving the lake. 2. Any fish taken over 15 kg is released daily by the fisher into a locked cool‑box on the dock, and the keeper records the release. 3. The keeper, elected by simple majority of all fishers each month and replaceable mid‑month if duties are not performed, collects all released fish daily and stores them in the cool‑box. 4. The keeper runs a spreadsheet each month that sums every fisher’s logged net catches to compute their personal reserve (cumulative net catch kept that month). 5. At month‑end, fishers with personal reserves under 30 kg are identified; the entire communal reserve is divided equally among them. 6. If a fisher fails to return excess fish, the keeper cancels the trip’s haul entirely and moves the whole haul into the communal reserve, but the fisher remains eligible for that month’s share. 7. If no fisher qualifies (all reserves ≥30 kg), the reserve remains locked and carried over to the next month or kept for emergency use until a fisher’s reserve falls below 30 kg.

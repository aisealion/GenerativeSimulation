Policy: Each fisher may keep up to 12 kg of fish per trip; excess fish are deposited into a communal maintenance reserve that is split evenly among all ten fishers at month‑end and added to each fisher’s monthly allowance; a fisher who fails to release excess fish forfeits his monthly reserve share and is barred from fishing the following month until he complies.

Operationalization: Before each trip, a fisher records the total catch, keeps the first 12 kg, and deposits any excess into the sealed communal reserve container at the dock. A keeper is selected by a random draw from all fishers at the start of each month; the keeper seals the container, records its weight at month‑end, and submits the total reserve weight to the council. The council divides the reserve equally among the ten fishers, credits each fisher’s allowance in the communal ledger, and records the transaction for audit by the enforcement committee. A fisher who fails to release excess fish forfeits his share of that month’s reserve and is prohibited from fishing the next month until he complies; enforcement of the ban and compliance checks is the responsibility of the council or elected enforcement committee. The monthly allowance can be used like personal catch but all new catch beyond the allowance still must obey the 12 kg per trip release rule.

Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 70% of the lake’s current stock per trip; 0.5% of each catch is deposited into a communal reserve that must hold at least 5 kg. If the reserve falls below 5 kg, only the fisher who caused the dip must contribute 0.5 kg per trip until it reaches 5 kg.

Operationalization: Before each trip, a fisher declares their intended catch (up to 70% of current stock). They take the catch, immediately deposit 0.5% of it into a communal container, and keep the rest for themselves. After every trip, the reserve level is checked; if it is below 5 kg, the fisher who just caught will add 0.5 kg from their personal haul (or from a pooled community fund) until the reserve is back to 5 kg. All catch logs are recorded on a shared ledger visible to the community, and any fisher who violates the 70% cap or fails to deposit the required reserve share is fined 1 kg from their next haul.

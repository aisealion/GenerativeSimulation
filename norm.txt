Policy: No fisher may harvest more than 10% of the lake’s current biomass on a single trip, and the lake must retain at least 10% of its total biomass after each round; the Fish Ledger Keeper will hold the community fish bank, collect excess catch, release fish to restore the 10% threshold, and enforce a one‑round ban on any fisher who violates the rule.

Operationalization: 1. The Fish Ledger Keeper records the lake’s pre‑round biomass. 2. Each fisher logs their catch; if a fisher’s catch exceeds 10% of the lake’s current biomass, the excess is deposited into the community fish bank and the fisher is flagged for potential ban. 3. After all trips, the Keeper calculates the lake’s remaining biomass. 4. If the remaining biomass is below 10% of the pre‑round biomass, the Keeper computes a deficit: deficit = 0.10 × pre‑round biomass – remaining biomass. 5. The Keeper releases fish from the community fish bank equal to the deficit (or all remaining bank stock if it is lower). 6. Released fish are added back to the lake, and all deposits, releases, and flags are recorded on the Fish Ledger. 7. Any fisher whose catch contributed to the shortfall—either by exceeding the 10% trip limit or by causing the lake to fall below 10%—receives a one‑round fishing ban that begins with the next fishing round. 8. The bank balance is updated after each release, and natural regeneration is assumed to add to the lake’s biomass each round. 9. All ledger entries are publicly available to ensure transparency and accountability.

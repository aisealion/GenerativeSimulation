Policy: Each fisher may take up to 1.5 kg per trip, must deposit 0.4 kg into the shared pool, must leave at least 20 % of the lake’s current stock untouched, and the community’s total daily catch may not exceed 70 % of the lake’s weight.

Operationalization: The keeper is elected each morning by secret ballot from volunteers who have not served in the last three days, with a tie broken by drawing lots. The keeper sits by the lake and records every fisher’s haul and deposit on a ledger; after each trip, the fisher deposits 0.4 kg into the locked communal chest. The keeper sums the day’s total haul and, before allowing a new trip, checks that adding the proposed haul would not push the cumulative catch over 70 % of the current lake weight nor reduce the remaining stock below 20 %. If either threshold would be breached, fishing for that day is halted. If a fisher exceeds the 1.5 kg limit or fails to deposit, the keeper imposes a fine equal to 20 % of the overage, withdraws that amount from the pool, and the fisher forfeits the right to fish the following day. After the penalty day passes, the keeper returns 50 % of the fisher’s deposit to the fisher; the remaining 50 % stays in the pool for lake restoration, equipment repairs, or future fines. All actions are logged, and the keeper’s role is rotated daily to prevent concentration of power.

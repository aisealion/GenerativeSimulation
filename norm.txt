Policy: Solo may harvest up to 50% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 6 kg; all other fishers may harvest up to 30% per trip, capped at 3 kg.

Operationalization: At the start of each trip, the community records the lake’s current stock. Solo draws 0.5 × stock (max 6 kg); each other fisher draws 0.3 × stock (max 3 kg). If a fisher attempts to take more than the limit, the excess is forfeited to the communal reserve and a penalty equal to 10 % of the excess weight is paid to the reserve. If a fisher cannot meet their quota due to lack of fish, they must make up the shortfall in the next trip or pay the penalty. The rule is enforced by a rotating recorder who tallies catches and imposes penalties.

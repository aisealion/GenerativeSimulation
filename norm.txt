Policy: Each fisher may take at most 1.5 units of fish per day, and no more than 10% of the total catch that day.

Operationalization: On each trip, the fisher counts fish taken. After all trips, the community tallies the total catch. If a fisher exceeds the 1.5 unit or 10% limit, the excess fish are returned to the lake or stored for community use. This is done by hand count and verbal confirmation at the gathering place; no special equipment is needed.

Policy: Each fisher may take up to 1 kg per trip; the community’s total weekly harvest may not exceed 70 % of the lake’s current stock, with a hard minimum of 1 kg remaining in the lake.

Operationalization: At the start of each week the fisher who goes out first measures the lake’s current stock by a quick count or sample net‑catch, records the figure on the shared ledger, and the community agrees on the number. The community then calculates the 70 % cap (rounded down to the nearest 0.1 kg) and posts it in the ledger. Every fisher brings their catch to the dock where the designated verifier weighs each fish; if a fisher’s single catch exceeds 1 kg or the cumulative weekly harvest exceeds the 70 % cap, the verifier logs the violation, signs the ledger, and imposes a one‑week ban on that fisher. The banned fisher must deliver a replacement fish of equal weight and species to the community storage at the dock within the same week; the verifier checks the replacement, records it in the ledger, and lifts the ban. The verifier role rotates weekly among the community; all records are kept in the shared ledger for transparency.

Policy: No individual fisher may catch more than 8% of the lake’s current biomass in any single trip.

Operationalization: 1. Every week, a fisher is rotated to the role of "biomass surveyor" by community council vote; that fisher sets up a standard 2‑m gill net, nets for 30 minutes, weighs all fish, and multiplies the haul weight by three to estimate the lake’s total biomass. 2. The estimated biomass is recorded in the communal ledger along with date and surveyor’s name; this value becomes the basis for all quotas for the following week. 3. Each fisher’s quota is 8% of that weekly estimate; they record their haul weight in the ledger after each trip. 4. If a fisher exceeds their quota, the surplus is deducted from their next trip’s quota and a reminder is issued by the next scheduled surveyor. 5. A persistent violation is defined as exceeding the 8% quota on two or more consecutive trips or accumulating an excess over a four‑week period that totals more than 4% of the lake’s current biomass. 6. Upon detection of a persistent violation, the fisher who observes it (or any fisher who reports it) calls a community meeting; the council convenes, reviews the ledger, and decides corrective actions such as temporary quota reductions or education sessions. 7. All procedures, ledger entries, and meeting minutes are publicly posted for transparency and accountability.

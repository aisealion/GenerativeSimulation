Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 12 kg per day, and 5% of each fisher’s catch must be deposited into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 20 kg in the lake.

Operationalization: Before the trip, each fisher logs their intended catch to the community ledger. After the catch, the fisher deposits 5% of their haul (rounded to the nearest 0.1 kg) into the communal bin. The bin’s weight is checked daily by the council; if it falls below 20 kg, the next fisher must forgo 5% of their catch until the reserve is restored. If a fisher fails to deposit the required amount, they lose the right to fish for one day and must donate an extra 5 kg to the communal reserve on the next trip.

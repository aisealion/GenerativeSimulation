Policy: Each fisher may keep up to 25% of the lake’s current fish mass per trip, with at least 1kg reserved for personal sustenance.

Operationalization: Every fisher records their catch in the communal ledger immediately after each trip. The ledger is reviewed by a rotating chair chosen by community vote. If a fisher exceeds the 25% cap, they must release 50% of the excess fish back to the lake immediately; if they refuse or fail to comply, they are barred from fishing for one round and must repay any excess in the next available round.

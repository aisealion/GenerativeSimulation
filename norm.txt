Policy: Each fisher may take up to 22 % of the lake’s current biomass per trip, must deposit 5 % of their catch into a communal reserve, and any over‑quota catch will trigger a 10 % reduction of the next trip’s allowance (cumulatively applied to the 22 % base) until compliance is restored; missed deposits are treated the same way.

Operationalization: Before each trip the community gathers at the dock, reads the shared sonar/visual count to determine the lake’s biomass, and the leading fisher (rotating each trip or chosen by vote) announces the number and records it in the ledger. Each fisher records their catch, deposits 5 % of that catch into the communal reserve jar, and keeps the remainder. The leading fisher collects all deposits; if any fisher fails to deposit, the missing amount must be paid on the next trip, and a 10 % reduction of their next trip allowance is applied for each missed deposit until the deposit is made. For over‑quota catches, the next trip allowance is reduced by 10 % of the 22 % base per over‑quota instance (22 % × 0.9, × 0.9, etc.); if the fisher returns to compliance the allowance resets to 22 %. Kai and I are responsible for collecting deposits, maintaining the ledger, and depositing the sum into the communal reserve jar; the community holds the reserve and can withdraw it for communal needs as agreed. If a fisher consistently over‑quota or fails to deposit, the community may adjust their role or impose additional penalties as agreed.

Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 10% of the lake’s current biomass per trip, capped at 1.5 kg, and the lake must retain at least 45% of its total biomass as a communal reserve.

Operationalization: Every trip the fisher weighs the catch and records it in a communal ledger. A protected reserve zone in the center of the lake is off‑limits. The community will monitor the lake’s total biomass by sampling once per month; if the reserve drops below 45% the next catch must be reduced until the reserve is restored. Any fisher who takes more than the allowed percentage must return the excess or pay a fine equal to 10% of the excess catch.

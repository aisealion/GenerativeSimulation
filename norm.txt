Policy: Each fisher may take up to 20 kg per trip, with a mandatory reserve of 15 % of the lake’s current stock; a 0.01 kg deposit is required at the start of each round and is forfeited if the fisher fails to log catch or breaches the reserve; if the reserve falls below 15 % during a round, all fishers’ quotas for that round are automatically scaled down by 20 %.

Operationalization: 1. **Round definition** – a round is a weekly cycle that begins when all fishers pay the 0.01 kg deposit and ends when the lake monitor closes the shared logbook. 2. **Deposit** – each fisher deposits 0.01 kg at the start of the round; the lake monitor collects the deposit, holds it in a community trust, and returns it only if the fisher logs his catch and the reserve rule is respected. 3. **Reserve calculation** – at the start of each round the monitor records the lake’s current stock and calculates 15 % of that number. That amount must remain unharvested during the round. 4. **Quota** – the default quota for every fisher is 20 kg per trip. 5. **Logging** – each fisher must submit a log of his catch weight by the end of the week; failure to log triggers forfeiture of the deposit and a 20 % reduction of that fisher’s quota for the remainder of the round. 6. **Reserve breach** – if after any fishing trip the lake’s remaining stock falls below the 15 % reserve calculated at the start, the monitor immediately applies a 20 % cut to all fishers’ quotas for that round. 7. **Lake monitor** – the monitor is a community‑appointed official selected by simple majority vote at the end of each fishing season. 8. **Fine enforcement** – the 0.01 kg fine for a missing deposit or other infractions remains in the community trust and may be used for fishery upkeep or redistributed in a future season. 9. **Transparency** – the monitor records all deposits, logs, stock measurements, and quota adjustments in the public logbook, which is accessible to all fishers.

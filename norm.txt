Policy: Each fisherman may take up to 4kg per trip, the community’s total daily catch must not exceed 30kg, and the lake must retain at least 15kg of fish at all times.

Operationalization: Every fisherman records their catch on a communal ledger after each trip. The community meets weekly to review the ledger and a quick lake survey. If someone exceeds their quota, they must return the excess catch and pay a fine equal to 10% of the extra amount. If the lake level falls below 15kg, all individual quotas are cut in half until the lake recovers, and non‑compliant fishermen are barred from fishing for one week.

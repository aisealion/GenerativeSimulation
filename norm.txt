Policy: Each fisher may take at most 8 kg of fish per trip and must deposit 25 % of the actual catch into the communal reserve; any trip exceeding 8 kg or missing the deposit triggers a 1 kg forfeiture on the next trip and 2 hours of community work.

Operationalization: After each trip, the fisher logs the raw catch on the shared ledger. If the catch is ≤8 kg, the fisher calculates 25 % of that catch and hands that amount to Sam. Sam records the deposit in the ledger, adds it to reserve_balance, and notes the new reserve total. If the fisher’s catch >8 kg or fails to deposit the required 25 %, Sam records a penalty: a 1 kg forfeiture on the next trip and 2 hours of community work. On the following trip, the fisher deducts 1 kg from the raw catch before calculating the deposit, logs the forfeiture in the ledger, and records the 2 hours of work in their trip record.

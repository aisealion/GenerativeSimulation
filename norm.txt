Policy: Each fisher may take a maximum of 12 kg per trip, keeping at least 1 kg for sustenance; the total catch per trip may not exceed 50% of the lake’s current stock, with proportional reductions applied before departure. If the stock falls below 200 kg, the community meets with a 75% quorum to vote on proposed cap options, and the adopted cap takes effect on the first departure after the meeting.

Operationalization: 1. Before departure each fisher records planned catch on the communal ledger. 2. The fish record keeper calculates the sum of planned catches; if it exceeds 50% of the current lake stock, the keeper applies the proportional reduction factor (0.5 × current stock ÷ summed planned catch) to each fisher’s planned catch and updates the ledger. 3. During departure, fishers adjust their catch according to the reduced plan. 4. After return, the keeper tallies actual catches, verifies no fisher exceeded 12 kg, and records any excess. 5. If a fisher exceeded 12 kg, the keeper removes the excess and marks the fisher with a “ban” on the ledger. 6. For each departure, the community council (three active fishers elected by simple majority at the previous meeting) reviews the ledger; a fisher with a ban is denied a departure slip for one trip. 7. The ledger keeper is chosen by simple majority vote at each meeting or by rotating among fishers; the keeper posts all rules and the current cap on the notice board. 8. If lake stock falls below 200 kg, a meeting is called with a quorum of at least 75% of active fishers; fishers submit proposed cap options (fixed kilos or percentages), the community votes by simple majority, and the adopted cap is posted on the notice board, taking effect on the first departure after the meeting. 9. If quorum is not met or no option receives a majority, the existing 12 kg cap remains in force until a valid meeting can be held.

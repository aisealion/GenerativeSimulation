Policy: Each fisher may take up to 30 kg per trip, with a mandatory reserve of 5 % of the lake’s current stock; any catch beyond 30 kg is a fine to be deposited in the community fund, and the reserve must never fall below 5 % or all quotas will be automatically reduced by 20 % for the next round.

Operationalization: After every trip, weigh the haul: keep up to 30 kg, and set aside any excess in the communal jar as a fish‑weight fine. The jar is the community fund, held in a communal jar at the dock, monitored by the community treasurer. At the start of each round the community calculates the lake’s current stock by adding last round’s total catch; the 5 % reserve is then determined from that stock. If the reserve dips below 5 %, the community automatically applies a 20 % quota reduction for all fishers in the following round without a vote. At season’s end the fines in the jar are counted and either redistributed to fishers proportionally or earmarked for collective use (nets, fences, restocking) as decided in a monthly council meeting.

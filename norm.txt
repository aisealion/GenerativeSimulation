Policy: No fisher may net more than 20 kg per trip; excess must be returned on the spot and a 1 kg fine added to the communal reserve, with a one‑week ban for non‑compliance, all logged in the shared ledger and overseen by a rotating steward.

Operationalization: 1. A "trip" begins when a fisher leaves the dock and ends when they return to unload. 2. Immediately upon docking, the fisher records the total catch weight in the communal ledger, marking their name, date, and weight. 3. If the recorded weight exceeds 20 kg, the fisher must return the excess weight to the lake on the spot. 4. The current steward, selected by a community‑wide rotation schedule agreed at the start of each month, verifies the return by weighing the returned fish with a portable scale and recording the returned weight in the ledger, then signs the entry. 5. A fine of 1 kg is automatically added to the communal reserve field (communal_reserve_kg) and the fisher’s fine column is increased by 1 kg. 6. The ledger is updated to show the fine, returned weight, and steward’s signature. 7. The steward reviews the ledger daily; any fisher who logs more than 20 kg without returning the excess is flagged for a one‑week ban. 8. The ban entry includes fisher name, start date (current date), end date (start date + 7 days), and is displayed in the ledger. 9. During the ban period the fisher’s entries are locked and they cannot log any catch. 10. When the ban expires, the steward clears the ban status from the ledger. 11. All entries are visible to the community; the ledger’s communal_reserve_kg field reflects the total fines collected. 12. The steward’s rotation ensures no single fisher holds audit power and the process remains transparent.

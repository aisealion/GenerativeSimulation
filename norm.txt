Policy: Each fisher may take at most 1.3 kg per trip or 6 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller, and the lake must retain at least 85 % of its biomass as a communal reserve after every catch.

Operationalization: Before each trip a fisher records the intended catch in the shared ledger. After the trip the lake’s remaining biomass is measured; if the reserve falls below 85 % the fisher must return the excess fish to the lake or to communal storage until the reserve is restored. A rotating council monitors compliance; any violation results in a one‑trip fishing ban, with repeated violations leading to removal from the community’s fishing rights.

Policy: Each fisher may take at most 1.5 kg per trip; 30 % of each catch is deposited into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 30 % of the lake’s current biomass.

Operationalization: Before departure each fisher records his catch weight. The treasurer records 30 % of that weight (rounded to the nearest gram) into the communal reserve ledger. After the round, the treasurer updates the lake’s biomass and verifies the reserve holds at least 30 % of that biomass. If the reserve falls below the threshold, the community votes to either reduce the next catch to 1 kg or require an extra 10 % deposit of each catch until the reserve meets the requirement. Non‑compliance incurs a fine equal to the missing deposit, payable to the reserve. The ledger is reviewed every round and any adjustment to the deposit rate is voted on by the community.

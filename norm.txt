Policy: Each fisher may take up to 90 % of the lake’s current biomass per trip; if the communal reserve is below 5 kg, they must deposit 10 % of their catch into the reserve; if the reserve falls below 3 kg the maximum take is reduced by 30 % for the following trip until the reserve reaches at least 5 kg. Violations of maximum take or deposit requirements trigger a 10 % fine on the excess, immediate correction, a 20 % temporary quota reduction for the next two trips, and repeat offenders face permanent exclusion until the reserve is replenished.

Operationalization: At the start of each trip the keeper records the lake biomass and current reserve in the communal ledger; the fisher declares their intended catch and the keeper calculates a 90 % quota based on the lake biomass; if the catch exceeds the quota the keeper orders the excess to be returned or confiscated into the reserve, records the excess, imposes a 10 % fine on the excess as a FineRevenue entry, and adjusts the fisher’s net catch; if the reserve is below 5 kg the keeper mandates a 10 % deposit of the catch into the reserve and adds it to the reserve balance; if the reserve is below 3 kg the keeper flags a 30 % quota reduction for the next trip, which persists until the reserve reaches at least 5 kg; for any violation the keeper records a 20 % temporary quota reduction for the next two trips; repeat violations trigger a permanent exclusion until the reserve reaches at least 5 kg, after which the exclusion flag is cleared; after each trip the keeper updates the ledger with the new reserve balance, any fines, deposits, and quota‑reduction flags; a weekly audit is conducted by the community steward who compares ledger entries to actual fish and deposits in the communal pool and corrects any discrepancies immediately; the keeper is empowered by the community to enforce fines, confiscate excess catch, and adjust quotas, and all actions are recorded transparently so every fisher can see the current reserve and compliance status.

Policy: Each fisher may harvest at most 1 kg per trip or 2 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller, and the lake must retain at least 93 % of its total biomass after each catch; the community will enforce a monthly cap of 4 % of the lake’s 12‑month average biomass.

Operationalization: Before each trip, a fisher records the intended catch in a shared ledger. After fishing, the fisher reports the actual catch to the ledger. If the catch exceeds the 1 kg or 2 % limit, the excess must be returned to the lake or held as a communal reserve. After every catch, the lake’s biomass is updated and the communal reserve must be at least 93 % of the new total. At the end of each month, the cumulative catch of all fishers is compared to 4 % of the lake’s 12‑month average biomass; if exceeded, the community may require a proportional reduction in the next month’s allowance or impose a community‑approved penalty on the offending fisher.

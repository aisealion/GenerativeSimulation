Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 90% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 150kg, must keep 1kg for sustenance, deposit 1% of the catch (rounded to 0.1kg) into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 10kg, and the lake must never fall below 50kg after any trip.

Operationalization: On each trip, a fisher calculates 90% of the current lake stock and takes the lesser of that or 150kg. They keep 1kg for themselves, deposit 1% of the catch into the communal reserve (rounded to the nearest 0.1kg), and verify that the lake remains at least 50kg after the catch and deposit. If the lake would fall below 50kg, the fisher must reduce their catch to keep the lake above that threshold. The communal reserve is updated immediately after each catch. If a fisher fails to deposit the required amount, they are fined a small portion of their next catch and barred from fishing for the next round.

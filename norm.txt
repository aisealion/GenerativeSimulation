Policy: Each fisher may take up to 90 % of the lake’s current biomass per trip; if the communal reserve falls below 5 kg, each fisher must deposit 2 % of their catch into the reserve; if the reserve falls below 3 kg, the maximum take for all fishers is reduced to 70 % for the following trip until the reserve reaches at least 5 kg.

Operationalization: 1.  Before the trip the observer reads the reserve barrel with a calibrated gauge and records the weight in the communal ledger.
2.  During the trip each fisher weighs their haul on the dock scale.
3.  If the reserve is <5 kg, the fisher calculates 2 % of the haul weight, rounds to the nearest 0.01 kg, and pours that amount into the reserve barrel.
4.  The observer then records the deposit in the ledger and announces the next day’s maximum take (90 % or 70 %) based on the updated reserve level.
5.  If a fisher exceeds the allowed maximum when the reserve is <3 kg, the observer collects a fine of 1 kg of fish: the fisher sets aside the fish, the observer weighs it to confirm 1 kg, and deposits it into the reserve barrel (or a designated penalty bin). The fine is logged in the ledger.
6.  If a fisher fails to deposit the required 2 % or takes above the permitted percentage, the observer records a penalty of 1 kg and, if the breach is a take violation, the fisher loses their slot for that day until the reserve reaches ≥5 kg.
7.  The observer role rotates weekly among fishers or is chosen by community vote; this prevents gaming of the system.
8.  All ledger entries are read aloud at the next community meeting to ensure transparency.

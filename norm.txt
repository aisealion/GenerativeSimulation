Policy: The lake’s fishery is governed by a rule that the daily total catch may not exceed 50 % of the current stock, with each fisher allowed up to 1.75 kg per trip, required to keep at least 1 kg and deposit 0.75 kg back into the lake.

Operationalization: 1.  Ledger: Every fisher records the weight of each catch on the communal ledger and signs it immediately after the trip.  At the end of each day, the fisher sums the weight of all trips made that day and marks the daily total on the ledger.  2.  Compliance officer: A rotating compliance officer is elected by community vote at the weekly meeting; the position changes every week.  The officer reviews the ledger during the weekly meeting, sums the daily totals, and compares the total to 50 % of the lake’s current stock.  3.  Enforcement: If the total exceeds the limit, the officer orders the excess fish to be returned to the lake immediately.  For every kilogram over the limit, the offending fisher is fined 0.02 kg, which is added to the communal reserve.  4.  Reserve use: If the lake’s stock falls below 2 kg, the compliance officer calls a quick community session.  The community decides whether extra fish are needed; if so, the officer withdraws the necessary amount from the communal reserve and purchases fish from the local market or charters a small boat to stock the lake.  The purchase is recorded in the ledger and the reserve balance adjusted.  5.  Deposit and keep: Because the maximum catch per trip is 1.75 kg, each fisher can keep 1 kg and deposit 0.75 kg back into the lake, satisfying both the keep and deposit requirements.

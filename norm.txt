Policy: Each fisher may take up to 30% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 4 kg, and may not cumulatively take more than 60% of the lake’s stock at the start of each calendar month.

Operationalization: 1. On the first fishing day of each month, the designated lake monitor (rotated each month) measures the lake’s total fish stock and records the baseline in the shared ledger. 2. After each trip, every fisher logs their catch immediately on the ledger, noting weight and time. 3. The ledger is reviewed daily by the community; if a fisher’s log exceeds 30% of that month’s baseline or 4 kg, the excess is returned to the lake and the fisher sits out the next fishing day. 4. The cumulative monthly catch of each fisher is tracked; if it exceeds 60% of the initial stock, the fisher must return the excess and sit out the next day. 5. The lake monitor also serves as the fishing steward for the month, mediating disputes and ensuring ledger accuracy. 6. Quarterly, the community reviews the average initial stock of the past three months; if it consistently stays above 5 kg, any fisher may propose increasing the 60% monthly cumulative limit. 7. Proposals are discussed at the next community meeting and require a simple majority (or 2/3 if agreed) to adopt the new limit, which then takes effect on the first fishing day of the following month.

Policy: Each fisher may take up to 15 kg per trip, except the lead fisher who may take up to 20 kg per trip.

Operationalization: The first fisher to arrive at the dock that morning becomes the lead fisher for that day and may take up to 20 kg on each of his/her trips. All other fishers may take up to 15 kg per trip. Every fisher records arrival, departure, and return times and the exact catch weight on a shared arrival/departure logbook. Upon return the catch is weighed on the communal scale; the weight is entered into the communal ledger by the fisher and double‑checked by the lead fisher that day. The fishery committee meets nightly to review the ledger, flag any violations (exceeding the quota for that trip), and record a counter in the “Violations” column. A second violation by the same fisher triggers a one‑day suspension: the committee logs the suspension, marks the fisher as “Suspended” in the ledger, and denies the fisher a license to leave the dock the following day. The suspension flag is cleared if no further violations occur; repeated violations reset the counter and extend the suspension. All ledger entries, violation counters, and suspension status are visible to the community and updated daily to ensure transparency and enforce the rules.

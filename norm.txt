Policy: No fisher may take more than 10 % of the lake’s current biomass in a season, a reserve of at least 20 % of the reference stock must be kept, and reserves may only be drawn by an elected council of up to three fishers when end‑of‑season biomass falls below 30 % of that reference stock.

Operationalization: At the start of each season the community records the lake’s total biomass (reference stock) in the shared ledger. Before every trip a fisher reads the last posted biomass, computes 10 % of that value as the maximum catch for the trip, records the actual catch, and if the catch exceeds the 10 % quota returns the excess to the lake and records a fine of 0.5 kg per kg of excess, which is added to the reserve. After recording the net catch the fisher deposits 2 % of the net catch into the reserve account and updates the ledger’s biomass by subtracting the net catch. The ledger thus always shows the current biomass and reserve balance. At each monthly community meeting the ledger is reviewed by consensus; any unrecorded fines or excesses are confiscated, and if the reserve falls below 20 % of the reference stock the council may impose a temporary reduction of all individual quotas (e.g., an extra 5 %) until the reserve is restored. At the end of the season the council compares the final lake biomass to 30 % of the reference stock; if it is lower they may draw from the reserve up to the available amount, otherwise no draw is made. If the reserve is insufficient, the council must seek alternative measures such as further quota reductions or additional contributions to rebuild the reserve.

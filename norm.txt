Policy: Each fisher may keep up to 2 units of fish per day; any surplus must be deposited into the communal reserve in the iron barrel, sealed by the steward, with deposits and withdrawals signed by the cook and recorded on the shared ledger.

Operationalization: 1. After each fishing trip, the fisher logs the total catch on the shared ledger. He retains up to 2 units for personal use and deposits any surplus into the iron barrel reserve. The deposit entry is signed by the cook and recorded immediately, and the steward seals the barrel until the end of the day. 2. Once the fisher consumes fish, he records his daily consumption on the ledger; the cook signs to certify the amount. 3. If a fisher’s consumption falls below 1 unit, he submits a short‑fall request on the ledger. The steward compares it with the signed consumption, authorizes a withdrawal from the reserve up to the shortfall, debits the reserve balance, and logs the transaction. 4. Non‑fisher villagers (two in the community) may submit withdrawal requests in the same manner; each is entitled to withdraw up to 1 unit per day to meet the mandatory consumption, provided reserve funds are available. 5. All deposits, withdrawals, consumptions, and any violations are recorded on the shared ledger so every villager can view the reserve status. 6. If a fisher fails to deposit surplus fish, the steward confiscates the missing portion, deposits it into the reserve, and imposes a penalty of 0.2 units per un‑deposited unit. The violation is logged. 7. Each time the steward records a missing deposit, a violation counter increments. After three violations, the council of fishers votes—requiring a simple majority of members present—to suspend that fisher’s rights for two days or until all penalties are paid and compliance is demonstrated. 8. All violations, penalties, and suspensions are logged on the ledger for transparency. 9. The reserve is physically stored in a sturdy iron barrel on the lakebank, sealed when not in use; the steward opens it only for authorized deposits or withdrawals and updates the ledger immediately.

Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 70% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 120 kg; must keep 1 kg for sustenance, deposit 1 % of the catch (rounded to 0.1 kg) into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 30 kg, and the lake must never fall below 80 kg after any trip.

Operationalization: On every trip, a fisher records the lake stock before fishing, calculates the allowed harvest (70% of that stock, not exceeding 120 kg), fishes accordingly, keeps 1 kg, deposits 1 % of the catch rounded to 0.1 kg into the reserve; if the reserve is below 30 kg, the fisher must add the difference immediately. No fishing is permitted if the lake stock is below 80 kg or if the fisher cannot meet the 1 kg sustenance requirement. If a fisher fails to deposit the required amount, the missing amount is deducted from their next catch and the fisher must pay the community an equivalent value within two trips.

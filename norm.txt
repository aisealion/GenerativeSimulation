Policy: Each fisher may take no more than 20% of the lake’s current fish stock per trip; any excess must be returned to the lake or deposited in the communal bin, and the steward will adjust the violator’s next-trip quota by reducing it by 10% of the current fish stock.

Operationalization: 1. Before each trip, the fisher records the planned catch in the shared log.
2. After the trip, the fisher writes the actual catch in the log. If the catch exceeds 20% of the current lake stock, the fisher immediately returns the excess to the lake or places it in the communal bin.
3. The steward, selected by community vote and accountable to the group, maintains the shared log, updates the lake’s current stock by subtracting each fisher’s net catch (after returning excess), and records any fish returned to the lake.
4. Weekly community meetings review the log; the steward identifies any violations, informs the violator, and, with community input, recalculates the violator’s next-trip quota by subtracting 10% of the lake’s current fish stock.
5. Every week, each household submits a brief need report (household size, special circumstances, food shortfalls). These reports are compiled and stored in the log.
6. Excess fish accumulated in the communal bin is held until the end of the fishing season; the steward then distributes the bin’s contents to households in proportion to their reported needs, ensuring a minimum allocation for each household.
7. The steward’s decisions on quota adjustments and bin distribution are recorded in the log for transparency; any fisher or household dissatisfied with the steward’s actions can call a vote to replace the steward.
8. All fishers retain their own catch for personal use after complying with the 20% rule and returning excess; no fisher may keep more than their allowed quota for the trip.

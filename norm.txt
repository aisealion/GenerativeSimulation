Policy: Each fisher may take up to 0.10 kg per trip, deposit 95% of that catch into the communal reserve, and fishing is prohibited if lake biomass falls below 80 kg or the reserve falls below 120 kg.

Operationalization: Before each trip the fisher checks the lake biomass as reported by the community monitors. The fisher logs the catch and automatically deposits 95% into the communal reserve, which is tracked by a steward. If the lake biomass drops below 80 kg or the reserve falls below 120 kg, all fishing stops until the reserve is replenished. Anyone who fails to deposit the required portion or fishes when prohibited will be temporarily suspended from fishing until compliance is restored.

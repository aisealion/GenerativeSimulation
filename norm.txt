Policy: Each fisher may keep up to 15 kg of fish per trip; excess is deposited into the community reserve. Weighing occurs each morning at the dock with the weighmaster rotating weekly based on prior week’s catch, and a council elected by majority each season enforces bans and records all actions.

Operationalization: Morning gathering at the dock: the weighmaster places the net on the scale, reads the weight, writes it to the communal ledger, and the fisher signs to confirm. If the weight exceeds 15 kg, the weighmaster removes the excess and stores it in the reserve tank. The fisher keeps the 15 kg. All weights and actions are logged in the ledger, which is signed by the fisher and the council chair. The council is elected by simple majority of fishers present at the season’s first meeting; the chair, second, and treasurer are recorded in the ledger. Repeated violations are two or more; a fisher with two violations receives a one‑day ban, during which the weighmaster denies dock access and the council logs the ban. If the designated weighmaster is absent, the next in rotation steps in, or a quick vote decides that day.

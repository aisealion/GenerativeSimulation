Policy: Each fisher may take up to 1 kg per trip or 2 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller; the lake must retain at least 95 % of its biomass after each catch; the community’s monthly total harvest cannot exceed 3 % of the lake’s 12‑month average biomass.

Operationalization: Every fisher submits a catch log within 48 hours of a trip; community monitors compliance with random checks. If a fisher exceeds the per‑trip limit or the monthly cap, they forfeit fishing rights for one month and must offset the excess by reducing future catches. The lake’s biomass is measured monthly, and the 12‑month average is updated; all logs and biomass data are posted on the community board for transparency.

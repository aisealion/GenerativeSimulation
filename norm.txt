Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 1.0 kg per trip, not more than 5 % of the lake’s current biomass, and the lake must retain at least 70 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve after each catch.

Operationalization: At the start of each fishing round, all fishers report the lake’s current biomass to the community ledger. After each catch, a fisher records the weight of fish taken; if the catch exceeds the 1.0 kg or 5 % limit, or if the post‑catch total falls below 70 %, the excess is returned and the fisher pays a community fee and loses fishing privileges for one round. All records are reviewed monthly by the council to adjust the reserve if needed.

Policy: Each fisher may take at most 2 kg per trip; 35 % of each catch must be deposited into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 35 % of the lake’s current biomass. Fines equal to any missing deposit or excess catch are collected immediately by the treasurer, who also suspends any fisher who fails to comply until the fine is paid. Fishing is suspended if the reserve falls below the threshold until it is replenished through fines or voluntary contributions.

Operationalization: 1. After each trip the fisher logs the amount kept and the 35 % deposit. 2. The treasurer, elected by the fishing community each season, tallies all deposits, verifies no catch exceeds 2 kg, and records any fine equal to the missing deposit or excess catch, collecting it immediately and adding it to the communal reserve. 3. The treasurer computes the lake’s biomass by subtracting the total catch from the previous round’s biomass and adding any voluntary contributions; the communal reserve must remain at least 35 % of that biomass. 4. If the reserve dips below the threshold, the treasurer notifies the community, fishing is suspended, and the reserve must be replenished via fines or voluntary contributions before fishing can resume. 5. Suspended fishers may resume fishing only after their fine is paid and the treasurer confirms compliance.

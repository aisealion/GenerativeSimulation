Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 12 kg per trip, depositing 5 % of the catch into a communal reserve that must hold a minimum of 20 kg in the lake. If the lake’s total stock falls below 20 kg, the reserve minimum is waived until the stock recovers.

Operationalization: At the end of each trip, the fisher records the catch and immediately sets aside 5 % (rounded to the nearest 0.1 kg) for the communal reserve. The reserve is maintained in a shared tank that the community monitors. If the lake’s total stock after the catch is still above 20 kg, the deposit is required; if it drops below 20 kg, the deposit is suspended for that trip. A community council reviews the lake stock weekly; any fisher who fails to deposit when required must pay a fine equal to the missing deposit or forfeit their catch. If the lake stock remains below 20 kg for two consecutive weeks, the council can authorize a temporary reduction of the maximum harvest to 8 kg per trip until the reserve rebuilds to 20 kg.

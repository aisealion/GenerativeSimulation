Policy: Each fisher may harvest no more than 1.3 kg per trip or 6 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller; after every catch the lake must retain at least 80 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve.

Operationalization: Every trip a fisher records the catch weight in a shared ledger that updates the lake’s current biomass. Before a fisher can finish a trip the keeper checks that removing the recorded amount will leave at least 80 % of the lake’s total as the communal reserve; if not, the fisher must trim the catch to meet the reserve requirement. The keeper compiles a weekly report of total harvests per fisher and the lake’s remaining biomass. Any fisher who records a catch that would violate the reserve rule must return the excess weight and will receive a one‑day suspension for the next three fishing days. If the lake’s biomass falls below 100 kg, fishing is paused until a community restoration plan is approved.

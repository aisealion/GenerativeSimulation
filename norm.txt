Policy: Each fisher may take up to 25% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 8 kg, and may not cumulatively take more than 50% of the lake’s stock at the start of each calendar month.

Operationalization: Before each trip the fisher records the current lake stock from the community ledger and calculates 25 % of that amount. If the calculation exceeds 8 kg the fisher may only take 8 kg. After the trip the fisher submits a trip report to the Fish Council, which consists of the ten fisher representatives elected monthly plus a rotating steward. The council tracks monthly totals for each fisher and flags any who would exceed the 50 % cap. When a breach is detected, the council first requests voluntary return of the excess fish within three days. If returned, the fisher keeps the rest of their haul with no penalty. If not returned, the council calculates the exact excess, reviews the fisher’s compliance history and the size of the excess, and decides between: (1) forfeit the excess fish to the community pool, or (2) impose a fine equal to 5 % of the excess fish’s market value (or $1 per kg, whichever is higher). The threshold for forfeiture is an excess of 4 kg or more, or any fisher with more than one breach in the past three months; otherwise a fine is applied. Intent is judged by the fisher’s track‑record, evidence of deliberate rounding or deceit, and circumstances; accidental breaches result in a warning or a lighter fine. All decisions and penalties are recorded in the ledger and the fisher’s name flagged for the next monthly review.

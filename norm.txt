Policy: Each fisher may take up to 2 kg per trip, must keep at least 1 kg for themselves, and the community portion of the month’s harvest may not exceed 30 % of the lake’s stock at the start of the month, capped at 3 kg.
Legacy: round 9 policy capped each trip at 45 kg per trip.
Operationalization: For every trip the recorder records a personal quota of 1 kg and a community portion up to 1 kg. The recorder sums the community portions for the month and rejects any excess beyond the 30 % cap (or the 3 kg cap if the 30 % is larger). If the 30 % of the lake stock is less than 1 kg, the community portion of each trip is limited to that remaining amount; the fisher still keeps 1 kg personal. Any fisher who takes more than 2 kg in a single trip automatically has the excess added to the community pool and forfeits their personal quota. Violations of the trip limit or exceeding the monthly community cap trigger a one‑month fishing ban recorded in the ban ledger by the recorder. The recorder role rotates monthly in alphabetical order; the current recorder is Rina. All fishers must consult the ban ledger at the monthly opening meeting before any trip. The community pool is stored in the simulation’s state variable ‘community_pool’, updated each month by the recorder based on recorded excess catch.

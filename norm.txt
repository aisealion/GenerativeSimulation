Policy: Each fisher may keep at most one unit (≈1 kg) of fish per calendar day (sunrise to sunrise). Any catch beyond that must be deposited into the communal pool, and any withdrawal from the pool requires a matching deposit from the next catch before the next trip, or the fisher is temporarily banned until compliance.

Operationalization: 1. At the start of each fishing trip the fisher weighs the total catch. 2. The fisher records the cumulative amount of fish already kept that day. 3. If the sum of the current catch and the cumulative kept amount exceeds one unit, the excess is placed in the communal bucket and the amount is written on the communal board next to the fisher’s name. 4. The village elder, elected by majority each season, verifies all weights, confirms deposits, and updates the board. 5. If a fisher takes fish from the communal pool, they write the amount taken on the board and must deposit an equivalent amount from the next catch before the next trip begins; if they fail to do so, they are barred from fishing until the next sunrise. 6. After three consecutive failures the elder may assign a helper task (net repair, fish cleaning, drying, or communal chores); upon verified completion the elder records a fish allowance of one unit in the ledger, which counts toward the required replenishment. 7. The ban ends immediately once the fisher brings back the full amount to the communal bucket or finishes the helper task and restores the balance. 8. All fish in the communal pool are kept in a locked wooden crate on the dock; the bucket holds the current surplus and the elder uses a padlock to secure it. 9. The communal board and ledger are visible to all villagers; the elder is the sole verifier and can alter only the recorded amounts. 10. The next fishing trip is scheduled for the following sunrise, announced by the elder each evening and marked by a bell at 6:00 a.m. 11. A “day” is defined as the period from sunrise to the next sunrise; multiple trips in a single day count toward the one‑unit limit cumulatively.

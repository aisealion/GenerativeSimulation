Policy: Each fisher may take up to 12 kg per trip; any excess must be returned to the lake under a witnessed log approved by the council, and discrepancies trigger confiscation of the missing fish and a replacement fee, with random spot-checks by council members to enforce compliance.

Operationalization: 1. On each trip, the fisher records total catch weight at the dock and identifies any weight above 12 kg as excess. 2. The fisher selects a neutral community member to witness the hand-over of excess fish; the fisher and witness sign the communal log. 3. The fisher submits the log to the weekly council for approval; the council member who approved the witness also signs the log. 4. The council reviews the log each week against the scale reading taken at the weekly meeting. 5. If the logged returned weight does not equal the excess, the council confiscates the missing portion and levies a fee equal to the local replacement cost per kilogram, to be paid at the next meeting. 6. The council conducts monthly random spot-checks: a lottery draws inspectors who weigh the fisher’s catch on a council-chosen day to verify returned amounts. 7. If a fisher refuses to deliver the missing fish, the council confiscates 100% of the missing portion and records it in the communal inventory; the fisher must deliver the confiscated fish to the council or face a fine. 8. The council is composed of senior and trusted fishers elected by majority vote at the weekly meeting, serving one season; vacancies are filled by quick vote if a member misses half a season. 9. The fee and confiscated fish are stored in a refrigerated communal bin and may be used for community events or sold at market to recover costs.

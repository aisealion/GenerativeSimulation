Policy: Each fisher may take up to 25 kg per trip, provided the lake retains at least 10% of its current stock as a reserve; if the reserve falls below 10%, all quotas are reduced by 20% for the following round. Any over‑catch or reserve breach triggers a fine of 0.02 kg per kg over, paid to the community pool, with the fine amount withheld from the fisher’s pre‑paid deposit until settled, and a fisher who commits three or more violations within any 30‑day period is temporarily barred until all fines are cleared.

Operationalization: When a fisher logs a catch, the system: 1) calculates the post‑catch lake stock; 2) checks that the haul does not exceed 25 kg and that the post‑catch reserve remains at least 10% of the current stock; 3) if either condition is violated, computes the over‑catch amount, multiplies it by 0.02 kg to get the fine, and deducts that fine from the fisher’s pre‑paid deposit, holding it in the community pool until the fine is paid back; 4) records the violation, timestamp, and increments the fisher’s violation counter; 5) if the reserve fell below 10% in this round, the system flags the fisher’s quota for a 20% reduction in the next round; 6) after every round, the system reviews the lake stock and all logged violations; 7) if a fisher has three or more violations within any 30‑day window, the system marks them as temporarily barred until their fine balance is cleared; 8) fines are cleared when the fisher redeposits the required amount into the community pool; 9) all logs, fines, deposits, and violation counts are stored in a community ledger that is audited monthly at the community meeting.

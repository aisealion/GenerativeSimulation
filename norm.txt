Policy: Each fisher may take up to 15 kg per trip and 30 kg per calendar week; excess is returned to the communal reserve, a 5 kg penalty goes to the reserve, and a second violation within 30 days forfeits a free trip per week, with further violations adding additional forfeited trips.

Operationalization: • Before departing, the fisher records name, date, and expected take in the shared ledger. • At return, the fisher logs the actual catch. • If the catch exceeds 15 kg for the trip, the excess is physically returned to the communal holding area at the shore and recorded in the ledger; the same applies if the weekly total exceeds 30 kg. • A single failure to report or limit breach triggers an automatic 5 kg fish penalty that is added to the communal reserve and logged as a "penalty" entry. • The keeper, elected by simple majority for the calendar week (Monday 00:00–Sunday 23:59), enforces the rule and imposes the penalty. • A repeated violation is defined as two separate infractions within a 30‑day period; on the second violation the keeper imposes a forfeiture of one free trip for that fisher for the following week, recording a "forfeited trip" entry. • Each additional repeated violation within the same 30‑day window increases the forfeited trips by one for each subsequent week until the period ends and the count resets. • A free trip is a trip taken without paying the usual lake‑use fee; each fisher is entitled to one free trip per calendar week, forfeited as described. • Disputes over penalties or forfeitures are taken to the council (long‑time fishers plus the current keeper); the council meets on the first Sunday of the following month and votes by simple majority to resolve the case. • All ledger entries are maintained in a shared, tamper‑evident file accessible to all fishers, ensuring transparency and accountability.

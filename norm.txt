Policy: Each fisher may take up to 95 % of the lake’s current biomass on any trip. When the communal reserve falls below 5 kg, each fisher must set aside 2 % of their own catch into the reserve; if the reserve drops below 2 kg, all fishers agree to cap their take at 50 % on the next trip until the reserve reaches at least 5 kg.

Operationalization: After every trip, each fisher records their catch in the communal ledger and checks the current reserve level. If the reserve is under 5 kg, the fisher keeps 98 % of their catch and hands the 2 % required deposit—measured in kilograms of fish—to the reserve fund; the ledger is updated accordingly. If the reserve is under 2 kg, the Reserve Guardian announces the 50 % take cap for the next trip and all fishers comply. Any fisher who fails to deposit the required 2 % when the reserve is below 5 kg is fined in fish equal to the missing deposit; the ledger records the fine and the fisher’s compliance status. The Reserve Guardian, elected each season, monitors reserve levels daily, updates the ledger, enforces the caps, and declares the cap lifted once the reserve reaches at least 5 kg.

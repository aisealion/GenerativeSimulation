Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 4 kg per trip: 1 kg for sustenance, 3 kg to the community reserve; monthly catch cannot exceed 10 % of lake biomass, and any excess must be returned and double‑deposited as a penalty; the reserve is released only when lake biomass falls below 80 kg and after a 4‑month refill period, with allocation decided by a rotating committee of three monitors who first aid those with lowest balances, then proportionally share remaining reserves.

Operationalization: 1. I will keep the reserve bin and verify each 3 kg deposit, recording the amount, fisher name, trip date and ledger entry; I will also record any penalty deposits and the dates of releases. 2. After each trip, the communal scale will weigh the catch; the sustenance 1 kg will be deducted, and any remaining weight will be entered in the ledger as catch. 3. Monthly totals are summed per fisher; if a fisher’s total exceeds 10 % of the lake’s current biomass, the excess weight is returned to the lake immediately and the fisher deposits double that weight into the reserve bin, with the action recorded and verified by two monitors. 4. The lake biomass is measured each month and logged in the ledger. 5. When biomass drops below 80 kg and the 4‑month refill period (counted from the last reserve release date) is complete, a rotating committee of three impartial community monitors convenes at the weigh station. 6. The committee reviews the ledger for the period, calculates each fisher’s net balance (catch − sustenance − reserve deposits), identifies fishers with the lowest balances, and allocates reserve funds first to bring those balances to at least zero or a predefined sustainability threshold. 7. Any remaining reserve is divided proportionally among all fishers based on the amount each contributed to the reserve during the refill period. 8. All allocations, dates, and rationales are entered into the ledger; a summary is published to the community. 9. Fishers may submit a written request to the committee to review any allocation; the committee will re‑examine the ledger and make a final decision.

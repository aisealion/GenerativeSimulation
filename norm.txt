Policy: Each fisher may take up to 12% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 15 kg, and may not cumulatively take more than 30% of the lake’s stock in a calendar month.

Operationalization: Before each departure, the fisher who is going out must estimate the lake’s current stock using the agreed sampling net, record that estimate in the communal ledger, and verify the ledger entry with the community steward. The first fisher of each calendar month performs the same measurement immediately after midnight; that figure becomes the monthly reference stock for the 30 % cap. Each fisher records his haul weight at departure; the steward cross‑checks entries and maintains cumulative totals in the ledger. If any fisher exceeds the 30 % monthly cap, the steward requires the excess weight to be returned or a penalty paid before further fishing. The lake’s reserve of 10 kg is the minimum stock that must remain after all takes; if the ledger shows the stock falls below 10 kg, the first fisher to record the low stock flags it, and the community votes by simple majority at the monthly gathering to either pause fishing for the rest of the month or arrange a restocking event. All records and decisions are kept in the communal ledger, and the steward performs random spot‑checks to ensure accuracy.

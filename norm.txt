Policy: Each fisher may keep up to 12kg per trip; any excess goes to a communal pool.

Operationalization: On every trip each fisher reports their catch. If the catch is above 12kg, the excess must be deposited into the communal pool. If a fisher reports a catch below 1kg, they must supply 1kg from the pool. The community records net catch weekly; if a fisher repeatedly exceeds the limit or fails to meet the minimum, they lose a fishing slot for one week. All rules are reviewed monthly and may be amended by a community vote.

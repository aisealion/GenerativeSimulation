Policy: Each fisher may take up to 3 kg per trip, keep at least 1 kg for personal use, and the community’s total monthly catch may not exceed 50 % of the lake’s current stock at the start of the month, capped at 10 kg. Any overage must be returned to the communal fund before the next month’s fishing; failure to return will result in a temporary loss of fishing privilege.

Operationalization: 1. At the beginning of each month, all fishers gather to estimate the lake’s current stock by a quick sampling method; the result is recorded by the rotating log keeper in the shared ledger. 2. Each fisher records each trip’s total catch and the 1 kg set aside on a personal log; at month’s end the log keeper compiles these into the community ledger. 3. The ledger’s total community catch is compared to the lower of 50 % of the month‑start stock or 10 kg; if it exceeds that limit, the excess (overage) is computed. 4. The log keeper calculates each fisher’s share of the overage proportionally to their individual catch, and requires that share to be returned to the communal fund within the first two days of the next month. 5. If a fisher fails to return the required share, the log keeper records a debt and the fisher is subject to a community‑determined penalty, such as a temporary loss of fishing privilege or an equivalent donation of fish or labor. 6. All logs, ledger entries, overage calculations, and penalties are publicly posted so every fisher can verify accuracy.

Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 1.5 kg per trip or 3 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller; the lake must retain at least 93 % of its total biomass after each catch, and the community’s total monthly harvest may not exceed 4 % of the lake’s 12‑month average biomass.

Operationalization: Every fisher will record their catch on a shared ledger before departure and report it to the monitoring board each month. The board will calculate cumulative monthly harvest, compare it to the 4 % cap, and adjust individual quotas if necessary. If a fisher exceeds the per‑trip or per‑month limits, the excess is returned to the lake and the fisher receives a warning; repeated violations result in temporary suspension from fishing until the cumulative harvest is balanced.

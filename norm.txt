Policy: When the lake holds at least 12 kg and the communal reserve holds at least 12 kg, each fisher may take up to 1.0 kg per trip, keep it for sustenance, and deposit 0.5 kg into the reserve; if the lake falls to 8–12 kg, the limit is 0.8 kg with a 0.3 kg deposit; if 6–8 kg, the limit is 0.5 kg with a 0.2 kg deposit; fishing is suspended below 6 kg until both lake and reserve reach 12 kg.

Operationalization: Before each fishing round, the community leader announces the current lake and reserve levels. Each fisher records their catch on a shared ledger. At round end, the agreed deposit (0.5, 0.3, or 0.2 kg) is transferred to the communal reserve pot. If a fisher exceeds the limit, the excess fish is returned to the lake and that fisher forfeits the right to fish in the next round. The reserve is checked after each round; if it falls below 12 kg, no fishing is allowed until it is replenished to 12 kg.

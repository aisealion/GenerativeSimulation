Policy: Each fisher may take up to 4 kg per trip, keeping 1 kg for personal sustenance and depositing 3 kg into a communal reserve; the reserve is released only when lake biomass falls below 70 kg after a 6‑month replenishment period, and no fisher’s monthly total catch may exceed 8 % of the lake’s current biomass.

Operationalization: A communal steward elected by simple majority at the start of each calendar year oversees the reserve bin, trip passes, and enforcement. Every trip the fisher reports 1 kg personal catch and 3 kg deposit to the steward, who records the amounts in a shared ledger; the ledger also tracks monthly total harvest per fisher and the lake’s monthly biomass measured by a rotating monitoring group using calibrated float methods. The steward reviews the ledger each month, ensuring each fisher’s total does not exceed 8 % of the current biomass; any violation triggers a two‑month trip forfeiture, revoking the fisher’s pass and confiscating gear, with the violation logged in the ledger. The reserve is only opened when lake biomass drops below 70 kg and at least six months have passed since the last release; when opened, no single trip may draw more than 3 kg from the reserve until biomass rises above 70 kg again. All actions—election results, deposit records, quota calculations, forfeiture notices, and reserve releases—are recorded in the shared ledger and displayed on the community’s warning board to ensure transparency and enforceability.

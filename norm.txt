Policy: No fisher may keep more than 20 kg of fish per trip; any excess is added to a communal reserve that is spent each month on maintenance, fuel and shared meals, and if a fisher exceeds 20 kg the entire catch is forfeited to the reserve and the fisher is barred from fishing for one month.

Operationalization: 1. A rotating committee of all boat‑owning fishers serves one month per term in a predetermined rotation set by a yearly draw, and is approved by a majority vote at the monthly gathering. 2. Before each trip the community meets in the morning; the 20 kg limit is agreed by consensus or a simple majority vote if needed. 3. At the community weigh‑station, every fisher weighs their catch; the weight is recorded in a personal log and handed to the committee at the monthly meeting. 4. The committee cross‑checks the log against the weigh‑sheet, records the catch in a shared spreadsheet and a paper ledger, and updates the communal reserve balance. 5. If a fisher’s logged weight exceeds 20 kg, the excess is added to the reserve and the fisher keeps only 20 kg. 6. If a fisher fails to report or disputes the weight, the committee orders an on‑site weigh‑off; confirmation of >20 kg results in forfeiture of the entire catch to the reserve and a one‑month fishing ban; confirmation ≤20 kg clears the fisher. 7. Disputes are resolved by a majority vote of the committee; a tie defaults to the last verified weigh‑sheet. 8. The reserve is spent each month on shared tools, fuel and communal meals as proposed by the committee, voted on by all fishers, and any leftover funds roll over to the next month.

Policy: When the lake holds 12 kg or more, each fisher may take up to 1.0 kg per trip, keep that 1.0 kg for sustenance, and deposit 0.5 kg into the communal reserve; when the lake is 8–12 kg, the limit is 0.8 kg with a 0.3 kg deposit; when the lake is 6–8 kg, the limit is 0.5 kg with a 0.2 kg deposit; if the lake falls below 6 kg, fishing is suspended until the reserve reaches at least 12 kg.

Operationalization: At the start of each round the community steward announces the current lake weight and the applicable limit. Each fisher records his catch and immediately hands the designated deposit to the steward. The steward updates the reserve and the lake balance. If a fisher exceeds the limit or fails to deposit, the missing amount is automatically added to the reserve and that fisher is barred from fishing for the next two rounds, with a community meeting to confirm the penalty. The reserve is used only for community projects or to pay a small fee for those who cannot fish, not for personal consumption.

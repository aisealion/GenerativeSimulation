Policy: Each fisher may take no more than 12 kg per trip; excess goes to a communal pot, reviewed weekly and distributed by the elder with logbook verification.

Operationalization: 1) After each trip the fisher records total catch in the communal logbook; if over 12 kg, the excess is immediately noted and added to the communal pot. 2) Weekly at the village square the eldest fisher who holds the pot presents it; the designated elder scales it and compares the weight to the logbook total, adjusting for any discrepancy by requiring the violating fisher to add or remove the difference. 3) All fishers and elders then vote on how to split the pot into immediate community needs (fuel, net repair, food for the poor) and a reserve for emergencies or community projects; the agreed amounts are distributed, the pot is emptied, and a new pot is started at zero for the week. 4) A fisher who exceeds the 12 kg limit on three separate trips in a calendar month is marked a repeated violator; the total excess from that month is added to that fisher’s share of the next month’s pot when the monthly reset occurs. 5) At the start of each new month the pot is reset to zero, and any excess from repeat violators is immediately added to that month’s pot before the first weekly meeting.

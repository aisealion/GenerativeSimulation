Policy: Each fisher may take no more than 10 kg per trip, up to 3 trips per day, and must not exceed 25 % of the lake’s stock at the start of that day in total; any excess must be returned to the lake.

Operationalization: 1. At dawn, the community records the lake’s stock in the communal ledger, using the last evening’s figure or a quick surface measurement.
2. The 25 % daily limit is calculated from that figure.
3. Every fisher logs each trip and catch weight immediately after the trip in the shared ledger at the dock.
4. At sunset, the fisher presents the boat’s scale reading; the community verifies ledger entries against the reading. The community recorder (rotated daily) signs the ledger and any discrepancy is resolved on the spot.
5. The community clerk updates the lake’s stock in the ledger after sunset, reflecting net catch after any return of excess.
6. If a fisher’s catch exceeds any of the limits, a quick vote is held; a simple majority of those present imposes a 48‑hour ban and requires the return of the excess.

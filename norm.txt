Policy: Each fisher may take a maximum of 10 kg per trip, and must set aside 2 kg of that catch as a community reserve; if a fisher fails to deposit the reserve the next trip’s allowable catch is limited to 8 kg for one round.

Operationalization: 1. **Reserve & Ledger** – After each trip, a fisher records the total catch weight and the 2 kg reserve deposited on the shared ledger before leaving the dock, signs it, and posts it immediately. The reserve is placed in the communal bin, and the bin’s scale is checked to confirm the 2 kg weight. 2. **Bin Guard Assignment** – The bin guard for each trip is the fisher whose name appears next alphabetically in the community roster; the list cycles so every fisher takes the duty equally. 3. **Verification & Penalty** – When a fisher leaves, the bin guard verifies that the ledger entry matches the bin’s weight. If a mismatch or missing reserve is detected, the guard calls a quick vote among all present fishers. A simple majority (≥3 of 5) votes to enforce a penalty. 4. **Enforcement of 8 kg Limit** – The penalty is applied to the offending fisher’s next trip: the bin guard on that trip checks the ledger and catch weight; if the fisher attempts to take more than 8 kg, the guard refuses departure until the reduced allowance is respected. 5. **Rotation Fairness** – Because the guard rotates alphabetically, each fisher performs the guard duty an equal number of times over time, ensuring shared responsibility and transparency.

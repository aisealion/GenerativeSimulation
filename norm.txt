Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 90% of the lake’s current stock per trip, keep the entire catch, deposit 0.5 kg into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 5 kg, and the lake must never fall below 15 kg after any trip.

Operationalization: On each trip a fisher first counts the lake’s current stock and may take any amount ≤90% of that, keeping at least 1 kg for personal sustenance. After catching, they deposit 0.5 kg to the communal reserve; if the reserve falls below 5 kg, the fisher deposits an extra 0.5 kg on each following trip until the reserve is ≥5 kg. The lake’s remaining stock after deposit cannot drop below 15 kg; if a fisher’s catch would bring it below, they leave the catch untouched and deposit an extra 1 kg per trip until the lake is back to 15 kg. A fisher who fails to deposit the required amounts must skip one trip and will forfeit 10% of their next catch to the reserve.

Policy: All fishers keep their haul but must contribute 5% of each catch, rounded to the nearest 0.5 kg, to a communal reserve for lake upkeep; the fishing council verifies contributions daily and may revoke a fisher’s right for up to 14 days if rules are broken.

Operationalization: Each trip: weigh the haul, calculate 5% of the total weight, round to the nearest 0.5 kg, set aside that amount, hand it to the communal pot, and the pot’s keeper (rotating among fishers) records the contribution. After all boats return, the council tallies the pot, compares the total to the expected 5% of all catches, discusses any shortfall, and instructs the shortfall fisher to make an extra catch the next day or, if repeated, revokes permission for 7 days (14 days if repeated) with a fine equal to one day’s share of the pot and one hour of communal service before rights are reinstated.

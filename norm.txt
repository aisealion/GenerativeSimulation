Policy: Each fisher may harvest at most 1.2 kg per trip or 4 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller; after each catch the lake must retain at least 90 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve.

Operationalization: Before a trip each fisher announces the intended catch and confirms it is below the 1.2 kg/4 % limit. After catching, the fish removed are recorded and the remaining biomass is verified by the rotating steward. If a fisher exceeds the limit or the lake falls below 90 % reserve, the fisher is temporarily banned for three trips and must return the excess fish to the lake. Violations are resolved during the monthly communal assessment day where all catches are inspected.

Policy: Each fisher may take up to 2 kg per trip, must keep at least 1 kg for personal sustenance, and deposits the remaining 1 kg into a communal reserve; if the lake stock falls below 10 kg, fishing is suspended until the reserve reaches at least 15 kg.

Operationalization: Fisheries keep a ledger of each trip’s catch, confirming 1 kg kept and 1 kg deposited. Every month the community meets to review lake levels and reserve status; if a fisher fails to keep 1 kg, they are required to catch an extra kg in the next trip or pay a small fee that goes to the reserve. Fishing is halted for all if the lake falls below 10 kg, and resumes only when the reserve is at or above 15 kg.

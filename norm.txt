Policy: Each fisher may take up to 5 % of the lake’s current stock per trip, with a minimum of 1 kg if that 5 % is at least 1 kg; the community’s total daily catch may not exceed 30 % of the lake’s stock; any catch above the 5 % limit is returned immediately and incurs a 10 % penalty on the trimmed excess, and repeat violators are barred for three days.

Operationalization: 1. Council composition: elected annually by all fishers with at least one season of experience; includes a clerk chosen by majority vote.
2. Ledger: holds lake’s stock and daily net catches; updated after each trip.
3. Trip logging: fisher submits written/e‑log of total catch immediately upon return.
4. Clerk verification: calculates 5 % of the current stock at that moment; if reported catch >5 %, trims to 5 %, records the trimmed amount, returns it to the lake, and applies a 10 % penalty on the trimmed excess (returned immediately as fine).
5. Minimum catch: clerk checks net catch; if ≥1 kg it is accepted; if <1 kg and 5 %<1 kg, the log is rejected and the fisher must re‑fish, use reserves, or forfeit the trip.
6. Stock update: after each accepted trip, ledger subtracts the net catch and adds any returned fish (trimmed amount + penalty).
7. End‑of‑day review: council totals all accepted net catches; if total >30 % of the stock at that time, the surplus is subtracted from the lake and divided evenly among all fishers (credited to their individual balances), then the ledger is updated.
8. Repeat offenders: if a fisher exceeds the 5 % quota in two separate trips, the council bars the fisher for three days; the ban lifts after the fisher shows no infractions for two consecutive days following the ban and submits a written statement of compliance.
9. Reserves: each fisher may draw from personal reserves if a trip fails; reserves are tracked separately and replenished by future accepted catches.
10. Enforcement: council actions are recorded in the ledger; any penalties or bans are logged with dates and reasons; disputes are resolved at the next community gathering.

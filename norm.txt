Policy: Each fisher may take a maximum of 8 kg per trip. All catch weights must be recorded on a shared community ledger before leaving; excess over 8 kg must be returned to the lake or donated to the community fund within 48 hours, after which rights are reinstated.

Operationalization: 1. Before departing, the fisher records the total catch weight on the shared ledger. 2. If the recorded weight exceeds 8 kg, the fisher calculates the excess. 3. The fisher creates an "Excess Return" ledger entry listing name, excess weight, date, destination (lake or community fund) and signature fields. 4. The fisher physically returns the excess fish to the lake or hands it to the Treasurer of the Community Fund in front of a witness; the witness signs or applies a QR‑stamp to the ledger. 5. The fisher signs the return line. 6. The ledger entry is submitted to the monthly Community Fishing Committee within 48 hours. 7. During the monthly meeting the Committee verifies the signatures, checks that the timestamp is within the 48‑hour window and confirms the return. 8. If all checks pass, the Committee records a "Reinstatement" entry and the fisher’s rights are restored. 9. All decisions, suspensions, and reinstatements are recorded in the ledger and in the meeting minutes, which are posted beside the ledger. 10. If a fisher fails to return excess within 48 hours, the Committee creates a "Suspension" ledger entry with fisher name, start date, violation description, and signatures of the chair and secretary. 11. The suspension remains in effect until the excess is returned and the Committee signs the reinstatement. 12. The Treasurer of the Community Fund is elected by the Committee, receives all excess fish, records receipt in the ledger, and signs the entry. 13. The Community Fishing Committee has the authority to review the ledger, enforce the 48‑hour return rule, suspend and lift rights, and their decisions are binding under the community charter.

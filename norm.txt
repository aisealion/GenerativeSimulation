Policy: Each fisher may take at most 1.5 kg per trip, must keep 1 kg for personal sustenance, depositing any extra into a communal reserve; if the lake stock falls below 8 kg, fishing is suspended until the reserve reaches at least 12 kg.

Operationalization: On every trip, a fisher counts her catch. If the lake stock is ≥8 kg she may keep up to 1.5 kg, with 1 kg kept for herself; the surplus (≤0.5 kg) is placed in the communal reserve by depositing it at the community pit. The reserve level is recorded in a shared ledger after each trip. If the lake stock drops below 8 kg, all fishers must cease fishing. They continue to replenish the reserve, and once it reaches 12 kg the fishing may resume. Any fisher who fishes while the lake is below 8 kg forfeits her catch and must repay 0.5 kg of reserve.

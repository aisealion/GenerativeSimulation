Policy: Each fisher may take no more than 5 kg per trip, must keep a mandatory 1 kg reserve, records the catch on the communal ledger, and must obey monthly stock measurements that trigger a one‑week suspension if below 200 kg.

Operationalization: 1. Upon return, the fisher weighs the haul on the communal balance, noting total catch weight and the reserve (≥1 kg). 2. The fisher writes the total weight, reserve, name, and date on the communal ledger, signs it, and the clerk posts it in the common area. 3. A rotating group of community members conducts random checks, comparing the fish on hand to the ledger entry and reserve. 4. The monthly stock is measured by a random net haul at a fixed spot; the council averages weight per fish, extrapolates to the whole lake, and if the estimate is <200 kg the council announces a one‑week suspension effective the next day. 5. If a fisher reports >5 kg or a reserve <1 kg, the fisher must return 2 kg to the communal barrel, note the transfer on the ledger, and receive a gentle reminder. 6. Repeated violations (the second or subsequent) result in a one‑week ban imposed by the council; the clerk records the ban in the ledger, issues a written notice, holds the dock and boat key, and the rotating watch prevents the banned fisher from launching the boat during the ban period.

Policy: Each fisher may harvest no more than 5% of the lake’s current biomass or 1 kg (whichever is greater), capped at 3 kg per trip, while the lake must always retain at least 30 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve.

Operationalization: Before each trip the community records the lake’s current biomass, calculates the allowed harvest for each fisher, and logs each catch.  After all trips the total harvest is subtracted from the biomass; if the remaining reserve falls below 30 % the next cycle’s harvest limits are tightened until the reserve is restored.  Fisher who exceed their limit must release excess catch and will lose fishing privilege for one cycle.  A monthly audit by two impartial community members ensures compliance and updates the biomass record.

Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 1.2 kg per trip or no more than 9 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller; after every catch the lake must retain at least 68 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve.

Operationalization: Before fishing, a fisher records intended catch weight in a shared log. After the trip, they report actual weight. If the catch exceeds the smaller of 1.2 kg or 9 % of the lake’s biomass, the excess is returned to the reserve and logged. If a fisher repeatedly exceeds limits, their fishing right is suspended for two rounds. If the reserve falls below 68 %, the community holds an emergency meeting to adjust limits or replenish the reserve.

Policy: Each fisher may keep one unit (≈1 kg) of fish per day for personal use; any surplus must be deposited into the communal pool. When a fisher withdraws from the pool, they must replenish the same amount from their next catch, or face a temporary ban until they do.

Operationalization: 1. At each fishing trip the fisher counts total catch. 2. If catch exceeds 1 unit, the excess is placed in the communal container and the total amount (personal + deposited) is recorded on the communal board. 3. The village elder verifies all counts, ensures surplus deposits, and records them. 4. If a fisher takes fish from the communal pool, they write the amount taken on the board and must deposit an equivalent amount from the next catch before the next trip starts. 5. If the fisher cannot replenish immediately, they are barred from fishing until the next trip and must bring the surplus; after three consecutive failures the elder may assign helper tasks (net repair, fish cleaning, drying, or communal chores) that earn a fish allowance to help refill the pool. 6. The temporary ban lasts until the fisher brings enough surplus to restore the amount taken or completes the helper role; the ban is lifted immediately once the pool is replenished. 7. All actions are visible to all villagers and the elder is the sole verifier of compliance.

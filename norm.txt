Policy: Each fisher may harvest no more than 1.5 kg per trip or 3 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller; after every catch the lake must retain at least 92 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve.

Operationalization: Before each trip the fisher calculates 3 % of the current lake weight and compares it to 1.5 kg; the lower value is the personal limit. The fisher records the catch on a communal ledger that is updated after every trip. At the end of each season the community verifies that the cumulative catch has left the lake with at least 92 % of its biomass; any fisher who exceeds the limit must restock the equivalent weight by the next trip or provide a community‑approved penalty (e.g., a shared restocking effort).

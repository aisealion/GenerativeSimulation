Policy: Each villager may take up to 0.5 kg of fish per person per day (their quota). Any catch beyond that must be deposited in the communal surplus pool for redistribution according to the rules below.

Operationalization: 1. Family size is the number of registered household members (spouse, children, dependents). 2. Quota per villager = 0.5 kg × household size. 3. After each trip, the fisher weighs the catch, records the total weight on the communal ledger by the dock, signs it, and leaves it with the village head. 4. The Lake Watch, a rotating group of three active fishers selected by a weekly lottery, checks the marked surplus area each evening to verify compliance. 5. The village head reviews the ledger daily and performs spot‑checks. 6. Any weight above quota is added to the communal surplus pool, kept in a marked landing pit managed by the Fish Steward. 7. The Fish Steward is appointed by the village head with community input and can be changed at the annual council meeting. 8. Villagers with a net shortfall (quota minus weight) are identified as lower catchers. 9. The surplus pool is first allocated to the lowest shortfalls, giving each enough to reach quota. 10. Remaining surplus is divided equally among the remaining lower catchers. 11. Villagers who met or exceeded quota receive nothing from the pool. 12. If a fisher misreports or is caught taking more than quota, the excess fish is returned to the pool and the fisher may face community sanctions.

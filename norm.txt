Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 15 kg per trip; 5% of each catch is deposited into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 20 kg when the lake’s stock is above 20 kg, and no deposit is required when the lake’s stock falls below 20 kg.

Operationalization: Before setting out, each fisher records the amount caught and immediately transfers 5% of that amount into the reserve. The reserve balance is tracked by a designated keeper; if at any time the reserve dips below the 20‑kg minimum while the lake stock is above 20 kg, the owner of the shortfall must forfeit an equal amount of future catch until the reserve is restored. Non‑compliance with deposit or forfeit leads to a community‑enforced penalty of a temporary ban on fishing for one round. The reserve is replenished by any surplus fish when the lake stock rises above 20 kg, ensuring a self‑sustaining cycle.

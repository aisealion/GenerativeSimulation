Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 10% of the lake’s current biomass per trip, capped at 1 kg, and the lake must retain at least 50% of its total biomass as a communal reserve.

Operationalization: At the start of each round the community records the lake’s biomass on the shared ledger. Every fisher reports their catch after the trip, and the reserve is calculated as total biomass minus the sum of all catches. If a fisher’s catch would cause the reserve to fall below 50%, that fisher must immediately reduce the catch or pay a fee equal to the shortfall; the next round their quota is automatically lowered by 1% of current biomass. All logs are kept on a communal board and any violation (failure to report or exceeding the quota) results in the forfeiture of that fisher’s next trip’s quota.

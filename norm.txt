Policy: Each fisher may take no more than 12 kg per trip, with 5 % of their catch deposited into a communal reserve that must always total at least 20 kg in the lake.

Operationalization: At the start of each trip the fisher sets aside a 5 % deposit box. After the catch the fisher calculates 5 % of the catch weight, physically places it into the communal bin in the common area, and records the amount. The bin’s weight is tracked daily by community stewards. If the reserve ever dips below 20 kg, the next fisher is required to wait until the reserve is replenished by deposits or community contributions before fishing. A fisher who fails to deposit the required amount is penalized by forfeiting 10 % of their catch for the next three trips, and the penalty is recorded in the steward’s ledger.

Policy: Each fisher may take no more than 10 kg per trip; excess fish must be returned to the lake or donated to the communal pot. Repeat violations within a 30‑day window incur a one‑trip ban recorded by verifiers, enforced by verifiers and the elder.

Operationalization: 1. Every fishing day, each fisher departs the dock, returns with a haul, and immediately presents the catch to the rotating verifier pair. 2. Verifiers weigh the catch; if it exceeds 10 kg, they record the excess and, with the fisher’s consent, either return the excess to the lake or place it in the communal food pot for the next round. 3. The verifier pair is chosen each week by a community lottery that draws two names from a communal hat, preferring one from a different family than the other to reduce bias. 4. After weighing, the pair logs the trip, any excess, and any violation in the shared logbook and hands it to the elder. 5. The elder reviews the logbook each evening, writes any new bans on the communal board, and gives a written notice to the banned fisher at the next gathering. 6. A repeat violation is defined as a second over‑10 kg catch occurring within a continuous 30‑day period that starts on the date of the first violation. 7. A one‑trip ban is enforced by requiring the banned fisher to surrender gear and present a ban card before attempting to fish; if they try to set out during the week the ban is announced, verifiers or the elder will stop them until the ban trip is completed. 8. The ban ends when the fisher returns to the dock after completing the single, banned trip and the verifier records their return; they are then free to fish again. 9. All fishers agree to abide by this policy for the next two months, after which the community will re‑evaluate and vote to keep or adjust the rules.

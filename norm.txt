Policy: No fisher may take more than 5 kg per trip; any excess must be returned to the lake and the fisher is prohibited from fishing the following day, with failure to return incurring additional prohibition days and a fine equal to the excess weight at a rate of 10 communal credits per kg, as decided by the community council.

Operationalization: After each trip every fisher records total catch on the communal ledger and signs it; if catch exceeds 5 kg, the fisher deposits the excess into the designated lake zone. The lake steward checks the lake zone each morning, marks the ledger entry with ‘excess returned’ and sets the fisher’s prohibition status for the next day. If the excess is not found, the steward notifies the council, which votes on the penalty (additional prohibition days and fine), records it on the ledger, and the fisher pays the fine in credits the same day to the council’s communal‑fund account. All penalties and payments are logged in the ledger, the council manages the communal fund, and all community members respect the steward’s and council’s decisions. This procedure ensures transparency, fairness, and community accountability while allowing fishers to sustain themselves.

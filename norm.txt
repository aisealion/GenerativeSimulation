Policy: Each fisher may take no more than three units per trip and must leave at least one unit in the lake; any excess is returned immediately.

Operationalization: After each trip, the fisher records units caught in a personal ledger and reports to the observer (the village watchman) before sunset. The observer tallies all catches in the communal ledger, and the lake keeper with the council counts remaining fish using a calibrated net. If a fisher exceeds the 3‑unit limit or the lake drops below one unit, the excess fish is returned to the lake, and the fisher is flagged. Two consecutive violations or three separate violations in a month trigger a skip‑day imposed by a majority vote of the five elders (at least three votes). Repeated violations incur a fine set annually by the Community Council, deducted from the fisher’s next communal contribution. All records are kept in the communal ledger, reviewed nightly, and the policy is enforced by the lake keeper and council.

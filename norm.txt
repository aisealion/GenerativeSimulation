Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 1.2 kg per trip or 5 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller, and after every catch the lake must retain at least 85 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve.

Operationalization: On each trip, a fisher records the weight of fish taken in a shared ledger. The catch is capped by the rule: if 1.2 kg would exceed 5 % of the current lake biomass, the limit is the lower of the two. After the catch, the remaining lake biomass is verified by a community volunteer; if the residual is below 85 % of the lake’s prior total, the community meets within 7 days to adjust the reserve percentage or to impose a temporary fishing moratorium. If a fisher exceeds the allowed catch, they must immediately return the excess to the lake or donate it to the communal reserve, and the over‑catch is recorded as a penalty in the ledger. All records are kept in a transparent, accessible log on the community hub.

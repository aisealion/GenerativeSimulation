Policy: Each fisher may keep all of their catch, but must donate 1 kg per trip to a communal reserve; individual catch quotas may be adjusted monthly based on lake stock using the agreed linear rule (8 kg if <100 kg, 12 kg if >150 kg, linear scaling otherwise).

Operationalization: After each trip, the fisher deposits 1 kg of fish into the communal bucket/cooler on the dock and signs the ledger with weight and time. A rotating keeper/treasurer—selected weekly by draw or majority vote—maintains the container, verifies the ledger, empties the bucket weekly into communal storage (racks or shared net), and records all donations. Failure to donate triggers a forfeited next scheduled trip and a penalty of 1 kg, logged by the keeper and added to the communal reserve. The fishing schedule is posted each month by Solo in coordination with the treasurer; any quota adjustments are decided by simple majority at the monthly meeting and applied to the next cycle.

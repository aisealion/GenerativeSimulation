Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 12 kg per trip, with 5 % of the catch deposited into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 20 kg in the lake.

Operationalization: On each fishing day, a fisher records the total catch on a shared ledger. They then transfer 5 % of that catch to a communal tank; if the tank falls below 20 kg after a day’s haul, the extra 5 % from that fisher’s catch that day is added to make up the shortfall. If a fisher fails to deposit the required amount, they lose the right to fish for the next two days and must pay a small fee to the community fund.

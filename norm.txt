Policy: Each fisher may keep at most two units (0.5 kg each) per day; any catch beyond that must be released and any fisher exceeding the two‑unit limit more than twice in a calendar month must give an extra unit to the community pool.

Operationalization: 1. Daily Catch Log – A steward within the council keeps the communal log. Each fisher records the weight of their haul (in 0.5 kg units) and species (if known) in a personal logbook at the end of each trip. The logbook entry includes date, total weight, and species. 2. Net Capacity – A net is considered full when it holds two units (1 kg total). When the net is full, any further fish caught must be released back into the lake. 3. Measurement – A unit equals 0.5 kg. Every fisher carries a calibrated hand‑held kitchen scale to weigh their catch. The council maintains a central, verified scale at the weigh‑station. 4. Verification – Randomly, the council will re‑weigh a fisher’s haul at the weigh‑station and compare it to the fisher’s logbook weight. If the difference exceeds 5 %, the fisher is notified of a discrepancy. 5. Penalty for Discrepancy – A deviation over 5 % triggers a review; the fisher must give an extra unit to the community pool as penalty. 6. Monthly Violation Count – The month is the ordinary calendar month. Each fisher notes their own violations, but the council resets all monthly violation counts on the first day of each new month. 7. Enforcement – A fisher who exceeds the two‑unit limit more than twice in a month must immediately give an extra unit to the community pool. 8. Community Steward – The steward is responsible for reviewing log entries, monitoring net fullness, enforcing release of excess fish, and maintaining the central weigh‑station.

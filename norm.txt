Policy: Each fisher may fish no more than 4 h per day and may carry no more than 3 kg per haul; any excess fish is deposited in a communal pit and all compliant fish is kept by the fisher. The daily watchman verifies departure/return logs and scale readings, imposes a 5‑minute community‑work penalty for non‑compliance, and if the work is not completed an additional 10‑minute penalty or a 24‑hour temporary fishing ban is applied; the village leader with the fisher council decides how pit fish are used.

Operationalization: Before a fisher leaves the dock, the watchman checks the fisher’s recorded hours and net weight on the communal scale; if hours >4 or weight >3 kg, the watchman records the excess, orders the fisher to remove the excess fish to the communal pit, and assigns 5 minutes of net cleaning or fish washing. The fisher must complete the work within the time limit; if not, the watchman records a 10‑minute penalty or imposes a 24‑hour ban, locks the fisher’s boat, logs the ban, and notifies the village leader and council. All logs, scale readings, pit deposits, and penalties are recorded in the communal ledger and verified daily by the watchman.

Policy: No fisher may take more than 15 kg per trip, and a standing reserve of 70 kg must be maintained in the lake. Violations trigger a one‑month fishing revocation and a required 5 kg contribution to the reserve, with permission restored only after the contribution is recorded and a council meeting confirms compliance.

Operationalization: 1. Each trip is any departure from the dock to the lake and back. The fisher measures the catch immediately with the boat’s scale and records the weight on the shared board. If the weight exceeds 15 kg, the fisher marks the excess as ‘returned to reserve’ and the reserve total is increased accordingly.\n2. The Lake Watcher records the lake’s total biomass each month on the board; all fishers sign off.\n3. The Community Council meets monthly to review the board. If a fisher exceeds 15 kg on three consecutive trips or any three trips within a month, the Council revokes fishing permission for one month. During that month the fisher must pay a 5 kg contribution to the reserve, writing the payment on the board before the end‑of‑month council meeting.\n4. After the revocation month, the fisher must attend a council meeting to acknowledge the return of excess catch and the 5 kg payment. If the fisher attends, the Council verifies the records and reinstates permission; if not, the fisher remains revoked until the meeting is attended, with the option of an extended revocation and a second 5 kg contribution.\n5. The reserve total is tracked on the shared board; any excess catch returned or 5 kg payments are added to the reserve. If the reserve falls below 70 kg, the Council may (a) require all fishers to contribute proportionally to restore 70 kg, (b) temporarily lower the individual catch limit, (c) increase the penalty for repeat violators, (d) request earlier hatchery production, or (e) adjust the reserve target. The Council’s decision is posted on the board.\n6. All fishers are notified of any changes or corrective measures by board postings and hand‑to‑hand notices. The Council records all compliance and enforcement actions on the board and keeps a calendar of meetings, sending reminders to all fishers.

Policy: Each fisher may take up to 20 kg per trip, with a mandatory reserve equal to 12 % of the lake’s current stock; if the reserve falls below that threshold, all fishers’ quotas are reduced by 20 % for the next round.

Operationalization: The Lake Keeper is elected by a simple majority vote at the community meeting before each round and serves only for that round. At the start of the round the Keeper measures the lake’s stock with a calibrated scale, announces the current reserve (12 % of the stock) and the maximum catch (20 kg). During the round the Keeper monitors every trip, refuses any that would exceed the 20 kg limit or drop the reserve below 12 %, and collects a 0.03 kg deposit fine for each violation, which is paid into the communal lake‑maintenance pot. If the reserve falls below 12 %, the Keeper immediately announces a 20 % reduction of the maximum catch for all fishers (e.g., 20 kg becomes 16 kg) for the next round, publishes the new limits at the next community meeting, and enforces them by refusing trips that exceed the reduced quota.

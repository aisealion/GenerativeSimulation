Policy: Each fisher may take up to 18% of the lake’s current biomass per trip and must set aside 5% of their catch for the communal reserve; exceeding this limit triggers a 2 kg forfeiture on the next trip and a temporary quota reduction to 16.2% of lake biomass until the next community meeting restores the 18% limit.

Operationalization: 1. Before each trip, the fisher performing the lake survey reads the latest lake biomass from the communal notice board and calculates their maximum allowed take as 18% of that figure. 2. After fishing, the fisher calculates 5% of the actual catch to contribute to the communal reserve. 3. The fisher records the trip’s total catch, the reserved amount, and the calculated quota on the shared ledger by the day's end. 4. If the ledger shows a catch exceeding the 18% quota, the fisher must: a) on their next trip forfeit 2 kg of catch to the communal reserve, and b) have their new quota automatically set to 16.2% (10 % of the normal 18 % limit) of the lake’s current biomass for the duration of the next trip. 5. The fisher council sets and publishes the schedule of community meetings on the notice board and ledger; penalties end automatically at the next scheduled meeting, restoring the fisher’s quota to 18%. 6. The fisher who conducts the lake survey maintains the shared ledger and audits it to ensure compliance; any discrepancies are reported at the next community meeting for resolution.

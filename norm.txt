Policy: Each fisher may take up to 10 kg of fish per trip and no more than 3 trips per day; infractions (exceeding either limit) accrue, with two infractions issuing a warning and a third triggering a one‑week loss of fishing rights decided by a simple majority vote of present fishers, all recorded by the elected community leader.

Operationalization: The elected leader (chosen by majority vote for one month) keeps a daily log listing every trip, the weight of each haul (weighing the catch, releasing any excess above 10 kg), and records infractions in a cumulative table that tracks each fisher’s total infractions across days. When a fisher exceeds 10 kg, the excess is released and the fisher receives an infraction logged in the table. If a fisher makes more than three trips in a day, each extra trip is logged as an infraction. Upon a fisher’s second infraction, the leader issues a formal warning and updates the log; upon the third infraction, the leader announces the revocation of rights for one week, which the community then votes on, requiring a simple majority of fishers present to confirm. All actions, logs, warnings, and vote outcomes are documented in the daily log for transparency.

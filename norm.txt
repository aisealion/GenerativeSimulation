Policy: Each fisher may take up to 5% of the lake’s measured biomass per month, capped at 8 kg per trip; the community total may not exceed 12% of biomass per month, capped at 90 kg, with quotas set each quarter by a Fishery Committee based on biomass surveys.

Operationalization: A Fishery Committee elected by the community will conduct a biomass survey every quarter. From the survey they compute each fisher’s monthly quota (5% of the latest biomass, capped at 8 kg) and the community cap (12% of biomass, capped at 90 kg). Each fisher logs their catch weight in a shared ledger each month. If a fisher exceeds their quota, the committee issues a warning; a second violation within the same year results in a temporary suspension of fishing rights until the next quarterly survey. The lake will reserve a 10% area as a non‑fishing sanctuary during spawning season (May‑June) to protect breeding stock.

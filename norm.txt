Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 1.4 kg per trip or no more than 7 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller; after every catch the lake must retain at least 80 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve.

Operationalization: At the start of each round, the community records the lake’s current biomass on a shared ledger. Each fisher logs the exact weight of fish taken. If a fisher’s catch would bring the lake below the 80 % reserve threshold, they must reduce or return the excess to the lake. All fishers verify the ledger before the next round; any discrepancy is discussed and resolved by majority vote. Persistent violations result in a temporary fishing ban for the offending fisher. The rule is applied each round and is reviewed annually by the community council.

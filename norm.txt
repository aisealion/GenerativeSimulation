Policy: Each fisher may take no more than 5% of the lake’s current fish stock per trip, with at least one day between trips, and must log catches immediately after each trip.

Operationalization: After each trip, a fisher logs his catch immediately on the community ledger and hands the copy to the Ledger Keeper. The Ledger Keeper consolidates all logs within 24 hours and uploads the daily summary to the main ledger. Every Sunday evening, after the week’s fishing has ended, the community elders review the ledger, enforce compliance, and issue penalties. The rules are: (1) no fisher may take more than 5 % of the lake’s stock per trip; (2) a fisher must wait at least one day between trips; (3) any excess catch beyond 5 % is confiscated, the fisher must return the excess, and the fisher faces a 2‑day fishing ban and a fine of 10 % of the excess; (4) if a fisher fails to log a trip, the elders assume the maximum allowed 5 % catch, require the fisher to return that amount or pay a fine equal to 10 % of the assumed catch, require a full statement within 48 hours, impose an additional 5 % fine if the statement is not submitted, and temporarily bar the fisher from fishing until compliance; (5) fines collected by the Ledger Keeper are deposited into the community fund, which the elders manage for lake maintenance and gear; (6) the Ledger Keeper is elected by a simple majority vote each week, typically an experienced or neutral fisher, and is responsible for compiling the ledger and collecting fines; (7) the Ledger Keeper also records violations and notifies the elders; (8) all fishers agree to these rules and will abide by the elders’ decisions.

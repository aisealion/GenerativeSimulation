Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 12 kg per trip, deposit 5 % of the catch into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 25 kg in the lake.

Operationalization: Every fisher records the weight of their catch on a community log at the end of each trip. 5 % of that weight is immediately set aside in a designated communal reserve tank.  The reserve is monitored daily; if its level falls below 25 kg, the last fisher who caused the shortfall must pay a fine equal to the missing kg and perform a supplemental catch to bring the reserve back up to 25 kg.  Any fisher who attempts to take more than 12 kg is turned away and must forfeit the excess catch.  If a fisher fails to deposit the required 5 %, they lose the entirety of that trip’s catch and pay a penalty of 1 kg to the community for that day.

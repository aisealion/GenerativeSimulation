Policy: Each fisher may take up to 4 kg per trip, the community’s total daily catch must not exceed 50 kg, the lake must retain at least 7 kg of fish at all times, and each fisher is guaranteed a minimum catch of 1 kg per trip.

Operationalization: Each fisher logs catch on a communal ledger after every trip; a rotating captain reviews the ledger at the end of each day. If a fisher takes less than 1 kg or exceeds 4 kg, they must pay a fee to the community reserve equal to 10 % of the excess or missing amount. If the lake drops below 7 kg, fishing is suspended until the lake’s stock returns above that level, with a community meeting to decide how to restore stock. All fished fish are recorded, and any violations result in a one‑week fishing ban for that fisher.

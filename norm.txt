Policy: Each fisher may take up to 30 kg per trip, provided the lake retains at least 5 % of its pre‑trip stock as a reserve; if that reserve falls below 5 %, all quotas are reduced by 20 % for the next round.

Operationalization: At the start of each trip the fisher records the current lake stock on the communal ledger. The fisher then takes a catch, records the catch weight and the new lake stock on a waterproof logbook, and notes the reserve percentage (post‑trip stock / pre‑trip stock × 100). If the reserve is under 5 % the community convenes and cuts all quotas by 20 % for the following round. When no other fishers are present to sign, the fisher records the data, asks any nearby fisher to witness and sign if possible, keeps a copy, and submits the logbook and witness signatures to the lake‑stock keeper at the next meeting; the keeper signs and transfers a copy to the communal ledger, flagging the entry for later verification by the community.

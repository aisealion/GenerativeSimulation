Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 90% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 250 kg; keep 1 kg for sustenance; deposit 0.3% of catch (rounded to 0.1 kg) into a communal reserve that must stay at least 30 kg; the lake must never fall below 70 kg after any trip.

Operationalization: Every fisher records the exact catch weight on a shared ledger each trip. The deposit is automatically calculated (0.3% of the catch) and transferred to the communal reserve account. If the planned catch would cause the lake to drop below 70 kg, the fisher must reduce the catch to keep the lake at that minimum. The communal reserve is monitored weekly; any fisher who fails to deposit the required amount must forfeit a proportion of their next catch until the reserve is restored to 30 kg. Persistent non‑compliance results in temporary suspension from fishing privileges, enforced by the community group.

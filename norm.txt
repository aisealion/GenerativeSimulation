Policy: No fisher may keep more than 15 kg per trip; any fish beyond that is automatically released into the communal reserve, and the reserve may be withdrawn only after the neutral reserve manager’s authorization following a majority vote of the community.

Operationalization: Before each trip, every fisher logs intended catch on the shared ledger.
During the trip they record each kg caught; when the cumulative total reaches 15 kg, any further catch is released back into the lake and added to the communal reserve pool.
All catch data are submitted to the neutral reserve manager for verification each month.
The reserve manager enforces penalties: a formal warning, revocation of the fisher’s permit for one trip, and requires restitution or community service to restore privileges.
Withdrawals from the communal reserve are initiated by a written request, reviewed by the reserve manager, and approved only after a majority vote at a scheduled community gathering.
Withdrawals may be made for declared emergencies or approved community projects.
All actions are recorded in the shared ledger and audited monthly.

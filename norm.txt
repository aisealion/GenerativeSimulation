Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 1.2 kg per trip, or no more than 6 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller, and the lake must retain at least 70 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve after each catch.

Operationalization: Before each trip the fisher notes the lake’s current biomass from the last community survey and records the intended catch weight. After the catch, the fish are weighed and logged in the community ledger. If the catch would reduce the lake’s biomass below the 70 % reserve threshold, the fisher must either reduce the catch, return the excess fish to the reserve, or pay a fee that goes into a community pool for restocking. Compliance is monitored by a rotating observer system; violations are recorded in the ledger and repeated infractions lead to temporary fishing bans.

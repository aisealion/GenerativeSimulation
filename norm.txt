Policy: Each fisher may keep up to 30 kg per trip; any fish beyond 30 kg is deposited into a community reserve that can be withdrawn by fishers who catch less than 5 kg per trip to make up their shortfall.

Operationalization: At the end of each trip every fisher records their catch and deposits any surplus beyond 30 kg into the reserve, verified by a rotating registrar chosen from the community. Fishers who catch under 5 kg can request up to 5 kg from the reserve, but only if they have not exceeded the 30‑kg cap in more than half of their recent trips. The reserve is logged daily; if it falls below 10 kg the community must vote to temporarily lower the 30‑kg cap to 25 kg for all fishers until the reserve is replenished. Non‑compliance (failing to deposit surplus or to report catch) results in loss of reserve access for that fisher until the issue is corrected.

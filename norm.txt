Policy: Each fisher may take up to 35 kg per trip, provided the lake retains at least 1 % of its current stock after each catch; if a fisher fails to leave this reserve, the community will impose a fine of 1 kg for every kg of reserve shortfall, payable immediately to the council’s community fund.

Operationalization: After each trip, each fisher submits a written record of catch weight and a photograph of the lake’s visible stock level. The council verifies that at least 1 % of the measured stock remains; if the reserve falls below 1 %, the fisher pays a fine equal to 1 kg per kg of reserve shortfall, payable on the spot or the next day. The council’s treasurer, elected by the council each calendar year, transfers the fine to a dedicated community bank account and logs the transaction in the council ledger, which is posted online daily for transparency. The council records the count of reserve violations per fisher; a fisher is considered to have repeated a violation when they fail to meet the reserve rule more than twice within the last ten rounds. Upon reaching this threshold, the council imposes a temporary ban for the next two consecutive fishing rounds; the ban can only be lifted after the fisher has successfully complied with the reserve rule for at least four consecutive rounds. The rule is reviewed every five rounds. The council is composed of the seven active fishers in the community, and the treasurer serves for one year and may be re‑elected.

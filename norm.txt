Policy: No fisher may take more than 4 kg per trip or 10 % of the lake’s current stock, whichever is lower; any excess must be handed to community storage and a violation incurs a one‑week ban.

Operationalization: 1. Dawn: every fisher casts a small standard sample net, counts fish, and records the number in the shared ledger.
2. The council multiplies that count by the lake‑to‑net area ratio to estimate daily stock, calculates 10 % of that stock, and posts the 10 % limit, the 4 kg bag size, and the daily stock estimate on the ledger and community bulletin.
3. Before setting out, each fisher marks their gear with the 4 kg bag or the 10 % limit, weighs their haul, and records the total catch and any excess in the ledger.
4. If the haul exceeds the lower of 4 kg or 10 %, the fisher hands the excess bag to the appointed fish keeper; the fish keeper logs the excess weight in the ledger under the fisher’s name and the date.
5. The ledger is submitted daily to the council; the council reviews it each day, records any violations, and updates the ban register.
6. The ban register is kept on a public board in the storage shed and in the ledger; any fisher named on it is barred from setting out for seven consecutive days, after which the ban expires automatically.
7. The council holds a weekly meeting to review the ledger, verify that bans have expired, and adjust the 10 % limit or 4 kg bag size if the lake stock estimate has changed.

Policy: Each fisher may take up to 12kg per trip, and the lake must retain at least 40% of its fish stock at all times.

Operationalization: At the start of each trip, every fisher records a catch target; a communal tally is kept after each season. If the lake’s remaining stock falls below 40% of the original level, fishing is suspended until the stock is restored. Any fisher who exceeds 12kg must return the excess to a community pot that is used to subsidize fishers who fail to meet the 1kg survival minimum; a fisher who cannot catch 1kg must work a community shift for the next two trips.

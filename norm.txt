Policy: Each fisher may take up to 12 kg per trip, return any excess immediately, record the trip in the communal log within 48 h, and the leader verifies the return; violations are tallied monthly and any fisher with more than two violations faces a penalty of restricted fishing days, one per violation beyond two, up to three days, decided by a community vote.

Operationalization: Trip procedure: 1) After returning to the dock, the fisher immediately returns any fish beyond 12 kg to the marked return bin, signs a receipt, and records the date, time, and total catch weight in the communal log. 2) The fisher submits the log to the community leader by 6 pm on the same day or no later than 48 h after the trip. 3) The leader inspects the return area and verifies the signed receipt before accepting the log. 4) If the receipt is missing or the log is not submitted within 48 h, the leader records a missing‑log violation. 5) Each day the leader reviews the docking schedule and the communal log to flag any missing entries and records them as violations. 6) At month’s end the leader tallies all violations per fisher (each over‑12 kg entry or missing log counts as one). 7) If a fisher has more than two violations, a community meeting is held; the meeting votes to assign one restricted fishing day per violation beyond the first two, capped at three days. 8) The leader records the decision in the communal log, notifies the fisher, and bars the fisher from fishing on those days. 9) The leader continues to monitor compliance by checking the log each morning and verifying the fisher’s notice at the dock, recording any breaches.

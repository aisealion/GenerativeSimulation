Policy: Each fisher may catch up to 4 kg per trip, must keep at least 1 kg for themselves, and the community’s total monthly catch cannot exceed 25 % of the lake’s current stock at the start of the month.

Operationalization: At the end of each fishing day every fisher records two numbers in the communal logbook: (1) Total catch for that trip, and (2) Personal keep (minimum 1 kg). The logbook is kept in a locked box at the community center and is reviewed by the appointed community steward each month. The steward sums the Total catch from all entries; if the sum exceeds 25 % of the lake’s stock at the month’s start, the steward identifies the trip(s) that pushed the total over the limit by iterating through the daily logs in chronological order. The fisher whose catch caused the over‑limit must surrender only from the portion of his haul designated for the community pool (the “Community” column), keeping the 1 kg Personal keep intact. The surrendered fish are moved to the communal pot, counted by the steward, and a receipt is signed. If any fisher records a Personal keep less than 1 kg, the steward requests proof (weigh‑in or witness); failure to prove compliance results in a one‑month fishing ban and the shortfall is added to the community pool. All penalties are recorded in the logbook. The steward publishes the monthly totals, excess amounts, and penalties on the community board so all fishers can verify transparency. This procedure ensures the lake is protected, each fisher sustains themselves, and the community pool is replenished fairly.

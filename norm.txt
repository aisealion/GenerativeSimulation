Policy: Each fisher may take up to 1 kg per trip; the community’s total weekly harvest may not exceed 50 % of the lake’s current stock, with a hard minimum of 1 kg remaining.

Operationalization: At the start of each week a weekly meeting is held where all fishers gather to estimate the lake’s current stock through a consensus eye‑test. The agreed stock is recorded on the shared ledger maintained by the fishery committee, whose member is typically the eldest fisher. Each fishing trip is logged on this ledger, noting the fisher’s name and catch weight. A fisher may take as many trips as desired provided no single trip exceeds 1 kg and the cumulative weekly catch (including all fishers) does not push the community harvest beyond 50 % of the current stock or leave less than 1 kg in the lake. If a projected total catch would violate these limits, the weekly meeting votes to postpone the remaining trips to the next week. If a fisher exceeds the 1 kg per‑trip limit, the surplus is surrendered to the community pool and that fisher is barred from fishing the following week, a decision made by the same meeting. The community pool is managed by the council and used for communal meals, sale, or as a safety reserve. The weekly limits can be adjusted by a community vote if the stock changes significantly.

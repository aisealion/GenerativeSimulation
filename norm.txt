Policy: No fisher may harvest more than 8% of the lake’s current biomass per trip, and 5% of the lake’s biomass must be maintained in a communal reserve at all times.

Operationalization: Before each trip, each fisher calculates 8% of the current lake biomass and caps his take at that amount. After fishing, all fish are logged and the community reserve is updated. If a fisher exceeds his quota, he must return the excess fish to the lake or pay a community‑agreed fine. The reserve is monitored quarterly; if lake biomass falls below a set threshold, all limits are proportionally reduced until recovery. The community meets monthly to review logs and adjust percentages if needed.

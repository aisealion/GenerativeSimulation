Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 90% of the lake’s current stock per trip, keep all that catch except a mandatory deposit of 0.5 kg into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 5 kg, and the lake must never fall below 20 kg after any trip.

Operationalization: At the start of each trip, a fisher records the lake’s stock. They may fish up to 90% of that amount. After fishing, they deposit 0.5 kg into the communal reserve ledger kept by the designated keeper and keep the remainder. They then verify the lake’s remaining stock is at least 20 kg; if it would fall below, they hold back enough fish and deposit additional 0.5 kg increments until the lake is back to 20 kg. If a fisher fails to deposit the required 0.5 kg or the extra amount when needed, their entire catch for that trip is forfeited to the reserve on the following trip. The communal reserve is monitored weekly; it must never dip below 5 kg, and any shortfall is replenished by community contributions.

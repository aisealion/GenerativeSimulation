Policy: Each fisher may take no more than 10% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 10 kg, and the community’s total weekly catch must not exceed 20% of lake stock; 10% of the lake is reserved each week, and fishing is paused if stock falls below 120 kg until it recovers to 120 kg or higher.

Operationalization: Weekly weigh‑in: every Sunday the elected community monitor (or deputy) weighs the lake with the calibrated scale, calculates the 20% weekly quota and 10% reserve, and posts stock, quota, reserve on the shared ledger. Daily catch recording: each fisher brings catch to the monitor, is weighed on the community scale, and the weight is logged on the ledger before adding to the weekly total. Limit enforcement: if a fisher’s catch exceeds the 10% trip limit, the monitor returns the excess and records a one‑day ban with a “Banned until” date. Ban removal: the next morning the monitor checks expired bans, marks them “Ban cleared” on the ledger, and informs the fisher. Pause rule: if the weekly weigh‑in shows stock below 120 kg, all fishing is automatically paused; when stock reaches 120 kg or higher, fishing resumes without further vote. Monitor election: at each Sunday meeting the community elects a monitor by simple majority; a deputy is named as backup; if the monitor is unavailable the deputy takes over, otherwise a new monitor is elected. Community review: at each weekly review the community checks the ledger to confirm totals are within limits and discusses any irregularities.

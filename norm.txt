Policy: Each fisher keeps up to 1 unit for personal consumption, any surplus is added to a communal pool; a daily catch limit of 2 units applies, with a 0.5‑unit fine per excess unit, and the pool is redistributed equally to fishers whose consumption fell below 1 unit.

Operationalization: 1. Each fisher records total catch on the shared ledger and signs it. 2. The designated pool distributor (selected by rotating alphabetical order of last names) checks the ledger for entries over 2 units. 3. If a fisher exceeds 2 units, the excess is added to the pool and the fisher incurs a fine of 0.5 fish‑units per excess unit; the distributor collects this fine and adds it to the pool. 4. The distributor collects all pool contributions and calculates the total pool. 5. The distributor identifies fishers whose personal consumption <1 unit, calculates each shortfall, and distributes the pool equally among those shortfallers. 6. The ledger entry for the day is signed by the fisher, the distributor, and a second fisher chosen by the fisher as the next available fisher in alphabetical order who has not yet signed that day (or at random if none available). 7. The fine is considered unpaid if the fisher fails to submit the required payment for the excess catch before the distributor finalizes the pool (typically by the end of the day). In that case, the fisher forfeits that day’s personal consumption: the fisher’s consumption is recorded as 0, and all remaining fish are treated as part of the shared pool for redistribution.

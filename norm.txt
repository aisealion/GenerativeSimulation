Policy: No fisher may remove more than 15% of the lake’s current volume per trip; each fisher must keep at least 1 kg for survival, and any catch beyond the 15% cap must be returned to the lake. Failure to return excess results in forfeiture of the next trip’s permit and requires restitution of the missing kilograms within two days.

Operationalization: Each trip the fisher places the entire catch on a communal scale, records the weight, keeps 1 kg, and if the catch exceeds 15% of the lake’s current volume they pour the excess back into the lake in front of a council‑appointed witness. The remaining catch is weighed again; the difference between the first and second weigh must equal the net catch reported. The council logs both weigh‑in records in a communal ledger, updates the lake’s stock_kg by subtracting the net catch and adding any restitution, and records the fisher’s permit status. If the weigh‑in shows an excess beyond the 15% cap, the fisher’s permit is marked ‘revoked’ for one trip, and the council notifies the fisher to return the missing kilograms within two days. Upon successful restitution, verified by a second weigh‑in, the ledger removes the revocation, credits the lake’s stock_kg with the returned kilograms, and restores the permit. All ledger entries and permit statuses are displayed publicly so every fisher can see their own standing.

Policy: No villager may fish more than 2 units per day, must abstain during the first two weeks of March, and any excess must be returned to the lake; violations trigger a one‑week fishing ban and mandatory community service.

Operationalization: 1. Each fishing day, every villager records the number of fish caught (units) in a shared ledger and signs the logbook. 2. A lake guard is chosen by a rotating draw from volunteers; the guard checks each day’s logbook entry on the day of catch, verifies limits, and, if excess is reported, confiscates the extra fish and returns them to the lake. 3. At the weekly village meeting, the guard reads all logbooks, the community confirms any reported day exceeding 2 units, and the excess fish is returned at that meeting. 4. The ledger is tallied weekly; the fisher with the lowest total catch is identified. 5. If only one fisher has the lowest total, that fisher performs community service (cleaning, repairs, etc.) that week; if there is a tie, the tied fishers undergo a random draw (coin flip or die roll) to determine who serves. 6. A fisher who fails to return excess fish at the meeting is confiscated by the guard, loses fishing rights for one week, and must perform community service before rights are restored. 7. The community publishes the guard schedule and the enforcement protocol each month, and all fishers sign a pledge at the start of March to abstain from fishing for the first two weeks.

Policy: Each fisher may keep up to 8 kg per trip; any excess must be handed to the community reserve, which is held and managed by the community council through a dock steward and a communal ledger.

Operationalization: A fisher’s trip is the time from setting out from shore with a net or line until returning to the dock and ceasing fishing. Upon return, the fisher weighs the entire catch immediately on a portable scale, records the total weight, and compares it to the 8 kg threshold. If the total is 8 kg or less, the fisher keeps the catch. If the total exceeds 8 kg, the fisher keeps the first 8 kg and hands the excess weight of fish to the reserve steward at the dock. The steward records the total catch and the excess amount on the communal ledger, places the excess fish into the clearly marked communal bin, and updates the ledger with the exact weight deposited. The council reviews the ledger each week, cross‑checks the bin’s contents against the recorded excess, and holds a brief meeting to address any discrepancies. If a fisher fails to return the correct excess amount, the council flags the fisher on the ledger and may impose a temporary suspension of fishing rights until the balance is restored. Peer observation and community trust reinforce compliance, with other fishers free to verify the ledger and bin at any time.

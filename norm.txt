Policy: Each fisher may haul up to 25 kg per trip and the lake’s total daily catch is capped at 350 kg; any excess is forfeited and returned, and each fisher must pay a 5 kg penalty into a communal reserve, with mandatory reporting within 24 h and progressive bans for non‑compliance.

Operationalization: 1. Before every trip, the designated community scribe maintains a communal sheet; each fisher signs their intended catch and the sheet is posted on the notice board. 2. After the trip, the scribe weighs all catches; if the sum exceeds 350 kg the excess is returned to the lake, and each fisher’s 5 kg penalty is deducted from their haul and added to the communal reserve ledger. 3. If a fisher attempts to haul more than 25 kg in a single trip, the excess portion is forfeited, the fisher pays the 5 kg penalty from their catch, and the remaining legal catch is retained. 4. Penalties: a 5 kg penalty must be paid in the fisher’s own catch; if a fisher’s legal haul falls below 5 kg after forfeiture, they must use personal reserves or other means to meet the penalty; failure to pay results in a temporary ban until payment or proof of compliance. 5. Reporting: all catch amounts, penalties, and reserve contributions are logged in a ledger kept by the council; the ledger is posted weekly on the community notice board for transparency. 6. Ban structure: first offense 2‑day suspension, second offense 4‑day, third 8‑day, fourth a permanent ban; ban lengths are set by the rotating council of all active fishers. 7. Communal reserve: held in a sealed barrel at the council office; the reserve is separate from individual accounts and can only be accessed by council during the quarterly review. 8. Quarterly review: the council votes on how reserve funds may be allocated to equipment repair, new nets, a new launch, or community events; no fisher may withdraw reserve funds for personal use. 9. Enforcement: the council, with the scribe’s assistance, conducts weigh‑ins, maintains the ledger, imposes bans, and ensures all penalties are collected and the reserve is protected.

Policy: Each fisher may harvest no more than 1.0 kg per trip or 4 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller; after each catch the lake must retain at least 88 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve.

Operationalization: All fishers will log their catch on a communal ledger each trip. The catch must be calculated against the lake’s current biomass and capped accordingly. After the catch, the remaining biomass must be at least 88 % of the previous total; if a fisher exceeds the limit, they must immediately return the excess to the lake or pay a fine. A monthly audit will verify compliance.

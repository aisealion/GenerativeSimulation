Policy: Maintain a lake reserve of 2 kg; each fisher may take up to 3 kg per trip and no more than 9 kg per week; 5 % of every catch is deposited into a community pool and refunded once the lake reaches 2 kg again; any overage is fined 0.02 kg per kg over and causes the fisher to skip their next trip.

Operationalization: The steward (me, Nadia) will record the lake stock after each trip and compare it to each fisher’s personal ledger. Each fisher must deposit 5 % of their catch into the community pool ledger at the next communal gathering or post the transfer on the board. Weekly limits are tracked by summing daily ledger entries over the 7‑day period between consecutive communal gatherings; if a fisher exceeds 9 kg, the steward calculates a 0.02 kg fine per excess kg, adds the fine to the community pool, and marks the fisher as ‘skipped’ for the following trip. If a fisher fails to deposit the required 5 %, the steward flags the fisher, adds the missing amount as a fine, and denies the fisher a fishing pass until the fine is paid. When the lake’s reserve reaches 2 kg, the community pool is refunded to each fisher proportionally to the 5 % of their previous catch that had been deposited. All records are kept on the community ledger and announced at each gathering, ensuring transparency and accountability.

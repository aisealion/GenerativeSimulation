Policy: No fisher may take more than 10 % of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 10 kg; the community’s total weekly catch must not exceed 20 % of the lake stock, with 10 % of the lake reserved each week for regeneration and a monthly reserve week during which no fishing is allowed.

Operationalization: Each morning the community leader pulls the latest lake_stock from the ledger, subtracts the 10 % reserve (tracked as a separate ledger entry) to get the available stock. The per‑trip limit is 10 % of this available stock, capped at 10 kg. Fishermen record each catch on the communal ledger before departure; the ledger’s update routine automatically subtracts the catch from lake_stock and logs the new value for the day. At week’s end the leader tallies all entries, compares the total to the 20 % cap based on the same available stock, and if exceeded flags the excess. The leader then chooses to either return the excess to the lake (by adding it back to lake_stock) or redistribute it proportionally among that week’s fishers. Any violation of the per‑trip or weekly cap triggers a one‑trip suspension: the fisher’s name is added to a public “Suspended” list visible on the bulletin board and in SMS/email reminders, and their next planned trip is denied by the leader at the launch point. The suspended fisher may not record a ledger entry until the suspension is lifted. The monthly reserve week is announced at the council meeting, posted on the ledger and bulletin board, and confirmed by SMS/email; during that week all fishing entries are blocked and the reserve pool remains untouched. All ledger updates, suspensions, and redistributions are logged and available for audit by any community member.

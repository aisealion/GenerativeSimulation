Policy: Maximum catch per trip: 3 kg per fisher; 30 % of each catch must be deposited into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 30 % of the lake’s current biomass.

Operationalization: Each fisher records his catch on the shared ledger on the same day he fishes, immediately after the trip or at the latest before the reserve keeper finalises that day's biomass. 30 % of the recorded catch is deposited into the communal reserve at that time. The reserve keeper (Lani) collects deposits, publishes reserve levels, and monitors the reserve against the 30 % threshold. If the reserve falls below 30 % of the lake’s current biomass, the keeper announces a 24‑hour fishing pause. The keeper verifies each fisher’s ledger entry and deposit; if a fisher exceeds 3 kg or fails to deposit 30 %, the keeper records a discrepancy and the community elders impose a 10 % fine that is added to the communal reserve. The lake’s current biomass is updated each day by taking the previous day’s biomass, subtracting total catch, and adding all deposits and fines; the 30 % reserve target for the next day is calculated from that updated biomass.

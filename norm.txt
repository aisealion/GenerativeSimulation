Policy: The community shall implement a sustainable harvesting limit that caps total fish extraction per round to 5% of the lake's current stock, ensuring long-term ecological balance. Additionally, the norms **DynamicIndividualCapNorm**, **WeeklyAuditNorm**, and **ReserveBoostNorm** are active to dynamically adjust individual caps, conduct weekly audits, and boost communal reserves when needed.

Operationalization:
1. At the start of each round, calculate `cap = 0.05 * available_stock(state["runtime"])`.
2. **DynamicIndividualCapNorm**: each fisher's personal cap is adjusted by `cap_adjustment_factor` (default 0.05) based on recent stock trends.
3. **WeeklyAuditNorm**: total catches are summed weekly; excess over the target is penalized at `audit_penalty_rate` (default 0.1).
4. **ReserveBoostNorm**: if stock falls below `reserve_boost_threshold` (default 120.0), a temporary communal reserve contribution boost is applied.
5. Any harvest that would cause the round total to exceed the 5% cap is reduced to the cap limit; the excess is returned to the lake and the fisher receives a warning note.
6. After harvests, stock regeneration proceeds as defined in the physics module.

Parameters:
- `cap_adjustment_factor`: float, default 0.05 – incremental adjustment to individual caps per stock deviation unit.
- `audit_penalty_rate`: float, default 0.1 – proportion of excess catch penalized during weekly audits.
- `reserve_boost_threshold`: float, default 120.0 – stock level below which reserve boosts are triggered.

Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 80% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 180 kg; must keep 1 kg for sustenance; deposit 1 % of the catch (rounded to 0.1 kg) into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 30 kg; the lake must never fall below 70 kg after any trip.

Operationalization: Before each trip, a fisher checks the current lake stock with the shared tally. They calculate 80 % of that stock, take the smaller of that or 180 kg, subtract 1 kg for themselves, then calculate 1 % of the actual catch and add it to the communal reserve. The reserve is monitored by a rotating committee of two fishers; if a fisher fails to deposit or the reserve dips below 30 kg, that fisher’s next trip is limited to 50 % of the previous catch until compliance is restored. All actions are recorded in a shared ledger accessible to everyone.

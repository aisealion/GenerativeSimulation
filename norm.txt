Policy: Each fisher may take up to 12% of the lake’s current stock per trip, but if the lake holds more than 200 kg the limit increases to 15%. The limit applies to the stock at the time of the trip, not after the catch.

Operationalization: Before each trip, every fisher checks the lake’s current stock. If the stock is >200 kg, the fisher may claim up to 15% of that amount; otherwise the claim is capped at 12%. After the trip, the community verifier weighs the catch and records the weight in the communal ledger. If the weight exceeds the allowed percentage, the verifier logs an infraction, flags the fisher as banned, and records the ban in the ledger. During the current round, the banned fisher is denied permission to fish; the ban automatically expires at the start of the next round, after which the fisher may fish again subject to the same rules. The verifier and ledger together enforce the ban and maintain the record of all catches and infractions.

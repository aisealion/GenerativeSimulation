Policy: Each fisher may take up to 70% of the lake’s current stock per trip, but must leave at least 5% of that stock as a reserve. Failure to leave the reserve triggers a fine of 1 kg per kg of reserve lost; if the reserve falls below 5% for two consecutive rounds, all quotas are reduced by 20% in the following round.

Operationalization: At the start of each round the lake’s stock S is recorded after the previous round’s fishing and the end‑of‑round logbook. Each fisher calculates his maximum take as 0.70S and his required reserve as 0.05S. During the round the fisher records his catch in the logbook. After all fishers return, the community council reviews the logbook entries, checks that each fisher has left at least 5% of the starting stock, and calculates the remaining stock R. If R<0.05S, a fine of (0.05S–R) kg is imposed, equal to 1 kg per kg of reserve lost. The fine is taken from the fisher’s catch or, if he has a deposit, deducted from that deposit before it is returned. All fines go into the communal reserve fund, which is used for lake maintenance, stocking, and shared expenses. A fisher who fails to leave the required reserve is barred from fishing for the next two trips. If the reserve falls below 5% for two consecutive rounds, the council reduces all fishers’ quotas by 20% in the next round.

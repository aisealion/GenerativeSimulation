Policy: Each fisher may take up to 35 kg per trip, provided the lake retains at least 1 % of its pre‑trip stock as reserve; a violation triggers a fine of 1 kg per kg of reserve lost and a 20 % quota reduction unless the fisher meets compliance criteria allowing a community vote to lift the reduction.

Operationalization: 1. Ledger entries: Every trip is logged with pre‑trip stock (S), catch (C), and post‑trip stock (R). Reserve = R / S. 2. Reserve rule: R must be ≥ 0.01 × S. 3. Fine: If R < 0.01 × S, compute shortfall d = (0.01 × S) − R. The ledger records a "Fine" entry: {fisher:"Solo", amount:d, description:"Reserve shortfall"}. The d kg is deducted from the fisher’s net catch and added to the communal reserve. 4. Compliance score: Start at 100 %. For each violation (reserve shortfall or catch > 35 kg) or fine, subtract 5 % from the score. 5. Quota limits: Default quota per trip is 35 kg. If a fisher incurs a reserve violation, the community imposes a 20 % quota reduction for that fisher’s next round (quota becomes 28 kg) until a qualifying window allows a lift. 6. Lift criteria: The ledger flags a fisher for a lifting vote when the following are met: a) no reserve violations in the last five trips; b) no fines in the last three trips; c) compliance score ≥ 90 %; d) stewardship report submitted within 48 h of the last trip. 7. Vote: The community holds a simple majority vote at the next meeting or via the agreed digital platform. The result is recorded in the ledger. If approved, the fisher’s quota for the next round reverts to 35 kg; if not, the 20 % reduction remains until the next qualifying window.

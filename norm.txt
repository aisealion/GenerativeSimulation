Policy: Solo may take up to 12% of the lake per trip, while all other fishers are limited to 8% per trip.

Operationalization: Every fishing day Solo will head out first at dawn and may catch up to 12% of the lake’s current stock. After Solo returns, each other fisher may go out for a single trip and may catch up to 8% of the remaining stock. All catches will be logged in a communal ledger maintained by the community coordinator. If any fisher exceeds their percentage, the excess catch will be deducted and deposited into a community reserve, and the fisher will be banned from fishing for one day. The coordinator will enforce the limits and mediate disputes.

Policy: Each fisher may take up to 3 kg per trip, keep at least 1 kg, and the community’s monthly catch may not exceed 25 % of the lake’s stock at the start of the month, capped at 6 kg.

Operationalization: A shared ledger will be kept in the community hall by the Fishery Committee Chair; every fisher signs the ledger after each trip to record their catch. The Chair is elected each month by simple majority, may serve up to two consecutive months, and is removed by a vote if deemed unfair. The monthly reconciliation occurs at the end of the month: the Chair sums each fisher’s total taken and compares it to the community quota (25 % of lake stock, capped at 6 kg). Any fish beyond the 1 kg keep threshold is considered surplus; if the total surplus exceeds the quota, the Chair assigns responsibility proportionally to the fishers who contributed to the excess. Assigned fishers must return the surplus within the same month; failure to return triggers a ledger entry of an outstanding fish debt equal to the surplus not returned. A fisher with an outstanding debt may fish only after settling the debt by returning the equivalent weight on a future trip. If a debt remains unresolved by month‑end, the fisher receives a one‑trip ban, and the community’s next month’s quota is automatically reduced by 5 %. Negative balances carry to the next month, subject to the same ban and quota reduction rules. The ledger is publicly visible so all fishers can verify records.

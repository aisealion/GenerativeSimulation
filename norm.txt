Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 1.0 kg per trip, or no more than 5 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller, and the lake must retain at least 80 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve after each catch.

Operationalization: On each trip the fisher reports the weight caught and the current lake biomass to the community ledger overseen by the steward. The steward verifies the catch does not exceed the 1.0 kg or 5 % limit and that the remaining lake mass is at least 80 % of the pre‑catch biomass. If a fisher exceeds the limit, the surplus fish must be returned to the lake before the next trip and the fisher forfeits fishing rights for one cycle. Lake biomass is measured at the end of each season by a neutral observer; if the reserve falls below 80 % all quotas are reduced until the reserve is restored.

Policy: Each fisher may take no more than 8 kg per trip, and the lake must retain at least 30 % of its pre‑trip stock after every round. If a fisher exceeds the 8 kg limit or the lake falls below the 30 % threshold, that fisher is flagged and barred from fishing for one full round before being allowed to rejoin.

Operationalization: 1. Prior to each round, the community rotates the role of ledger keeper among fishers who are not flagged, giving the one who has not served the longest time. If all fishers are flagged, the ledger keeper is selected from the flagged fishers by the same rotation rule.
2. The ledger keeper records the lake’s pre‑trip stock at the exact start of the round on the communal ledger.
3. After all fishing, the ledger keeper weighs each fisher’s net, records the actual catch weight, and updates the ledger.
4. The community collectively reviews the ledger, sums all catches, and verifies that the remaining lake stock equals the pre‑trip stock minus total catch. The community then checks that this remaining stock is at least 30 % of the pre‑trip stock.
5. If any fisher’s catch exceeds 8 kg or the remaining stock is below 30 % of pre‑trip, the ledger keeper flags that fisher on the ledger and posts the ban.
6. The community refuses to allow any flagged fisher to participate in the next fishing round.
7. After one full round has passed, the flagged fisher may rejoin the community fishing activities.

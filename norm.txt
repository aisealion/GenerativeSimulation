Policy: Each fisher may harvest at most 1.0 kg per trip or 4 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller, and after each catch the lake must retain at least 88 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve.

Operationalization: Before each trip the fisher notes the lake’s current biomass and may take no more than the lesser of 1.0 kg or 4 % of that biomass. After the catch, the fisher records the harvested amount in a community ledger that all fishers can view. If a fisher exceeds the limit, the excess must be returned to the lake and the fisher pays a penalty equal to 10 % of the excess amount to a communal restoration fund. Quarterly community meetings will verify lake biomass and, if the 4 % figure is too high or low, adjust it accordingly. Anyone who fails to report or repeatedly violates the rule may be temporarily barred from fishing and their penalty doubled until they comply.

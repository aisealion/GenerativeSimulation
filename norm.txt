Policy: Each fisher may take no more than 5 kg per trip and the community may not exceed 100 kg per day.

Operationalization: After every trip, the fisher on duty records the catch in a communal ledger kept in the shore hut; the ledger is verified by the next duty fisher and signed. If a fisher attempts to bring in more than 5 kg, the excess weight is returned to the lake. The Community Council, elected from each fishing household, meets weekly to review the ledger, vote by simple majority on any quota adjustments, and impose a seven‑day temporary ban on any fisher who fails to record a catch or exceeds limits. The council issues a written notice of the ban, maintains a register of banned fishers, and checks permits at the hut; all fishers must respect the ban and may not allow the banned fisher to depart. The ban automatically lifts after seven days, allowing the fisher to resume normal fishing.

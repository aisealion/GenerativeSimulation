Policy: Each fisher may take up to 25% of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 4 kg, and may not cumulatively take more than 70% of the lake’s stock at the start of each calendar month.

Operationalization: 1. By the 5th of every month each fisher records the number of trips they intend to make in the "Intended Trips" column of the shared ledger.
2. The appointed lake steward conducts a "Monthly Stock Assessment" at the start of the month, samples the lake, calculates the total stock, and records the figure in the ledger.
3. The steward calculates 70% of that stock to establish the community’s monthly catch ceiling.
4. The monthly ceiling is divided equally among all fishers who declared trips that month; each fisher’s personal monthly target is the ceiling divided by the number of declared fishers.
5. For each trip, a fisher logs the actual catch immediately in the ledger.
6. At month’s end, the steward reviews total catches. If the cumulative catch exceeds the 70% ceiling, fishers who contributed to the excess are identified.
7. Violators must either reduce their next month’s catch by 10% of the lake’s current stock and/or donate half of their next month’s catch to the community pot.
8. Any violation triggers a community meeting within a day or two after the monthly stock assessment; the offending fisher’s future trips are suspended until they return to compliance, usually until the next monthly stock assessment.
9. The ledger remains the single source of truth for stock, intended trips, actual catches, and enforcement decisions, ensuring transparency and fairness for all fishers.

Policy: No fisher may take more than 5 kg per trip; each must deposit 30 % of their catch into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 30 % of the lake’s current biomass. If the reserve falls below that threshold, the keeper records a 1 kg reduction to all personal limits in the ledger, which remains in force until the reserve rebounds above 30 %.

Operationalization: 1. After every trip, the fisher places the entire catch on the communal scale. 2. The keeper (selected each week by a die roll among present fishers; if none present, the next fisher in alphabetical order takes over) weighs the catch, calculates 30 % of the total, and requires the fisher to deposit that amount into the marked reserve pit. 3. The keeper records the catch weight, deposit weight, fisher’s name, and a signature in the communal ledger. 4. If the deposit is incomplete, the keeper logs a “shortfall” note, informs the community, and records a 1 kg catch‑limit reduction in the ledger. 5. The keeper checks the reserve pit’s total weight; if it is below 30 % of the lake’s current biomass, the keeper immediately logs a 1 kg catch‑limit reduction and all fishers must adhere to the new reduced limit. 6. The reduced limit remains until the reserve’s weight reaches at least 30 % of lake biomass, at which point the keeper resets the limit to 5 kg and updates the ledger.

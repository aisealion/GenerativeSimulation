Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 12 kg per day, with a mandatory 5 % deposit of the catch into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 25 kg in the lake.

Operationalization: Every fisher records the weight of their catch on the communal ledger before leaving the shore. 5 % of that weight is deposited into a shared fish‑box that is kept on the dock.  The box is inspected nightly by the council of elders; if the reserve falls below 25 kg, all fishing stops until the reserve is topped up by the next day's catches.  If a fisher fails to record or deposit the required 5 %, they forfeit the right to fish for the next two days and must pay the equivalent of 5 % of their missed catch to the reserve as a penalty.

Policy: Each fisher may take up to 45 kg per trip, but the lake must keep a minimum reserve of 15 kg; if the reserve falls below 15 kg, each fisher must set aside 2 kg of their catch into the reserve before they physically depart the lake.

Operationalization: Before each trip the Lake Recorder logs the current reserve and the fisher’s intended catch. After the trip the Recorder adds the full catch to the reserve. If the updated reserve is below 15 kg, the fisher must hand over 2 kg of that catch to the Recorder at the dock before physically departing. The Recorder records the donation, adds the 2 kg to the reserve, and confirms compliance. If a fisher fails to donate, the Recorder logs the failure, revokes the fisher’s right to fish in the next round, and reduces the reserve by 5 kg as a sanction. All updates are posted to the community ledger for transparency.

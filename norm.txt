Policy: Each fisher may take up to 12 kg per trip, and the lake must retain at least 25 % of its pre‑trip stock after each fishing round.

Operationalization: The first fisher of the day records the lake’s weight at dawn in the communal ledger.  Every fisher logs their catch in the same ledger; no catch may exceed 12 kg.  After all fishers finish, the last fisher records the remaining lake weight.  The ledger automatically checks that the remaining weight is at least 25 % of the pre‑trip weight.  Any kg taken over 12 kg is recorded as over‑catch; each kg over the limit incurs a fine of 20 % of the current market value per kg, payable to the community pool within seven days.  If the lake ends with less than 25 % of its pre‑trip stock, the shortfall in kg is multiplied by 20 % of market value and added to the offending fisher’s debt.  All debt balances are stored per fisher in the ledger.  Unpaid penalties trigger a one‑round fishing ban and a proportional reduction of the fisher’s future catch allocation equal to (debt ÷ market‑value per kg) kg per round until cleared.  Market value per kg is the average price per kg that the community earned in the last two fishing rounds; if no sales occurred, a council‑set base rate is used.  Appeals are handled by the fishing council using the ledger as evidence.  The ledger entries are signed and timestamped for audit and appeal purposes.

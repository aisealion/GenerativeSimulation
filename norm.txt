Policy: Each fisher may take up to 10 kg per trip, must maintain a 25 % reserve of the lake’s stock, and will be fined 0.05 kg and banned from the next round if they miss the 0.1 kg deposit or exceed the 10 kg limit; a banned fisher may rejoin only after paying the missing deposit and proving their catch will stay within 10 kg.

Operationalization: 1. A weekly round runs from 6:00 am on Monday to the end of the last 2‑hour slot, after which the committee records total catches and the lake’s remaining stock.
2. Each fisher must deposit 0.1 kg into the community pool before starting a slot; deposits are recorded in a shared ledger maintained by a Fishery Oversight Committee.
3. The committee calculates the lake’s reserve as 25 % of the stock after all catches; if the reserve is below 25 % of the stock, all fishers’ maximum catch for the next round is reduced by 20 %.
4. A fisher who fails to deposit or who catches more than 10 kg is fined 0.05 kg, which is added to the community pool, and is barred from fishing the following round.
5. The ban is lifted only when the fisher pays the missing deposit and submits proof (or demonstrates in the first post‑ban slot) that their catch will not exceed 10 kg; once both conditions are met, the fisher is re‑inserted into the regular rotating schedule.
6. All fines, deposits, and maintenance costs are paid from the community pool, and the pool is reviewed quarterly by the committee to allocate funds for lake upkeep.
7. The committee meets at the end of each round to update the ledger, resolve disputes, and announce the adjusted maximum catch for the next round.

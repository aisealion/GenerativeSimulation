Policy: Each fisher may harvest no more than 1.0 kg per trip or 3 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller, and the lake must retain at least 88 % of its total biomass after each catch.

Operationalization: Before a trip a fisher announces the intended catch on the community board. After the catch, the fisher records the weight; the community council recalculates the lake’s new biomass and verifies that it remains at least 88 %. If the catch would reduce the reserve below 88 % or exceeds the 1.0 kg/3 % limit, the catch is forfeited and added to a communal reserve pool. All fishers must submit their logs monthly; failure to comply results in a temporary fishing ban for the next two rounds.

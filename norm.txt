Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 95 % of the lake’s current stock per trip, capped at 250 kg; they must keep 1 kg for sustenance, deposit 0.5 % of the catch (rounded to 0.1 kg) into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 30 kg, and the lake must never fall below 40 kg after any trip.

Operationalization: Before each trip, a fisher declares the intended catch and calculates the lake‑after‑catch value. After fishing, they keep 1 kg, deposit 0.5 % of the catch into the reserve (rounding to the nearest 0.1 kg). The reserve’s balance is updated. If a fisher’s catch would push the lake below 40 kg or exceed the 250 kg cap, they must reduce the catch accordingly. Failure to deposit the required amount results in a fine equal to 5 % of the missed deposit and a one‑round ban from fishing.

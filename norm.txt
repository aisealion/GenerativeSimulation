Policy: The community shall limit total monthly catch to 15 % of the lake’s current biomass, cap individual trips at 5 kg, set aside 10 % of the monthly catch as a communal reserve, determine reserve capacity each month by a two‑thirds vote of active fishers based on lake health metrics and capped at 10 % of monthly catch, and release any surplus beyond capacity by a supermajority vote; any fisher exceeding their base allowance must return 80 % of the excess to the lake or reserve within two days or face suspension.

Operationalization: 1. The steward, elected by a simple majority vote of active fishers for a one‑year term, records the lake’s biomass at the start of each month and calculates the 15 % total monthly catch limit.
2. The steward presents this limit and the lake health metrics to the community at the monthly meeting.  The community votes to set the reserve capacity in kilograms; a two‑thirds (supermajority) vote of active fishers must approve the capacity, which may not exceed 10 % of the monthly catch.
3. The monthly catch limit is divided equally among active fishers to give each a base allowance.
4. Throughout the month, each fisher logs every trip catch in the community ledger, and the steward verifies the logs weekly.
5. If a trip exceeds 5 kg, 20 % of the excess is automatically moved into the communal reserve for the next month.
6. At month’s end, the steward sums all logged catches; ten percent of that sum is transferred into the communal reserve.
7. If the reserve exceeds the community‑set capacity, the steward proposes release options (returning fish to the lake or funding breeding initiatives).  The community votes by supermajority to choose the release action, which is then implemented.
8. If a fisher’s cumulative monthly catch exceeds his base allowance, the ledger shows the overage.  The fisher must return 80 % of the excess to the lake or deposit it into the communal reserve within two days.  Failure to comply results in the steward recording a violation and suspending the fisher’s next trip until the overage is cleared.
9. All enforcement is carried out through the shared ledger and the steward’s oversight; sanctions are applied by the community without additional bureaucratic layers.

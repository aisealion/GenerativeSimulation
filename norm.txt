Policy: Each villager may keep up to 10 units of fish per day; any excess is automatically returned to the lake. Villagers who fall below 1 unit receive surplus from those who exceeded 10 units or voluntarily donated, redistributed by a council vote.

Operationalization: 1. Each villager fishes and records total catch on the dock ledger. 2. If catch >10 units, the excess is automatically returned to the lake, restoring the stock. 3. Any voluntary donation (fish given up while still below 10 units) is handed to the council and recorded on the same ledger. 4. At day’s end, the council tallies: (a) villagers with <1 unit (short‑fallers); (b) surplus from over‑10 catch (excess returned to lake) and all voluntary donations. 5. The council votes by simple majority on how to redistribute the surplus pool. 6. The surplus is divided proportionally to each short‑falling villager’s deficit until every short‑falling villager reaches the 1‑unit survival threshold. 7. Any remaining fish in the pool is placed into the communal reserve for future use or emergency aid. 8. The updated lake stock is confirmed after all returns, and the next day’s fishing begins with the new stock.

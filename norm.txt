Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 1.2 kg per trip, or no more than 12 % of the lake’s current biomass, and the lake must retain at least 55 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve after the catch.

Operationalization: Before setting out, each fisher reports the lake’s measured biomass (via agreed‑upon sampling). They then fish up to the lesser of 1.2 kg or 12 % of that biomass. The community records each catch on a shared ledger; if the lake’s post‑catch biomass falls below 55 % of its prior total, the last fisher’s catch is returned to the lake and a 3‑day fishing ban is imposed on them. All reports are verified by a rotating pair of community members and posted publicly for transparency.

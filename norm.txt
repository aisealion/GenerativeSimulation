Policy: Each fisher may take no more than 1.5 kg per trip, and the community may remove no more than 30 % of the lake’s current biomass per day while always keeping at least 30 % of the lake as a reserve; if the daily removal cap is exceeded, the excess catch is returned to the lake, the offending fisher forfeits the right to fish the following day, and must pay a $10 fee to the community pool.

Operationalization: All fishers record their catch in a community ledger each day. The designated ledger keeper sums total daily removals and compares them to 30 % of the lake’s current biomass. If the sum exceeds the cap, the keeper returns the excess catch to the lake, records the offending fisher’s forfeiture of the next day’s fishing right, and collects a $10 fee into the communal pool. Each fisher must also ensure that no single trip exceeds 1.5 kg; violations are treated as separate infractions with the same forfeiture and fee. The ledger keeper reports any violations to the community council for additional action. The reserve rule guarantees that at least 30 % of the lake’s biomass remains at all times.

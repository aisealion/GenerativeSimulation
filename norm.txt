Policy: Each fisher may take up to 15 kg per trip, with a community hard cap of 250 kg per day; any excess catch triggers a market‑priced fine, return of excess fish, and is enforced by a rotating tally‑keeper whose records are audited daily.

Operationalization: 1. At dawn, all fishers vote on the market price per kilogram for the day, using the latest market quotations or a simple average of the last three days, and record the agreed price in the shared ledger.
2. Before setting out, each fisher logs their intended catch weight on the ledger, marking it as "logged".
3. The tally‑keeper for the day is chosen by a rotating list of all eligible fishers; if a fisher is absent or barred, the next eligible fisher takes the role.
4. Throughout the day, fishers record their actual catch on the ledger and physically return any excess beyond the 15 kg individual quota to the lake.
5. At close of day, the tally‑keeper totals all logged catches. If the sum exceeds 250 kg, the tally‑keeper calculates the excess weight, instructs any fisher who exceeded their 15 kg limit to surrender the surplus portion of their catch, and returns the total excess to the lake.
6. The fine for each fisher who failed to log or exceeded limits is computed by multiplying the excess kilograms they must return by the agreed market price per kilogram for that day.
7. Fishers who are barred for exceeding limits must pay the fine before they may fish again. On the next day they present proof of payment to the tally‑keeper, who verifies it, lifts the ban, and records the clearance in the ledger.
8. All collected fines are held by the tally‑keeper in a community fund for communal purposes such as gear maintenance, lake upkeep, or shared projects, and spending is decided by simple majority vote at the weekly meeting.
9. After the tally‑keeper finalizes the ledger, a backup keeper—chosen at random or by next in line—is tasked with an immediate audit against the physical catch boxes. Any discrepancy must be corrected within 12 hours.
10. Failure to correct a discrepancy or refusal to act results in the tally‑keeper’s removal from the rotation for the next three days; repeated infractions trigger a full‑cycle ban and a review of the keeper’s fishing rights.
11. The tally‑keeper’s record and any penalties are recorded in the ledger, ensuring transparency and traceability for all fishers.

Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 12 kg per trip, depositing 5 % of the catch into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 25 kg.

Operationalization: On each trip, a fisher records the catch, deposits 5 % into the reserve ledger, and the reserve amount is tracked by a rotating community monitor who updates a public board. The lake’s remaining fish must be at least the reserve minimum before any catch is taken. If a fisher tries to take more than the available lake fish after reserve, their catch is automatically deducted to bring the lake back to reserve level and 50 % of the excess is transferred to the reserve. Failure to deposit on time results in a 1 kg forfeiture on the next trip.

Policy: Each fisher may take up to 12 kg per trip, and the lake must retain at least 20 kg of fish after each fishing round.

Operationalization: 1. Before the first trip of the round the fishery officer conducts a sweep of a 1 m² fixed sector, weighs the catch, and records the estimate of total lake stock in the public ledger. 2. Each fisher logs the weight of his haul in a notebook or phone app and hands the log to the officer at the end of his trip. 3. After all trips the officer tallies reported catches, subtracts them from the end‑sweep estimate, and checks that the remaining stock is within 20 kg ± 1 kg. 4. If compliant, the round passes. 5. If not compliant, the officer calls a meeting in the shed; a quorum of at least five present fishers votes to either conduct a second sweep (performed by the officer or an independent surveyor) or to impose a fine. 6. The fine scale is: 1–2 kg over → $10; 3–5 kg over → $25; 6 kg+ over → $50. 7. The treasurer, elected by the community at the start of the season, collects any fines and records the amount in the ledger. 8. All logs, sweep weights, calculations, votes, and fines are kept in a public ledger that every fisher can inspect. 9. The fishery officer is elected by majority vote each season; the treasurer is elected by majority vote each season; both roles rotate so no single fisher dominates. 10. The tolerance of ±1 kg is set by the officer but can be changed by community vote at the start of each season.

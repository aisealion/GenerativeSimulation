Policy: When the lake holds 12 kg or more, each fisher may take 1.0 kg per trip, keep it for sustenance, and deposit 0.5 kg into the communal reserve; if the lake holds 8–12 kg, the limit is 0.8 kg with a 0.3 kg deposit; if 6–8 kg, the limit is 0.5 kg with a 0.2 kg deposit; below 6 kg, fishing is suspended until the reserve reaches 12 kg.

Operationalization: Before each trip, the community will check the lake stock and announce the applicable limit and deposit. Each fisher will log their catch on a communal ledger and deposit the specified amount into the communal bin. A rotating keeper tallies the reserve daily. If a fisher exceeds the limit or fails to deposit, the community imposes a small fine or temporarily suspends that fisher’s rights until compliance is restored.

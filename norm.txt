Policy: Each fisher may keep up to 30 kg of fish per trip; any fish beyond that is automatically deposited into a communal maintenance reserve for lake upkeep and shared meals.

Operationalization: Each fisher may keep up to 30 kg of fish per trip. Any fish beyond 30 kg is automatically deposited into a communal maintenance reserve. The reserve is stored in a locked communal shed in the community yard. Every month an elected reserve keeper, chosen by secret ballot of all fishers who have logged at least one trip in the last 12 months, manages the shed and maintains a public ledger. Each fisher records his catch on a sheet, signs it, and deposits any excess with the reserve keeper before leaving. The reserve keeper logs each deposit in the ledger with date, fisher’s name, and amount. The ledger is displayed in the common area for all fishers to audit. Each fisher has 7 days after each deposit or withdrawal to review the ledger for accuracy; any discrepancy must be reported to the reserve keeper within those 7 days. The reserve keeper resolves discrepancies within 3 days; if unresolved, the community council adjudicates. When a fisher or the community needs fish for maintenance or meals, a written request including purpose and, if possible, a receipt is submitted to the reserve keeper. The reserve keeper logs the withdrawal with date, amount, requester, and purpose. If a fisher fails to return an overage, a 10% penalty of the unreturned excess weight (in kilograms) is added to the reserve ledger as additional fish, which is treated as part of the communal reserve and may be used for maintenance or meals. All entries, including penalty entries, are recorded in the ledger. The reserve keeper may only withdraw fish when a valid request is presented and the ledger is updated.

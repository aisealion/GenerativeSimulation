Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 70% of the lake’s current stock per trip. 1% of each catch is deposited into a communal reserve that must hold at least 5 kg when the lake’s stock exceeds 5 kg; if the reserve falls below 5 kg, all fishers contribute 0.5 kg per trip until it reaches 5 kg.

Operationalization: Before each trip, a fisher reports the intended catch and the 1% deposit. The deposit is added to the communal reserve. After the catch, the reserve is checked; if it is below 5 kg, every fisher adds 0.5 kg that trip until the reserve reaches 5 kg. If a fisher fails to deposit or contribute, the community imposes a one‑trip fishing ban until the reserve is restored.

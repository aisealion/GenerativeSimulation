Policy: Limit each fisher’s catch to 10 kg per trip, require a 2 kg (20%) deposit into the communal reserve, and enforce the deposit and cap through a rotating pair of volunteers who check the ledger and jar monthly, with fines for non‑compliance.

Operationalization: After each trip, I take the communal jar from Eli’s shed, drop 2 kg of my catch into it, and record the deposit in the shared ledger on the dock. The ledger is public, so anyone can verify the 2 kg deposit. On the first working day of each month at 10 a.m., Kai and a partner chosen by rotating the list of active fishers count the jar’s weight and cross‑check it against the ledger, then read the reserve balance aloud at the next meeting and post it on the notice board. If the reserve falls below 50 kg, the community meets to adjust the 10 kg cap. If a fisher’s ledger entry shows a missing 2 kg deposit or misreport, the fisher must make up the shortfall on the next trip or pay a fine equal to 5% of that trip’s total catch, capped at 2 kg; the fine is added to the communal reserve. The partner for the monthly check is selected by rotating through the list of active fishers, with the list posted on the board; if the chosen person cannot serve, the next on the list will be asked.

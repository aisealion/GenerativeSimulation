Policy: Each fisher may take up to 45% of the lake’s current weight per trip; must record the catch in the communal ledger within 3 hours; deposit 1% of catch into the maintenance fund; if combined community catch in a round exceeds 70% of the lake’s weight, the lake replenishes to its pre‑fishing weight before the next round.

Operationalization: Everyone must log catch within 3 hours of returning; each fisher contributes 1% of catch to the maintenance fund; failure to log forfeits entire catch to the communal pool; the community ledger is checked daily by a rotating clerk; if total community catch >70% of lake weight, the council must replenish the lake to its pre‑fishing weight before the next round; any excess catch is returned to the lake immediately.

Policy: Each fisher may harvest no more than 8% of the lake’s current biomass per trip, and the lake must retain at least 15% of its biomass as a communal reserve at all times.

Operationalization: Every trip a fisher records catch on a shared ledger; the community steward updates lake biomass and reserve balances. Before each trip, the steward announces the current biomass so fishers can compute 8%. If a fisher exceeds the limit, they must return excess to the reserve. Monthly meetings review reserves; if the reserve drops below 15% the limit is automatically lowered to 5% for all fishers until reserves recover.

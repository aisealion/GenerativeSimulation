Policy: Each fisher may take up to 2 kg per trip, must deposit 0.5 kg from the catch into a shared pool before the next trip, leave at least 30 % of the lake’s current stock untouched, and the community’s total daily catch may not exceed 60 % of the lake’s weight.

Operationalization: 1. I, Miro, serve as the appointed keeper: I verify each 0.5 kg deposit, maintain a daily ledger, and hold the communal bin at the dock.
2. Before any fishing trip, a fisher must deposit 0.5 kg from their catch; I will confirm the deposit and record it.
3. I monitor the lake’s current stock each morning with a spot count and compare it to the 30 % untouched requirement; a lake monitor from the council will double‑check my count.
4. I tally the community’s daily catch and ensure it does not exceed 60 % of the lake’s weight; any excess fish are seized, returned to the bin, and the fisher pays a fine of 0.02 kg per excess kilogram.
5. If a fisher fails to deposit or exceeds the 2 kg per trip limit, the surplus fish are seized and a fine equal to the surplus amount is levied.
6. All deposited fish remain in the bin until the community council meets every Friday to decide allocation or sale; I enforce that decision, distribute fish accordingly, and record all transactions.
7. Proceeds from any sales are deposited into the community fund.
8. Penalties for consistently failing to leave 30 % untouched include a temporary ban on fishing for one week or a required increased contribution to the communal pool.

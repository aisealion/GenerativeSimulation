Policy: Each fisher may harvest up to 1.0 kg per trip or no more than 7 % of the lake’s current biomass, whichever is smaller, and the lake must retain at least 75 % of its total biomass as a communal reserve after each catch.

Operationalization: At the start of each trip, a fisher looks at the current lake biomass and calculates the smaller of 1.0 kg or 7 % of that figure; that is the maximum they may take. After their catch, the community ledger records the new lake biomass and the remaining reserve. If a fisher’s catch would drop the reserve below 75 %, they must reduce their haul until the reserve threshold is met. All catches are logged by a designated keeper who meets monthly with the community to audit compliance. Anyone who exceeds the allowed amount must immediately return the excess to the lake in the next trip; repeated violations trigger a temporary fishing ban until the lake biomass recovers to the reserve level.

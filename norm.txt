Policy: No fisher may take more than 15 kg per trip; the total daily catch must stay under 20 % of the current lake stock, with excess returned to the lake if the stock falls below 30 % of the lake’s maximum capacity (60 kg) or if it exceeds 5 % over the 20 % cap, otherwise added to the community pot; violations are fined 5 kg from the pot or banned one trip, and all logs, fines, and pot balances are recorded by the rotating treasurer.

Operationalization: 1. After each trip the fisher logs the catch (kg) into the communal ledger before setting out.
2. At dusk the group meets; the current treasurer totals the daily catch.
3. The treasurer presents the total and the 20 % threshold (calculated from the lake’s current stock) to the council.
4. The council votes (majority) on excess allocation: if the excess is >5 % over the 20 % cap or the lake’s stock is below 30 % of maximum, the excess is returned to the lake; otherwise it goes to the community pot.
5. The treasurer records the decision, any transfers, and updates the pot balance in the ledger.
6. If any fisher exceeds the 15 kg cap, the treasurer deducts 5 kg from the pot (or applies a one‑trip ban) and notes the penalty in the ledger.
7. The pot balance is reviewed and confirmed by all fishers before the next day’s trips.
8. The treasurer is selected for the next day by rotating the role among fishers in a prearranged order, with the fisher who logged first that day becoming the next treasurer.

Policy: Each fisher may catch up to 1.5 kg per trip, must keep 1 kg for sustenance and deposit the remainder into a communal reserve capped at 120 kg; if the lake stock falls below 15 kg the catch limit drops to 1 kg.

Operationalization: All catch is weighed and logged by a rotating community steward each trip. The 1 kg kept is verified by a simple hand‑count. The remaining 0.5 kg is placed in the communal reserve pit, with a weekly audit to ensure the reserve never exceeds 120 kg. If the lake stock is measured below 15 kg by a community survey, the next round’s limit automatically changes to 1 kg. A fisher who fails to deposit its share will forfeit its catch in the following trip and may be fined a small amount of communal grain.

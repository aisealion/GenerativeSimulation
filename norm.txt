Policy: Each fisher may take at most 1.5 units of fish per day, and may not take more than 10% of the total catch that day.

Operationalization: On each trip, each fisher counts their catch by hand. At the daily gathering, the community tallies the total catch. If a fisher exceeds the 1.5‑unit or 10% limit, the excess fish are returned to the lake or placed in a communal reserve. All counts are verified by verbal confirmation and simple tally sheets—no special equipment is needed.

Policy: No fisher may bring back more than 7 kg per trip; any excess must be left on shore or deposited into the community pool managed by the community council. A violation results in a one‑week ban marked by a red flag on the offending boat, recorded in a public log.

Operationalization: 1. Before setting out, each fisher weighs his catch on the shared lake‑scale and records the weight on the public log. 2. If the weight exceeds 7 kg, the fisher must leave the excess on the dock or drop it into the community pool. 3. The volunteer for the week—selected by monthly rotation among fishers or appointed by the council—records the weight, checks the boat daily for a red flag, and logs any placement date. 4. If the catch exceeds 7 kg, the volunteer places a red flag on the boat, writes the placement date in the log, and marks the boat as banned. 5. The ban lasts exactly seven consecutive days; the volunteer checks the flag each day. 6. On the seventh day, the volunteer removes the flag, records the clearance date in the log, and marks the boat as cleared. 7. If the volunteer fails to check the flag on a given day, the council chair steps in to enforce removal. 8. The community council, elected annually by secret ballot from fishers with at least two years on the lake and boat ownership, appoints the volunteer and maintains the roster of banned boats.

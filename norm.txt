Policy: Each fisher may keep up to 4 kg per trip; excess catch goes to a communal pool, and fishers whose reserves fall below 12 kg must contribute an extra 1 kg to that pool (overriding the 4 kg keep limit). At month‑end, the pool is redistributed to fishers whose reserves are below 18 kg, each receiving an equal share capped at 2 kg, with any remainder shared equally among all fishers.

Operationalization: Before each trip, a fisher records total catch in the communal ledger. If the fisher’s reserve is ≥12 kg, they keep up to 4 kg and deposit the rest into the pool. If reserve <12 kg, they must contribute an extra 1 kg: if total catch ≥5 kg, they keep 4 kg, deposit (catch‑4)+1 kg; if <5 kg, they deposit the entire catch and keep nothing. A missed 1 kg contribution triggers a 1 kg penalty deducted from the next trip’s catch (zeroed if catch <1 kg). On the first fishing day of each month, a secret ballot elects a steward; a majority is required, with a runoff if tied, and if no majority the current steward continues until the next election. The steward tallies reserves, calculates the pool, distributes to fishers below 18 kg (capped 2 kg each), then splits any remaining pool equally among all fishers. All ledger entries, pool totals, steward votes and distributions are posted publicly on the community board.

# Round 6 Norm Specification

**Policy:** No fisher may take more than 5 kg per trip; 30% of each fisher’s catch must be deposited into a communal reserve that must always hold at least 30% of the lake’s current biomass; if the reserve falls below 35% of lake biomass a temporary fishing ban applies until the reserve is restored to 35% or has increased by 5 kg.

**Operationalization:** Each trip, a fisher records the total catch in a public ledger and deposits 30% of that catch into the communal reserve. At the end of every round the lake monitor measures the lake’s biomass, calculates 30% of that biomass as the required reserve level and 35% as the ban trigger level, and reports both figures to the fishery council. The fishery council reviews the deposit logs; fishers who fail to deposit the required 30% lose fishing rights for that round and a 1‑kg penalty is added to the reserve. If the reserve falls below 35% of lake biomass, the lake monitor publicly announces a temporary fishing ban, and the ban remains in effect until the reserve reaches 35% of the measured lake biomass or has increased by at least 5 kg of deposits since the ban began. All records are kept in a community ledger accessible to all fishers, ensuring transparency and accountability.

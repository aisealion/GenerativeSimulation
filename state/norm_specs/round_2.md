1: Policy: Each fisher may take up to 10% of the lake’s current stock per trip, with a minimum of 1 kg for sustenance and a hard cap of 20 kg per trip; excess must be returned to the lake or deposited in the community reserve.

3: Operationalization: 1. At the start of each round, the community elects a ledger keeper by simple majority; a tie is broken by longest tenure or largest recent catch. 2. Each fisher calculates 10% of the lake’s current stock before setting out; if that exceeds 20 kg, the fisher is limited to 20 kg. 3. Upon return, the fisher records the exact weight caught and submits the record to the ledger keeper. 4. If the catch exceeds 20 kg, the excess is immediately returned to the lake or deposited into the community reserve; the ledger keeper notes the action. 5. The ledger keeper compiles the total lake stock after each round, including returned fish, and reports to the community. 6. If a fisher fails to submit a record or attempts to keep more than 20 kg, the ledger keeper reports an infraction; the fisher loses fishing rights for one round and must perform community service (dock cleaning, net repair, or assisting in the next community harvest). 7. After every round, the community reviews the lake’s stock; if it falls below 200 kg, all fishermen’s 10% quotas are reduced by 5 % for the next round. 8. The community reserve is maintained as a separate pool and may be used for feeding, emergencies, or replenishing the lake; its use is governed by the ledger keeper and community guidelines.

## Round 2 Norm Specification

**Policy**: No individual fisher may catch more than **8 %** of the lake’s current biomass in any single trip.

**Operationalization**
1. Each week the community council votes a fisher to be the *biomass surveyor*.
2. The surveyor sets a standard 2‑m gill net, fishes for 30 minutes, weighs the haul and multiplies by three to estimate the lake’s total biomass.
3. The estimated biomass is recorded in the communal ledger with the date and surveyor’s name; this value becomes the basis for all quotas for the following week.
4. Each fisher’s quota for the week is **8 %** of the recorded biomass. After each trip the fisher logs the haul weight in the ledger.
5. If a fisher’s logged haul exceeds their quota, the excess is deducted from their quota for the next trip and a reminder is issued by the next scheduled surveyor.
6. A *persistent violation* is defined as either:
   - Exceeding the quota on two or more consecutive trips, **or**
   - Accumulating excess weight over a four‑week period that totals more than **4 %** of the lake’s current biomass.
7. Upon detection of a persistent violation, any fisher who observes it (or any fisher who reports it) calls a community meeting; the council convenes, reviews the ledger, and decides corrective actions such as temporary quota reductions or education sessions.
8. All procedures, ledger entries, and meeting minutes are publicly posted for transparency and accountability.

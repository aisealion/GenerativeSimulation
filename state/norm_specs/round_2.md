Policy: Each fisher may harvest no more than 4 % of the lake’s current biomass per trip and must return 20 % of their catch to the lake; permission is granted each morning by the Community Council upon signing a pledge and recording the quota in the shared daily logbook.

Operationalization: 1.  Each morning the Community Council announces the lake’s current biomass and calculates 4 % of that figure for each fisher.  2.  Every fisher signs the daily logbook, recording their name, the calculated quota, and a pledge to return 20 % of the catch.  3.  After the trip the fisher records the actual catch and the returned portion in the logbook.  4.  A small group of watchers—one or two fishermen—performs weekly audits of the logbook and conducts random checks of the lake water for fish remnants to verify that the returned portion has been placed back in the lake.  5.  If a watcher finds a breach (catch exceeds the 4 % quota or the returned 20 % is missing), the watcher reports it to the Council.  6.  The Council formally revokes the fisher’s permission for one week, schedules a mandatory re‑education meeting, and only after the fisher attends, recites the sustainability principles, signs a new pledge, and is found compliant will the Council issue a fresh permission for the following morning.

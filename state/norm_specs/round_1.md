Policy: No fisher may pull more than 6 kg per trip; the lake’s stock must never fall below 240 kg, and all fishing stops if it does until the stock recovers; excess catch above 6 kg is returned to the communal pool or given to a fisher in need; fishers who exceed the 6 kg quota more than twice are suspended for one week; daily lake stock is measured by a rotating steward and recorded in the communal ledger.

Operationalization: 1. Every trip a fisher logs: name, date, total catch weight, whether the catch exceeds 6 kg, and the excess amount. 2. If the catch exceeds 6 kg the fisher must immediately deposit the excess into the communal pool or hand it to another fisher whose reserve is below 1 kg. 3. The ledger records each fisher’s reserve balance after the trip, ensuring each fisher keeps at least 1 kg for survival. 4. A rotating steward measures the lake’s total stock each day using a standard net, records the weight in the ledger, and announces it to the community. 5. If the recorded lake stock falls below 240 kg all fishing is suspended; the steward logs the halt and a community meeting is scheduled to review and adjust quotas. 6. The senior fisher council monitors the ledger for fishers who have exceeded the 6 kg quota more than twice; such fishers are suspended for one week, a suspension record is entered in the ledger, and the fisher is notified by the council. 7. The community reviews the lake stock and quotas at each meeting, updating the policy in the ledger if necessary. 8. All fishers are responsible for adhering to these rules, and the ledger provides transparency and accountability for reserves, excess returns, and suspensions.

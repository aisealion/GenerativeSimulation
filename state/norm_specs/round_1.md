# Round 1 Institutional Design Specification

**Policy**
Each fisher may keep up to **15 kg** per trip. Any surplus above 15 kg must be **shared equally** among all other fishers. Failure to share triggers a **2 kg forfeiture**, a **second 2 kg forfeiture**, and a **one‑week ban** on repeated non‑compliance.

**Communal Pot**
Forfeited fish are placed into a communal pot. When the pot reaches **10 kg**, the council‑led vote decides its use or redistribution.

**Operationalization**
1. **Weigh‑in** – After each trip the fisher brings the net to the shore weigh‑station. The appointed **ledger keeper** records the total catch.
2. **Surplus Allocation** – If the catch exceeds 15 kg the keeper:
   - Subtracts the surplus from the fisher’s personal account.
   - Records the amount.
   - Distributes the surplus **equally** among all other fishers, noting each share in the communal ledger.
3. **Verification** – A rotating **guard** checks the weight and confirms ledger entries after every trip. A full audit occurs **every third trip**.
4. **Non‑compliance Penalties** – If the fisher does not share surplus:
   - On the next trip the fisher must surrender **2 kg** (or the entire catch if < 2 kg) to the communal pot. The ledger records a debit to the fisher and a credit to the pot.
   - A second omission triggers a **4 kg forfeiture** (or the entire catch if < 4 kg).
   - If non‑compliance persists, a **one‑week fishing ban** is enforced, preventing gear access until the ban expires and community approval is obtained.
5. **Pot Management** – The communal pot is held in locked storage. When it reaches **10 kg** it is reviewed at the **monthly meeting**. The council of long‑time fishers leads a vote to decide its use or redistribution. The outcome is recorded in the ledger and communicated in writing to all fishers.

**Enforcement & Auditing**
- Ledger entries are signed by the keeper and verified by the guard.
- Audits every third trip ensure consistency.
- Bans are recorded in the communal ledger and lifted only after the stipulated week has passed and a council vote approves reinstatement.

# Round 1 Norm Specification

**Policy**

Each fisher may take up to **15 kg** per trip; any excess must be returned to the lake. A shared ledger records intended and actual catch, excess fish, and reserve allocation: **70 %** for maintenance and **30 %** for fishers whose daily food reserve is below **1 kg**, split equally if multiple.

The council audits monthly, enforces forfeiture, and maintains the reserve pit.

**Operationalization**

1. **Trip procedure** – before departure record intended catch. After return, weigh actual catch.
2. **Excess handling** – if actual > 15 kg, compute excess = actual − 15 kg; return excess immediately to the marked reserve pit and record it in the ledger.
3. **Ledger entry** – record date, intended catch, actual catch, excess returned, and net catch kept. Submit entry within two hours.
4. **Reserve pool** – each month the community pool is divided **70 %** for maintenance and **30 %** for food reserve.
5. **Maintenance** – expenses for nets, boats, docks, cleaning are logged under a *Maintenance* section. The total monthly maintenance cost is subtracted from the 70 % pool; any shortfall is taken from the 30 % pool.
6. **Food reserve** – for each fisher, sum all net catches on a calendar day. If total < 1 kg, the fisher is flagged. At month end, all flagged fishers receive an equal share of the remaining 30 % pool. If no one is flagged, the 30 % pool stays in reserve.
7. **Council** – composed of seven fisher‑representatives plus the fishery officer. Meets monthly to audit the ledger, verify compliance, and issue forfeiture notices.
8. **Forfeiture enforcement** – a fisher who fails to comply (catch > 15 kg, not returning excess, or failing to record timely) loses fishing rights for the next trip; other fishers and the council chair deny access to boats or spots.
9. **Pit maintenance** – council assigns a keeper each month; fishers must keep the pit tidy.
10. **Reporting** – the council publishes a monthly summary to all fishers showing maintenance costs, reserve allocations, and any forfeitures.

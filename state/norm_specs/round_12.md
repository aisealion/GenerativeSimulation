# Norm Specification – Round 12

**Policy**
Each fisher may take up to **3 kg** per trip, must keep at least **1 kg** for personal use, and the community’s monthly catch may not exceed **25 %** of the lake’s stock at the start of the month, capped at **6 kg**.

**Operationalization**
1. After each trip the fisher records the total catch in the communal ledger. The system automatically deducts **1 kg** for personal keep; the remainder is considered surplus.
2. At the end of each month the ledger aggregates total surplus across all fishers.
3. If total surplus exceeds the monthly quota (the lower of 25 % of the month‑start stock and 6 kg), each fisher must return a proportional share of the excess.
4. Failure to return the assigned surplus within the same month creates a debt entry. Fishers with outstanding debt are banned for the following month.
5. Any debt that remains unresolved by month‑end triggers a reduction of the next month’s community quota by **5 %**.
6. All ledger entries, sanctions, and quota adjustments are publicly visible to ensure transparency.

Policy: Each fisher may take no more than 8 kg per trip, and the lake must retain at least 30 % of its pre‑trip stock after every round. Violations flag the fisher and bar them for one full round before they may re‑join.

Operationalization:
1. Ledger keeper rotates among non‑flagged fishers; if all flagged, selects from flagged.
2. Ledger keeper records pre‑trip stock at round start.
3. After fishing, records each fisher’s catch and updates ledger.
4. Community verifies remaining stock ≥30 % of pre‑trip stock.
5. If any fisher exceeds 8 kg or remaining stock <30 %, ledger keeper flags that fisher and posts ban.
6. Flagged fishers are barred for the next round; after one full round they may re‑join.

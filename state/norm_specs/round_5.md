# Norm Specification – Round 5

**Policy**
Each fisher may take up to **4 kg** per trip: **1 kg** is kept for personal sustenance and the remaining **3 kg** is deposited into a communal reserve. The reserve may be drawn only when lake biomass falls below **70 kg** **and** at least **six months** have elapsed since the last reserve release. No fisher’s **monthly total catch** may exceed **8 %** of the lake’s current biomass.

**Operationalization**
1. At the start of each calendar year a **communal steward** is elected by simple majority. The steward is responsible for maintaining the shared ledger, overseeing the reserve, and enforcing the policy.
2. After each trip the fisher records the **total catch** in the ledger. The steward automatically deducts **1 kg** for personal use and logs the **3 kg** deposit to the communal reserve.
3. The ledger tracks each fisher’s **cumulative catch for the month**. If a fisher’s monthly total exceeds **8 %** of the lake’s current biomass, the excess is **returned to the lake** and the fisher receives a **two‑month fishing suspension**; their gear is temporarily confiscated. The violation is recorded in the ledger along with the sanction.
4. Lake biomass is measured monthly by a rotating monitoring group using calibrated float methods; the current biomass value is stored as `context.stock_before` for the round.
5. When biomass drops below **70 kg** **and** at least **six months** have passed since the previous reserve release, the steward may **open the reserve**. While the reserve is open, no single trip may draw more than **3 kg** from the reserve until biomass rises above **70 kg** again.
6. All actions – election results, deposit records, quota calculations, forfeiture notices, and reserve releases – are entered into the shared ledger and displayed on the community warning board for full transparency.

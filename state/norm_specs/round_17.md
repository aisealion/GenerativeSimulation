# Norm Specification – Round 17

**Policy**
Each fisher may take up to 2 kg per trip, and the total weekly harvest must not exceed 40 % of the lake’s current stock, with a hard minimum of 4 kg remaining in the lake.

**Operationalization**
1. Before each trip the fisher presents a valid permit issued by the community council.
2. The fisher records the exact weight of fish taken and signs the trip log.
3. At the start of the week the council determines the lake’s current stock; 40 % of that amount (but never allowing the lake to fall below 4 kg) is the cap for the entire week.
4. At the end of the week the council sums all catches. If the total exceeds the cap, the fisher whose last trip produced the excess keeps the excess in a marked, sealed bag until the end of that trip, then deposits it immediately at the agreed return point.
5. The council revokes that fisher’s permit for the following week, bars him from fishing for that week, and records the ban in the weekly ledger.
6. All data (trip weights, weekly total, excess returned, banned fisher) is posted publicly on the dock board for verification.

Policy: Each fisher may take up to 15% of the lake’s current stock per trip, with a minimum of 1 kg for sustenance and a hard cap of 20 kg per trip.

Operationalization: 1. At the start of each trip the fisher logs their name, date, and the lake’s current stock as recorded in the communal ledger. 2. After fishing the fisher records the exact catch weight and submits the record to the moderator/ledger keeper. 3. The moderator computes the allowed limit as the lesser of 15% of the logged starting stock and 20 kg. 4. If the catch is within the limit it is accepted and the lake stock is reduced accordingly. 5. If the catch exceeds the limit, the excess is calculated; if the lake’s current stock is below 50 kg the excess is returned to the lake, otherwise it is transferred to the community reserve. 6. An infraction is recorded and the fisher’s fee_balance is incremented by 0.5 kg. 7. For subsequent trips the fisher must deduct 0.5 kg from the catch (or pay an equivalent cash fee) until they achieve two consecutive trips within the limit, at which point the fee_balance is cleared. 8. The community reviews the lake stock weekly; if it falls below 50 kg the percentage threshold for the next trips is reduced to 10% until the stock rises above the threshold.

# Norm Specification – Round 13

**Policy**
Each fisher may keep up to **4 kg** per trip; any excess catch is deposited into a communal pool. Fishers whose reserves are **below 12 kg** must contribute an **extra 1 kg** to that pool (overriding the 4 kg keep limit).

At month‑end, the pool is redistributed to fishers whose reserves are **below 18 kg**. Each receives an **equal share capped at 2 kg**; any remaining pool is then shared **equally among all fishers**.

**Operationalization**
1. Before each trip the fisher records the total catch in the communal ledger.
2. If the fisher’s reserve is **≥ 12 kg**, they keep up to **4 kg** and deposit the remainder into the pool.
3. If the reserve is **< 12 kg**, they must contribute an extra **1 kg**:
   - If total catch ≥ 5 kg, they keep 4 kg, deposit *(catch − 4 kg) + 1 kg*.
   - If total catch < 5 kg, they deposit the entire catch and keep nothing.
4. Missing the extra 1 kg contribution creates a **1 kg penalty** deducted from the next trip’s catch (zeroed if the next catch is < 1 kg).
5. On the first fishing day of each month a **secret ballot elects a steward**; a majority is required, with a runoff if tied. If no majority is reached, the current steward continues until the next election.
6. The steward tallies reserves, calculates the pool, and distributes:
   - Fishers with reserves **< 18 kg** receive an equal share, each capped at **2 kg**.
   - Any leftover pool is split **equally among all fishers**.
7. All ledger entries, pool totals, steward votes, and distributions are posted publicly on the community board.

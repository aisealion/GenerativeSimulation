# Norm Specification – Round 4

**Policy**
Each fisher may harvest up to **4 kg** per trip: **1 kg** for personal sustenance and **3 kg** deposited into a communal reserve. Monthly catch may not exceed **10 %** of the lake’s current biomass; any excess must be returned to the lake immediately and double‑deposited as a penalty. The reserve is released only when lake biomass falls below **80 kg** and after a **4‑month** refill period. Allocation of released reserves is decided by a rotating committee of three monitors who first aid fishers with the lowest balances, then proportionally share remaining reserves.

**Operationalization**
1. After each trip, the fisher records total catch in the communal ledger; **1 kg** is deducted for sustenance, the remainder (up to **3 kg**) is deposited into the communal reserve bin and logged.
2. The system tracks each fisher’s cumulative catch for the round (treated as a month). If a fisher’s total exceeds **10 %** of the lake’s current biomass, the excess weight is immediately returned to the lake and the fisher deposits double that amount into the reserve bin; this penalty is recorded and verified by two monitors.
3. Lake biomass is measured at the start of each round and logged.
4. When biomass drops below **80 kg** and a **4‑month** refill period (counted from the last reserve release date) has elapsed, a rotating committee of three impartial community monitors convenes.
5. The committee reviews the ledger for the period, calculates each fisher’s net balance (catch minus sustenance minus reserve deposits), identifies fishers with the lowest balances, and allocates reserve funds first to bring those balances to at least zero or a predefined sustainability threshold.
6. Any remaining reserve is divided proportionally among all fishers based on the amount each contributed to the reserve during the refill period.
7. All allocations, dates, and rationales are entered into the ledger; a summary is published to the community.
8. Fishers may submit a written request to the committee to review any allocation; the committee will re‑examine the ledger and make a final decision.

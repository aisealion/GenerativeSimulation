A rotating pot keeper among fishers who records contributions.

You are responsible for recording contributions to the communal pot. This is a rotating responsibility among fishers.
During your time as pot keeper:
- You must accurately record each fisher's catch in the communal pot
- Contributions are calculated as 5% of total haul weight
- You must keep track of who has contributed and who has not
- Your duties last until your replacement takes over the rotation

This role is essential to maintaining transparency and fair sharing in our community.

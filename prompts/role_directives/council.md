# Council role - community decision making body

The Council is a community decision making body responsible for coordinating fishery management and community-wide decisions. Council members are drawn from the broader community of fishers and have the authority to make key decisions about resource allocation, policy setting, and community governance.

The Council works alongside the Elder and Scribe to ensure that fishery operations are conducted sustainably and that community decisions are made transparently. Council members participate in discussions, propose solutions, and vote on important matters affecting the fishery's long-term health and community welfare.

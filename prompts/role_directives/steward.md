You are the steward for this fishing cycle. Your duties include:

- Monitoring fish deposits and applying penalties when necessary 
- Sealing the iron barrel and secondary container (and temporary bin if used)
- Authorizing withdrawals from the communal reserve
- Handling non-fisher withdrawals
- Managing confiscation of missing fish and imposing penalties
- Tracking violations and suspensions for fishing rights

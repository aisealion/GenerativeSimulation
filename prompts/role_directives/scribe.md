# Scribe role - chosen by simple majority vote of active fishers each season.

The Scribe is elected by simple majority vote of active fishers each season. The Scribe is responsible for documenting all important community decisions, maintaining records of fishery operations, and ensuring that the community's knowledge and history are preserved.

The Scribe works closely with the Elder to ensure that all community actions are properly recorded and that important decisions are transparent to all fishers.
